# Knowledge Rambling in the North (知北遊)
Source: Zhuangzi _ Outer Chapters _ Knowledge Rambling in the North - Chinese Text Project.html
Status: complete (26 sections)

## Section 1

**ZH:** 知北遊於玄水之上，登隱弅之丘，而適遭無為謂焉。知謂無為謂曰：「予欲有問乎若：何思何慮則知道？何處何服則安道？何從何道則得道？」三問而無為謂不答也，非不答，不知答也。知不得問，反於白水之南，登狐闋之丘，而睹狂屈焉。知以之言也問乎狂屈。狂屈曰：「唉！予知之，將語若，中欲言而忘其所欲言。」知不得問，反於帝宮，見黃帝而問焉。黃帝曰：「無思無慮始知道，無處無服始安道，無從無道始得道。」
**PY:** zhī běi yóu yú xuán shuǐ zhī shàng， dēng yǐn fèn zhī qiū， ér shì zāo wú wèi wèi yān。 zhī wèi wú wèi wèi yuē：「 yǔ yù yǒu wèn hū ruò： hé sī hé lǜ zé zhī dào？ hé chù hé fú zé ān dào？ hé cóng hé dào zé dé dào？」 sān wèn ér wú wèi wèi bù dá yě， fēi bù dá， bù zhī dá yě。 zhī bù dé wèn， fǎn yú bái shuǐ zhī nán， dēng hú què zhī qiū， ér dǔ kuáng qū yān。 zhī yǐ zhī yán yě wèn hū kuáng qū。 kuáng qū yuē：「 āi！ yǔ zhī zhī， jiāng yǔ ruò， zhōng yù yán ér wàng qí suǒ yù yán。」 zhī bù dé wèn， fǎn yú dì gōng， jiàn huáng dì ér wèn yān。 huáng dì yuē：「 wú sī wú lǜ shǐ zhī dào， wú chù wú fú shǐ ān dào， wú cóng wú dào shǐ dé dào。」

**EN:** Knowledge (知), roaming north upon the Dark Waters, climbed the hill of Hidden Rise, where he happened upon Non-Action's-Saying (無為謂). Knowledge said to Non-Action's-Saying: "I have something to ask you: What thoughts, what considerations — then one knows the Way? What dwelling, what practice — then one rests in the Way? What path, what way — then one gets the Way?" Three questions, and Non-Action's-Saying did not answer. It was not that he would not answer — he did not know how to answer. Knowledge got no answer, returned south of the White Waters, climbed the hill of Fox Watch, and there caught sight of Wild-and-Witless (狂屈). Knowledge put the same words in question to Wild-and-Witless. Wild-and-Witless said: "Ah! I know it, and was about to tell you — but just as I wanted to speak, I forgot what I wanted to say." Knowledge got no answer, returned to the Emperor's palace, saw the Yellow Emperor, and asked him. The Yellow Emperor said: "No thoughts, no considerations — then you begin to know the Way. No dwelling, no practice — then you begin to rest in the Way. No path, no way — then you begin to get the Way."

## Section 2

**ZH:** 知問黃帝曰：「我與若知之，彼與彼不知也，其孰是邪？」黃帝曰：「彼無為謂真是也，狂屈似之，我與汝終不近也。夫知者不言，言者不知，故聖人行不言之教。道不可致，德不可至。仁可為也，義可虧也，禮相偽也。故曰：『失道而後德，失德而後仁，失仁而後義，失義而後禮。禮者，道之華而亂之首也。』故曰：『為道者日損，損之又損之，以至於無為，無為而無不為也。』今已為物也，欲復歸根，不亦難乎！其易也，其唯大人乎！生也死之徒，死也生之始，孰知其紀！人之生，氣之聚也，聚則為生，散則為死。若死生為徒，吾又何患！故萬物一也，是其所美者為神奇，其所惡者為臭腐；臭腐復化為神奇，神奇復化為臭腐。故曰：『通天下一氣耳。』聖人故貴一。」
**PY:** zhī wèn huáng dì yuē：「 wǒ yǔ ruò zhī zhī， bǐ yǔ bǐ bù zhī yě， qí shú shì xié？」 huáng dì yuē：「 bǐ wú wèi wèi zhēn shì yě， kuáng qū shì zhī， wǒ yǔ rǔ zhōng bù jìn yě。 fū zhī zhě bù yán， yán zhě bù zhī， gù shèng rén xíng bù yán zhī jiào。 dào bù kě zhì， dé bù kě zhì。 rén kě wèi yě， yì kě kuī yě， lǐ xiāng wěi yě。 gù yuē：『 shī dào ér hòu dé， shī dé ér hòu rén， shī rén ér hòu yì， shī yì ér hòu lǐ。 lǐ zhě， dào zhī huá ér luàn zhī shǒu yě。』 gù yuē：『 wèi dào zhě rì sǔn， sǔn zhī yòu sǔn zhī， yǐ zhì yú wú wèi， wú wèi ér wú bù wèi yě。』 jīn yǐ wèi wù yě， yù fù guī gēn， bù yì nán hū！ qí yì yě， qí wéi dà rén hū！ shēng yě sǐ zhī tú， sǐ yě shēng zhī shǐ， shú zhī qí jì！ rén zhī shēng， qì zhī jù yě， jù zé wèi shēng， sàn zé wèi sǐ。 ruò sǐ shēng wèi tú， wú yòu hé huàn！ gù wàn wù yī yě， shì qí suǒ měi zhě wèi shén qí， qí suǒ è zhě wèi chòu fǔ； chòu fǔ fù huà wèi shén qí， shén qí fù huà wèi chòu fǔ。 gù yuē：『 tōng tiān xià yī qì ěr。』 shèng rén gù guì yī。」

**EN:** Knowledge asked the Yellow Emperor: "You and I know it; those two did not know it. Who is right?" The Yellow Emperor said: "That Non-Action's-Saying is truly right; Wild-and-Witless is near it; you and I will never come close. Those who know do not speak; those who speak do not know. Therefore the sage practices the teaching without words. The Way cannot be brought to you; virtue cannot be arrived at. Humaneness can be practiced; rightness can be impaired; ritual is mutual falsity. Hence it is said: 'When the Way is lost, there is virtue; when virtue is lost, there is humaneness; when humaneness is lost, there is rightness; when rightness is lost, there is ritual. Ritual is the flower of the Way and the beginning of disorder.' Hence it is said: 'Working at the Way, one diminishes daily; diminishes and again diminishes, until one arrives at non-action — non-action, and nothing is not done.' Now, having already become a thing, to wish to return to the root — is that not hard! For whom would it be easy? Only for the great man! Life is death's companion; death is life's beginning — who knows their thread! Man's life is a gathering of breath: gathered, there is life; scattered, there is death. If death and life are companions, what have I to fear! So the ten thousand things are one. What one finds beautiful is the spirit-like and strange; what one finds ugly is the stinking and rotten. The stinking and rotten transforms again into the spirit-like and strange; the spirit-like and strange transforms again into the stinking and rotten. Hence it is said: 'All under heaven is one breath.' Therefore the sage prizes oneness."

## Section 3

**ZH:** 知謂黃帝曰：「吾問無為謂，無為謂不應我，非不我應，不知應我也。吾問狂屈，狂屈中欲告我而不我告，非不我告，中欲告而忘之也。今予問乎若，若知之，奚故不近？」黃帝曰：「彼其真是也，以其不知也；此其似之也，以其忘之也；予與若終不近也，以其知之也。」
**PY:** zhī wèi huáng dì yuē：「 wú wèn wú wèi wèi， wú wèi wèi bù yīng wǒ， fēi bù wǒ yīng， bù zhī yīng wǒ yě。 wú wèn kuáng qū， kuáng qū zhōng yù gào wǒ ér bù wǒ gào， fēi bù wǒ gào， zhōng yù gào ér wàng zhī yě。 jīn yǔ wèn hū ruò， ruò zhī zhī， xī gù bù jìn？」 huáng dì yuē：「 bǐ qí zhēn shì yě， yǐ qí bù zhī yě； cǐ qí shì zhī yě， yǐ qí wàng zhī yě； yǔ yǔ ruò zhōng bù jìn yě， yǐ qí zhī zhī yě。」

**EN:** Knowledge said to the Yellow Emperor: "I asked Non-Action's-Saying, and Non-Action's-Saying did not answer me. It was not that he would not answer me — he did not know how to answer me. I asked Wild-and-Witless, and Wild-and-Witless, midway to telling me, did not tell me. It was not that he would not tell me — midway to telling me, he forgot. Now I asked you, and you know it. Why are you not close?" The Yellow Emperor said: "He is truly right, because he did not know it. The other is near it, because he forgot it. You and I will never come close, because we know it."

## Section 4

**ZH:** 狂屈聞之，以黃帝為知言。
**PY:** kuáng qū wén zhī， yǐ huáng dì wèi zhī yán。

**EN:** Wild-and-Witless heard of it, and considered that the Yellow Emperor knew how to speak.

## Section 5

**ZH:** 天地有大美而不言，四時有明法而不議，萬物有成理而不說。聖人者，原天地之美而達萬物之理。是故至人無為，大聖不作，觀於天地之謂也。
**PY:** tiān dì yǒu dà měi ér bù yán， sì shí yǒu míng fǎ ér bù yì， wàn wù yǒu chéng lǐ ér bù shuō。 shèng rén zhě， yuán tiān dì zhī měi ér dá wàn wù zhī lǐ。 shì gù zhì rén wú wèi， dà shèng bù zuò， guān yú tiān dì zhī wèi yě。

**EN:** Heaven and earth have a great beauty and do not speak; the four seasons have a bright law and do not discuss; the ten thousand things have a completed pattern and do not tell. The sage traces the beauty of heaven and earth and penetrates the pattern of the ten thousand things. Therefore the utmost man does nothing; the great sage does not create — this is called contemplating heaven and earth.

## Section 6

**ZH:** 今彼神明至精，與彼百化，物已死生方圓，莫知其根也，扁然而萬物自古以固存。六合為巨，未離其內；秋豪為小，待之成體。天下莫不沈浮，終身不故；陰陽四時運行，各得其序。惛然若亡而存，油然不形而神，萬物畜而不知。此之謂本根，可以觀於天矣。
**PY:** jīn bǐ shén míng zhì jīng， yǔ bǐ bǎi huà， wù yǐ sǐ shēng fāng yuán， mò zhī qí gēn yě， biǎn rán ér wàn wù zì gǔ yǐ gù cún。 liù hé wèi jù， wèi lí qí nèi； qiū háo wèi xiǎo， dài zhī chéng tǐ。 tiān xià mò bù shěn fú， zhōng shēn bù gù； yīn yáng sì shí yùn xíng， gè dé qí xù。 hūn rán ruò wáng ér cún， yóu rán bù xíng ér shén， wàn wù chù ér bù zhī。 cǐ zhī wèi běn gēn， kě yǐ guān yú tiān yǐ。

**EN:** Now that spirit-bright utmost essence joins with things in their hundred transformations: things have died, been born, squared, and rounded, and none knows their root; sweeping and turning, the ten thousand things have been secure from of old. The six coordinates are huge, yet never leave its inside; the autumn hair is small, yet waits on it to form its body. Nothing in the world does not sink and float; to the end of life nothing stays as it was. Yin and yang and the four seasons run on, each getting its own sequence. Dim as though lost, yet present; welling forth, formless, yet spirit-like; the ten thousand things are nurtured by it and do not know it. This is called the root — from it one may behold Heaven.

## Section 7

**ZH:** 齧缺問道乎被衣，被衣曰：「若正汝形，一汝視，天和將至；攝汝知，一汝度，神將來舍。德將為汝美，道將為汝居，汝瞳焉如新出之犢而無求其故！」言未卒，齧缺睡寐。被衣大說，行歌而去之，曰：「形若槁骸，心若死灰，真其實知，不以故自持。媒媒晦晦，無心而不可與謀。彼何人哉！」
**PY:** niè quē wèn dào hū bèi yī， bèi yī yuē：「 ruò zhèng rǔ xíng， yī rǔ shì， tiān hé jiāng zhì； shè rǔ zhī， yī rǔ dù， shén jiāng lái shě。 dé jiāng wèi rǔ měi， dào jiāng wèi rǔ jū， rǔ tóng yān rú xīn chū zhī dú ér wú qiú qí gù！」 yán wèi zú， niè quē shuì mèi。 bèi yī dà shuō， xíng gē ér qù zhī， yuē：「 xíng ruò gǎo hái， xīn ruò sǐ huī， zhēn qí shí zhī， bù yǐ gù zì chí。 méi méi huì huì， wú xīn ér bù kě yǔ móu。 bǐ hé rén zāi！」

**EN:** Nie Que asked Piyi about the Way. Piyi said: "Set your form right, make your gaze one — Heaven's harmony will arrive. Gather in your knowledge, make your measure one — the spirit will come to lodge. Virtue will be your beauty; the Way will be your dwelling. And you — wide-eyed as a newborn calf, not seeking out the why!" Before he had finished speaking, Nie Que fell fast asleep. Piyi was greatly delighted; singing as he walked, he went away: "Form like a withered skeleton, heart like dead ashes; true and solid his knowing, not holding to the why; dim and dark, heartless, with whom nothing can be plotted. What manner of man is he!"

## Section 8

**ZH:** 舜問乎丞曰：「道可得而有乎？」曰：「汝身非汝有也，汝何得有夫道？」舜曰：「吾身非吾有也，孰有之哉？」曰：「是天地之委形也；生非汝有，是天地之委和也；性命非汝有，是天地之委順也；孫子非汝有，是天地之委蛻也。故行不知所往，處不知所持，食不知所味。天地之強陽氣也，又胡可得而有邪？」
**PY:** shùn wèn hū chéng yuē：「 dào kě dé ér yǒu hū？」 yuē：「 rǔ shēn fēi rǔ yǒu yě， rǔ hé dé yǒu fū dào？」 shùn yuē：「 wú shēn fēi wú yǒu yě， shú yǒu zhī zāi？」 yuē：「 shì tiān dì zhī wěi xíng yě； shēng fēi rǔ yǒu， shì tiān dì zhī wěi hé yě； xìng mìng fēi rǔ yǒu， shì tiān dì zhī wěi shùn yě； sūn zi fēi rǔ yǒu， shì tiān dì zhī wěi tuì yě。 gù xíng bù zhī suǒ wǎng， chù bù zhī suǒ chí， shí bù zhī suǒ wèi。 tiān dì zhī qiáng yáng qì yě， yòu hú kě dé ér yǒu xié？」

**EN:** Shun asked Cheng (丞): "Can the Way be gotten and possessed?" He said: "Your own body is not yours to possess — how could you possess the Way?" Shun said: "If my body is not mine to possess, whose is it?" He said: "It is a form entrusted by heaven and earth. Life is not yours to possess — it is a harmony entrusted by heaven and earth. Your inborn lot is not yours to possess — it is a course entrusted by heaven and earth. Your children and grandchildren are not yours to possess — they are a molting entrusted by heaven and earth. So: walk, not knowing where you go; dwell, not knowing what you hold; eat, not knowing what you taste. It is the strong roving breath of heaven and earth — how could it be gotten and possessed?"

## Section 9

**ZH:** 孔子問於老聃曰：「今日晏閒，敢問至道。」
**PY:** kǒng zi wèn yú lǎo dān yuē：「 jīn rì yàn xián， gǎn wèn zhì dào。」

**EN:** Confucius asked Lao Dan: "Today, at quiet leisure, I venture to ask about the utmost Way."

## Section 10

**ZH:** 老聃曰：「汝齊戒，疏𤅢而心，澡雪而精神，掊擊而知！夫道，窅然難言哉！將為汝言其崖略。
**PY:** lǎo dān yuē：「 rǔ qí jiè， shū 𤅢 ér xīn， zǎo xuě ér jīng shén， póu jī ér zhī！ fū dào， yǎo rán nán yán zāi！ jiāng wèi rǔ yán qí yá lüè。

**EN:** Lao Dan said: "Fast and keep vigil; dredge and channel your heart; bathe and snow your spirit; beat down your knowledge! The Way — how deep and hard to tell of! I will tell you its rough outline."

## Section 11

**ZH:** 夫昭昭生於冥冥，有倫生於無形，精神生於道，形本生於精，而萬物以形相生，故九竅者胎生，八竅者卵生。其來無跡，其往無崖，無門無房，四達之皇皇也。邀於此者，四肢彊，思慮恂達，耳目聰明，其用心不勞，其應物無方。天不得不高，地不得不廣，日月不得不行，萬物不得不昌，此其道與！
**PY:** fū zhāo zhāo shēng yú míng míng， yǒu lún shēng yú wú xíng， jīng shén shēng yú dào， xíng běn shēng yú jīng， ér wàn wù yǐ xíng xiāng shēng， gù jiǔ qiào zhě tāi shēng， bā qiào zhě luǎn shēng。 qí lái wú jī， qí wǎng wú yá， wú mén wú fáng， sì dá zhī huáng huáng yě。 yāo yú cǐ zhě， sì zhī jiàng， sī lǜ xún dá， ěr mù cōng míng， qí yòng xīn bù láo， qí yīng wù wú fāng。 tiān bù dé bù gāo， dì bù dé bù guǎng， rì yuè bù dé bù xíng， wàn wù bù dé bù chāng， cǐ qí dào yǔ！

**EN:** "Brightness is born of darkness; order is born of the formless; the spirit is born of the Way; the root of form is born of essence — and the ten thousand things give birth to one another by form. Therefore those with nine openings are born from the womb; those with eight openings are born from the egg. Its coming leaves no track; its going has no shore; it has no gate, no room — vast and open in all four directions. One who meets with this has strong four limbs; his thoughts and considerations are free and penetrating; his ears and eyes are keen; he uses his heart without toil; he responds to things without direction. Heaven cannot help but be high; earth cannot help but be broad; sun and moon cannot help but move; the ten thousand things cannot help but flourish. This is its Way!"

## Section 12

**ZH:** 且夫博之不必知，辯之不必慧，聖人以斷之矣。若夫益之而不加益，損之而不加損者，聖人之所保也。淵淵乎其若海，魏魏乎其終則復始也，運量萬物而不匱，則君子之道，彼其外與！萬物皆往資焉而不匱，此其道與！
**PY:** qiě fū bó zhī bù bì zhī， biàn zhī bù bì huì， shèng rén yǐ duàn zhī yǐ。 ruò fú yì zhī ér bù jiā yì， sǔn zhī ér bù jiā sǔn zhě， shèng rén zhī suǒ bǎo yě。 yuān yuān hū qí ruò hǎi， wèi wèi hū qí zhōng zé fù shǐ yě， yùn liàng wàn wù ér bù guì， zé jūn zǐ zhī dào， bǐ qí wài yǔ！ wàn wù jiē wǎng zī yān ér bù guì， cǐ qí dào yǔ！

**EN:** "Besides, breadth of learning does not guarantee knowledge; disputation does not guarantee wisdom — the sage has cut such things off. As for that which, added to, is not increased, and diminished, is not diminished — that is what the sage keeps. Deep as the sea; towering — ending, it begins again; it moves and measures the ten thousand things without running out. The way of the gentleman is but its outer fringe! The ten thousand things all go to it for their supply, and it never runs out. This is its Way!"

## Section 13

**ZH:** 中國有人焉，非陰非陽，處於天地之閒，直且為人，將反於宗。自本觀之，生者，暗醷物也。雖有壽夭，相去幾何？須臾之說也。奚足以為堯、桀之是非？
**PY:** zhōng guó yǒu rén yān， fēi yīn fēi yáng， chù yú tiān dì zhī xián， zhí qiě wèi rén， jiāng fǎn yú zōng。 zì běn guān zhī， shēng zhě， àn yì wù yě。 suī yǒu shòu yāo， xiāng qù jǐ hé？ xū yú zhī shuō yě。 xī zú yǐ wèi yáo、 jié zhī shì fēi？

**EN:** "Here is a man within the Middle Kingdom — neither yin nor yang, dwelling between heaven and earth, for the moment simply a man, about to return to his source. Seen from the root, what lives is a lump of fermenting vapors. Though there be long life and early death — how far apart are they? The tale of a moment. How could that be enough to settle the rights and wrongs of Yao and Jie!"

## Section 14

**ZH:** 果蓏有理，人倫雖難，所以相齒。聖人遭之而不違，過之而不守。調而應之，德也；偶而應之，道也。帝之所興，王之所起也。
**PY:** guǒ luǒ yǒu lǐ， rén lún suī nán， suǒ yǐ xiāng chǐ。 shèng rén zāo zhī ér bù wéi， guò zhī ér bù shǒu。 diào ér yīng zhī， dé yě； ǒu ér yīng zhī， dào yě。 dì zhī suǒ xìng， wáng zhī suǒ qǐ yě。

**EN:** "Fruits and melons have their pattern; human relations, difficult as they are, have that by which they fit together tooth on tooth. The sage, meeting them, does not defy them; passing them, does not hold to them. To meet them in tune and respond is virtue; to meet them as counterpart and respond is the Way. It is that by which emperors arose and kings arose."

## Section 15

**ZH:** 人生天地之間，若白駒之過郤，忽然而已。注然勃然，莫不出焉；油然漻然，莫不入焉。已化而生，又化而死，生物哀之，人類悲之。解其天弢，墮其天𧙍，紛乎宛乎，魂魄將往，乃身從之，乃大歸乎！
**PY:** rén shēng tiān dì zhī jiān， ruò bái jū zhī guò xì， hū rán ér yǐ。 zhù rán bó rán， mò bù chū yān； yóu rán liáo rán， mò bù rù yān。 yǐ huà ér shēng， yòu huà ér sǐ， shēng wù āi zhī， rén lèi bēi zhī。 jiě qí tiān tāo， duò qí tiān 𧙍， fēn hū wǎn hū， hún pò jiāng wǎng， nǎi shēn cóng zhī， nǎi dà guī hū！

**EN:** "Man's life between heaven and earth is like a white colt passing the crack — a flash, and it is over. Welling, surging — there is nothing that does not come forth; gliding, dissolving — there is nothing that does not go back in. Having transformed, it is born; transforming again, it dies. Living things grieve over it; mankind sorrows over it. Undo its heavenly bow-case; cast off its heavenly wrapping — tumbling, turning, the souls are about to go, and the body follows after: the Great Return!"

## Section 16

**ZH:** 不形之形，形之不形，是人之所同知也，非將至之所務也，此眾人之所同論也。彼至則不論，論則不至。明見無值，辯不若默。道不可聞，聞不若塞。此之謂大得。」
**PY:** bù xíng zhī xíng， xíng zhī bù xíng， shì rén zhī suǒ tóng zhī yě， fēi jiāng zhì zhī suǒ wù yě， cǐ zhòng rén zhī suǒ tóng lùn yě。 bǐ zhì zé bù lùn， lùn zé bù zhì。 míng jiàn wú zhí， biàn bù ruò mò。 dào bù kě wén， wén bù ruò sāi。 cǐ zhī wèi dà dé。」

**EN:** "That the formless becomes form and form becomes formless — this all men know together; it is not what one who is arriving labors at. It is what the multitude discusses together. He who arrives does not discuss; he who discusses does not arrive. To see it clearly is to meet nothing; disputation cannot compare with silence. The Way cannot be heard; hearing cannot compare with stopping up. This is called the great attainment."

## Section 17

**ZH:** 東郭子問於莊子曰：「所謂道，惡乎在？」莊子曰：「無所不在。」東郭子曰：「期而後可。」莊子曰：「在螻蟻。」曰：「何其下邪？」曰：「在稊稗。」曰：「何其愈下邪？」曰：「在瓦甓。」曰：「何其愈甚邪？」曰：「在屎溺。」東郭子不應。
**PY:** dōng guō zi wèn yú zhuāng zi yuē：「 suǒ wèi dào， è hū zài？」 zhuāng zi yuē：「 wú suǒ bù zài。」 dōng guō zi yuē：「 qī ér hòu kě。」 zhuāng zi yuē：「 zài lóu yǐ。」 yuē：「 hé qí xià xié？」 yuē：「 zài tí bài。」 yuē：「 hé qí yù xià xié？」 yuē：「 zài wǎ pì。」 yuē：「 hé qí yù shèn xié？」 yuē：「 zài shǐ nì。」 dōng guō zi bù yīng。

**EN:** Dongguozi asked Zhuangzi: "This thing called the Way — where is it?" Zhuangzi said: "There is nowhere it is not." Dongguozi said: "Name a place, and then I shall be satisfied." Zhuangzi said: "In the ants." He said: "How low!" He said: "In the wild rice-grass." He said: "Lower still!" He said: "In the tiles and shards." He said: "Worse and worse!" He said: "In the piss and dung." Dongguozi did not answer.

## Section 18

**ZH:** 莊子曰：「夫子之問也，固不足質。正獲之問於監市履狶也，每下愈況。汝唯莫必，無乎逃物。至道若是，大言亦然。周、遍、咸三者，異名同實，其指一也。嘗相與游乎無何有之宮，同合而論，無所終窮乎！嘗相與無為乎！澹而靜乎！漠而清乎！調而閒乎！寥已吾志，無往焉而不知其所至；去而來而不知其所止，吾已往來焉而不知其所終；彷徨乎馮閎，大知入焉而不知其所窮。物物者與物無際，而物有際者，所謂物際者也；不際之際，際之不際者也。謂盈虛衰殺，彼為盈虛非盈虛，彼為衰殺非衰殺，彼為本末非本末，彼為積散非積散也。」
**PY:** zhuāng zi yuē：「 fū zǐ zhī wèn yě， gù bù zú zhì。 zhèng huò zhī wèn yú jiān shì lǚ xī yě， měi xià yù kuàng。 rǔ wéi mò bì， wú hū táo wù。 zhì dào ruò shì， dà yán yì rán。 zhōu、 biàn、 xián sān zhě， yì míng tóng shí， qí zhǐ yī yě。 cháng xiāng yǔ yóu hū wú hé yǒu zhī gōng， tóng hé ér lùn， wú suǒ zhōng qióng hū！ cháng xiāng yǔ wú wèi hū！ dàn ér jìng hū！ mò ér qīng hū！ diào ér xián hū！ liáo yǐ wú zhì， wú wǎng yān ér bù zhī qí suǒ zhì； qù ér lái ér bù zhī qí suǒ zhǐ， wú yǐ wǎng lái yān ér bù zhī qí suǒ zhōng； páng huáng hū féng hóng， dà zhī rù yān ér bù zhī qí suǒ qióng。 wù wù zhě yǔ wù wú jì， ér wù yǒu jì zhě， suǒ wèi wù jì zhě yě； bù jì zhī jì， jì zhī bù jì zhě yě。 wèi yíng xū shuāi shā， bǐ wèi yíng xū fēi yíng xū， bǐ wèi shuāi shā fēi shuāi shā， bǐ wèi běn mò fēi běn mò， bǐ wèi jī sàn fēi jī sàn yě。」

**EN:** Zhuangzi said: "Your question, sir, inherently cannot touch the substance. When Inspector Huo asked the market overseer about treading pigs to gauge their fatness, the answer was: the lower down, the better the gauge. Do not insist on some one place — there is no escaping it in things. The utmost Way is like this, and so are great words. Whole, universal, all — three names for the same substance; their purport is one. Let us try to roam together in the palace of Nothing-Whatever, to join in one discourse, never to be exhausted! Let us try together to be without action! How calm and still! How indifferent and clear! How attuned and at ease! My will is empty and still: I go nowhere and do not know where I arrive; I leave and return and do not know where I stop; I have gone back and forth in it and do not know where it ends; I wander in the vast and boundless; great knowledge enters it and does not know its limit. That which treats things as things has no boundary with things; that within things which has boundaries is what is called the boundary of things. The boundaryless boundary; the bounded boundarylessness. Speak of fullness and emptiness, decline and decay: it makes fullness and emptiness, yet is not full or empty; it makes decline and decay, yet does not decline or decay; it makes root and branch, yet is neither root nor branch; it makes gathering and scattering, yet neither gathers nor scatters."

## Section 19

**ZH:** 婀荷甘與神農同學於老龍吉。神農隱几闔戶晝瞑，婀荷甘日中奓戶而入，曰：「老龍死矣！」神農隱几擁杖而起，嚗然放杖而笑，曰：「天知予僻陋慢訑，故棄予而死。已矣！夫子無所發予之狂言而死矣夫！」
**PY:** ē hé gān yǔ shén nóng tóng xué yú lǎo lóng jí。 shén nóng yǐn jǐ hé hù zhòu míng， ē hé gān rì zhōng zhā hù ér rù， yuē：「 lǎo lóng sǐ yǐ！」 shén nóng yǐn jǐ yōng zhàng ér qǐ， bó rán fàng zhàng ér xiào， yuē：「 tiān zhī yǔ pì lòu màn yí， gù qì yǔ ér sǐ。 yǐ yǐ！ fū zǐ wú suǒ fā yǔ zhī kuáng yán ér sǐ yǐ fū！」

**EN:** Ahegan (婀荷甘) and Shennong studied together under Old Longji (老龍吉). Shennong, leaning on his armrest, had shut his door for a midday nap. Ahegan at noon pushed open the door and came in: "Old Long is dead!" Shennong, from his armrest, took up his staff and rose; then with a crash he threw down the staff and laughed: "Heaven knew I was low and narrow, slow and heedless, so it abandoned me and died. It is over! The master died without opening for me the least of his wild words!"

## Section 20

**ZH:** 弇堈弔聞之，曰：「夫體道者，天下之君子所繫焉。今於道，秋豪之端，萬分未得處一焉，而猶知藏其狂言而死，又況夫體道者乎！視之無形，聽之無聲，於人之論者，謂之冥冥，所以論道，而非道也。」
**PY:** yǎn gāng diào wén zhī， yuē：「 fū tǐ dào zhě， tiān xià zhī jūn zǐ suǒ xì yān。 jīn yú dào， qiū háo zhī duān， wàn fēn wèi dé chù yī yān， ér yóu zhī cáng qí kuáng yán ér sǐ， yòu kuàng fū tǐ dào zhě hū！ shì zhī wú xíng， tīng zhī wú shēng， yú rén zhī lùn zhě， wèi zhī míng míng， suǒ yǐ lùn dào， ér fēi dào yě。」

**EN:** Yangang Diao (弇堈弔), hearing of this, said: "One who embodies the Way is that to which all the gentlemen of the world are tied. Now this man, in the Way, had not attained one ten-thousandth of the tip of an autumn hair — and yet he knew to hide away his wild words and die. How much more one who embodies the Way! Look at it — it has no form; listen to it — it has no sound. What men discourse upon is called the dark darkness; what they discourse on as the Way is not the Way."

## Section 21

**ZH:** 於是泰清問乎無窮曰：「子知道乎？」無窮曰：「吾不知。」又問乎無為。無為曰：「吾知道。」曰：「子之知道，亦有數乎？」曰：「有。」曰：「其數若何？」無為曰：「吾知道之可以貴，可以賤，可以約，可以散。此吾所以知道之數也。」泰清以之言也問乎無始，曰：「若是，則無窮之弗知，與無為之知，孰是而孰非乎？」無始曰：「不知深矣，知之淺矣；弗知內矣，知之外矣。」於是泰清中而歎曰：「弗知乃知乎！知乃不知乎！孰知不知之知？」無始曰：「道不可聞，聞而非也；道不可見，見而非也；道不可言，言而非也。知形形之不形乎？道不當名。」
**PY:** yú shì tài qīng wèn hū wú qióng yuē：「 zi zhī dào hū？」 wú qióng yuē：「 wú bù zhī。」 yòu wèn hū wú wèi。 wú wèi yuē：「 wú zhī dào。」 yuē：「 zi zhī zhī dào， yì yǒu shù hū？」 yuē：「 yǒu。」 yuē：「 qí shù ruò hé？」 wú wèi yuē：「 wú zhī dào zhī kě yǐ guì， kě yǐ jiàn， kě yǐ yuē， kě yǐ sàn。 cǐ wú suǒ yǐ zhī dào zhī shù yě。」 tài qīng yǐ zhī yán yě wèn hū wú shǐ， yuē：「 ruò shì， zé wú qióng zhī fú zhī， yǔ wú wèi zhī zhī， shú shì ér shú fēi hū？」 wú shǐ yuē：「 bù zhī shēn yǐ， zhī zhī qiǎn yǐ； fú zhī nèi yǐ， zhī zhī wài yǐ。」 yú shì tài qīng zhōng ér tàn yuē：「 fú zhī nǎi zhī hū！ zhī nǎi bù zhī hū！ shú zhī bù zhī zhī zhī？」 wú shǐ yuē：「 dào bù kě wén， wén ér fēi yě； dào bù kě jiàn， jiàn ér fēi yě； dào bù kě yán， yán ér fēi yě。 zhī xíng xíng zhī bù xíng hū？ dào bù dāng míng。」

**EN:** Thereupon Grand Clarity (泰清) asked Infinite (無窮): "Do you know the Way?" Infinite said: "I do not know." He asked Non-Action (無為) again. Non-Action said: "I know the Way." He said: "Your knowing the Way — does it have a method?" He said: "It has." He said: "What is its method?" Non-Action said: "I know the Way can be honored, can be despised, can be gathered, can be scattered. This is the method by which I know the Way." Grand Clarity took these words and asked Beginningless (無始): "If it is so, then of Infinite's not-knowing and Non-Action's knowing — who is right and who is wrong?" Beginningless said: "Not-knowing is deep; knowing is shallow. Not-knowing is within; knowing is without." Thereupon Grand Clarity, midway, sighed: "Not-knowing is knowing! Knowing is not-knowing! Who knows the knowing of not-knowing?" Beginningless said: "The Way cannot be heard; heard, it is not it. The Way cannot be seen; seen, it is not it. The Way cannot be spoken; spoken, it is not it. Do you know that what forms the formed is formless? The Way admits no name."

## Section 22

**ZH:** 無始曰：「有問道而應之者，不知道也。雖問道者，亦未聞道。道無問，問無應。無問問之，是問窮也；無應應之，是無內也。以無內待問窮，若是者，外不觀乎宇宙，內不知乎太初，是以不過乎崑崙，不遊乎太虛。」
**PY:** wú shǐ yuē：「 yǒu wèn dào ér yīng zhī zhě， bù zhī dào yě。 suī wèn dào zhě， yì wèi wén dào。 dào wú wèn， wèn wú yīng。 wú wèn wèn zhī， shì wèn qióng yě； wú yīng yīng zhī， shì wú nèi yě。 yǐ wú nèi dài wèn qióng， ruò shì zhě， wài bù guān hū yǔ zhòu， nèi bù zhī hū tài chū， shì yǐ bù guò hū kūn lún， bù yóu hū tài xū。」

**EN:** Beginningless said: "One who, asked about the Way, answers — does not know the Way. And even the one who asks has not heard the Way. The Way has no asking; asking has no answering. To ask what has no asking is a bankrupt asking; to answer what has no answering is a contentless answering. To meet a bankrupt asking with a contentless answering — being such, outwardly one has not beheld the universe; inwardly one does not know the Grand Beginning. Therefore one cannot pass beyond Kunlun; one cannot roam in the Great Void."

## Section 23

**ZH:** 光曜問乎無有曰：「夫子有乎，其無有乎？」光曜不得問，而孰視其狀貌，窅然空然，終日視之而不見，聽之而不聞，搏之而不得也。光曜曰：「至矣！其孰能至此乎！予能有無矣，而未能無無也，及為無有矣，何從至此哉！」
**PY:** guāng yào wèn hū wú yǒu yuē：「 fū zǐ yǒu hū， qí wú yǒu hū？」 guāng yào bù dé wèn， ér shú shì qí zhuàng mào， yǎo rán kōng rán， zhōng rì shì zhī ér bù jiàn， tīng zhī ér bù wén， bó zhī ér bù dé yě。 guāng yào yuē：「 zhì yǐ！ qí shú néng zhì cǐ hū！ yǔ néng yǒu wú yǐ， ér wèi néng wú wú yě， jí wèi wú yǒu yǐ， hé cóng zhì cǐ zāi！」

**EN:** Light's Brilliance (光曜) asked Nothingness (無有): "Do you, Master, have being, or do you not have being?" Light's Brilliance got no answer, and looked intently at his face and form: deep and empty. All day he looked and did not see; listened and did not hear; grasped and did not touch. Light's Brilliance said: "The utmost! Who could arrive at this! I can manage to have nothing, but not yet to have no nothing; and as for becoming Nothingness — by what road could I arrive at that!"

## Section 24

**ZH:** 大馬之捶鉤者，年八十矣，而不失豪芒。大馬曰：「子巧與？有道與？」曰：「臣有守也。臣之年二十而好捶鉤，於物無視也，非鉤無察也。是用之者，假不用者也以長得其用，而況乎無不用者乎！物孰不資焉？」
**PY:** dà mǎ zhī chuí gōu zhě， nián bā shí yǐ， ér bù shī háo máng。 dà mǎ yuē：「 zi qiǎo yǔ？ yǒu dào yǔ？」 yuē：「 chén yǒu shǒu yě。 chén zhī nián èr shí ér hǎo chuí gōu， yú wù wú shì yě， fēi gōu wú chá yě。 shì yòng zhī zhě， jiǎ bù yòng zhě yě yǐ zhǎng dé qí yòng， ér kuàng hū wú bù yòng zhě hū！ wù shú bù zī yān？」

**EN:** The Grand Steward's hook-forger was eighty years old, and he never missed by a hair's breadth. The Grand Steward said: "Are you skillful, or do you have a Way?" He said: "Your servant has something he keeps. At twenty your servant took delight in forging hooks; at things he never looked; what was not a hook he never examined. Thus what is used is borrowed from what is not used, so that the use lasts long. How much more for one who never fails to use nothing! What thing would not draw its supply from him?"

## Section 25

**ZH:** 冉求問於仲尼曰：「未有天地可知邪？」仲尼曰：「可。古猶今也。」冉求失問而退，明日復見，曰：「昔者吾問『未有天地可知乎』，夫子曰：『可。古猶今也。』昔者吾昭然，今日吾昧然，敢問何謂也？」仲尼曰：「昔之昭然也，神者先受之；今之昧然也，且又為不神者求邪？無古無今，無始無終。未有子孫而有子孫，可乎？」冉求未對。仲尼曰：「已矣，末應矣！不以生生死，不以死死生。死生有待邪？皆有所一體。有先天地生者物邪？物物者非物。物出不得先物也，猶其有物也。猶其有物也，無已。聖人之愛人也終無已者，亦乃取於是者也。」
**PY:** rǎn qiú wèn yú zhòng ní yuē：「 wèi yǒu tiān dì kě zhī xié？」 zhòng ní yuē：「 kě。 gǔ yóu jīn yě。」 rǎn qiú shī wèn ér tuì， míng rì fù jiàn， yuē：「 xī zhě wú wèn『 wèi yǒu tiān dì kě zhī hū』， fū zǐ yuē：『 kě。 gǔ yóu jīn yě。』 xī zhě wú zhāo rán， jīn rì wú mèi rán， gǎn wèn hé wèi yě？」 zhòng ní yuē：「 xī zhī zhāo rán yě， shén zhě xiān shòu zhī； jīn zhī mèi rán yě， qiě yòu wèi bù shén zhě qiú xié？ wú gǔ wú jīn， wú shǐ wú zhōng。 wèi yǒu zi sūn ér yǒu zi sūn， kě hū？」 rǎn qiú wèi duì。 zhòng ní yuē：「 yǐ yǐ， mò yīng yǐ！ bù yǐ shēng shēng sǐ， bù yǐ sǐ sǐ shēng。 sǐ shēng yǒu dài xié？ jiē yǒu suǒ yī tǐ。 yǒu xiān tiān dì shēng zhě wù xié？ wù wù zhě fēi wù。 wù chū bù dé xiān wù yě， yóu qí yǒu wù yě。 yóu qí yǒu wù yě， wú yǐ。 shèng rén zhī ài rén yě zhōng wú yǐ zhě， yì nǎi qǔ yú shì zhě yě。」

**EN:** Ran Qiu (冉求) asked Confucius: "Before heaven and earth existed — can that be known?" Confucius said: "It can. The past is as the present." Ran Qiu, at a loss for his next question, withdrew. The next day he saw him again and said: "Yesterday I asked, 'Before heaven and earth existed — can that be known?' and you, Master, said, 'It can. The past is as the present.' Yesterday I was clear; today I am dark. May I ask what it means?" Confucius said: "Yesterday you were clear because your spirit received it first. Today you are dark because you seek it again with what is not spirit. There is no past, no present; no beginning, no end. That, not yet having children and grandchildren, one has children and grandchildren — is that admissible?" Ran Qiu made no answer. Confucius said: "Enough — do not answer! One does not use life to give birth to death; one does not use death to put life to death. Do death and life wait upon anything? Each has its one body. Is there any thing that was born before heaven and earth? That which treats things as things is not a thing. Things that come forth cannot precede things; yet it is as though there were things; as though there were things — endlessly. The sage's love for men, endless to the last, is also taken from this."

## Section 26

**ZH:** 顏淵問乎仲尼曰：「回嘗聞諸夫子曰：『無有所將，無有所迎。』回敢問其遊。」仲尼曰：「古之人，外化而內不化；今之人，內化而外不化。與物化者，一不化者也。安化安不化，安與之相靡，必與之莫多。狶韋氏之囿，黃帝之圃，有虞氏之宮，湯、武之室。君子之人，若儒、墨者師，故以是非相𩐋也，而況今之人乎！聖人處物不傷物。不傷物者，物亦不能傷也。唯無所傷者，為能與人相將、迎。山林與！皋壤與！使我欣欣然而樂與！樂未畢也，哀又繼之。哀樂之來，吾不能禦，其去弗能止。悲夫！世人直為物逆旅耳！夫知遇而不知所不遇，知能能而不能所不能。無知無能者，固人之所不免也。夫務免乎人之所不免者，豈不亦悲哉！至言去言，至為去為。齊知之所知，則淺矣。」
**PY:** yán yuān wèn hū zhòng ní yuē：「 huí cháng wén zhū fū zǐ yuē：『 wú yǒu suǒ jiāng， wú yǒu suǒ yíng。』 huí gǎn wèn qí yóu。」 zhòng ní yuē：「 gǔ zhī rén， wài huà ér nèi bù huà； jīn zhī rén， nèi huà ér wài bù huà。 yǔ wù huà zhě， yī bù huà zhě yě。 ān huà ān bù huà， ān yǔ zhī xiāng mí， bì yǔ zhī mò duō。 xī wéi shì zhī yòu， huáng dì zhī pǔ， yǒu yú shì zhī gōng， tāng、 wǔ zhī shì。 jūn zǐ zhī rén， ruò rú、 mò zhě shī， gù yǐ shì fēi xiāng 𩐋 yě， ér kuàng jīn zhī rén hū！ shèng rén chù wù bù shāng wù。 bù shāng wù zhě， wù yì bù néng shāng yě。 wéi wú suǒ shāng zhě， wèi néng yǔ rén xiāng jiāng、 yíng。 shān lín yǔ！ gāo rǎng yǔ！ shǐ wǒ xīn xīn rán ér lè yǔ！ lè wèi bì yě， āi yòu jì zhī。 āi lè zhī lái， wú bù néng yù， qí qù fú néng zhǐ。 bēi fū！ shì rén zhí wèi wù nì lǚ ěr！ fū zhī yù ér bù zhī suǒ bù yù， zhī néng néng ér bù néng suǒ bù néng。 wú zhī wú néng zhě， gù rén zhī suǒ bù miǎn yě。 fū wù miǎn hū rén zhī suǒ bù miǎn zhě， qǐ bù yì bēi zāi！ zhì yán qù yán， zhì wèi qù wèi。 qí zhī zhī suǒ zhī， zé qiǎn yǐ。」

**EN:** Yan Yuan asked Confucius: "I once heard from you, Master: 'Nothing to escort away, nothing to run to welcome.' May I ask about the roaming of it?" Confucius said: "The men of old transformed without, but did not transform within; the men of today transform within, but do not transform without. One who transforms along with things has a oneness that does not transform. At peace with change, at peace with no change, at peace going along grinding with them — one is sure not to exceed them. The park of Xiwei; the garden of the Yellow Emperor; the palace of Youyu; the chambers of Tang and Wu — ever smaller the lodging. And gentlemen like the teachers of the Confucians and Mohists still batter one another over right and wrong — how much more the men of today! The sage, dwelling among things, does not injure things. Not injuring things, things also cannot injure him. Only one who injures nothing can be the one to escort and welcome men. The mountain forests! The banks and fields! They make me glad and joyful! But the joy is not yet finished when sorrow follows after it. When sorrow and joy come, I cannot keep them out; when they go, I cannot stop them. Alas! The men of the world are nothing but a lodging-house for things! They know what they meet and do not know what they do not meet; they can what they can and cannot what they cannot. To have no knowledge and no ability — that, surely, no man escapes. To labor at escaping what no man escapes — is that not also sad! Utmost speech leaves speech behind; utmost action leaves action behind. To make all knowledge equal to what knowledge knows — that is shallow."
