# The Great and Most Honoured Master (大宗師)
Source: Zhuangzi _ Inner Chapters _ The Great and Most Honoured Master - Chinese Text Project.html
Status: complete (14 sections)

## Section 1

**ZH:** 知天之所為，知人之所為者，至矣。知天之所為者，天而生也；知人之所為者，以其知之所知，以養其知之所不知，終其天年而不中道夭者，是知之盛也。雖然，有患。夫知有所待而後當，其所待者特未定也。庸詎知吾所謂天之非人乎？所謂人之非天乎？且有真人，而後有真知。
**PY:** zhī tiān zhī suǒ wèi， zhī rén zhī suǒ wèi zhě， zhì yǐ。 zhī tiān zhī suǒ wèi zhě， tiān ér shēng yě； zhī rén zhī suǒ wèi zhě， yǐ qí zhī zhī suǒ zhī， yǐ yǎng qí zhī zhī suǒ bù zhī， zhōng qí tiān nián ér bù zhōng dào yāo zhě， shì zhī zhī shèng yě。 suī rán， yǒu huàn。 fū zhī yǒu suǒ dài ér hòu dāng， qí suǒ dài zhě tè wèi dìng yě。 yōng jù zhī wú suǒ wèi tiān zhī fēi rén hū？ suǒ wèi rén zhī fēi tiān hū？ qiě yǒu zhēn rén， ér hòu yǒu zhēn zhī。

**EN:** To know what Heaven does, and to know what man does — that is the utmost. To know what Heaven does is to be born of Heaven. To know what man does is to use what one's knowledge knows to nourish what one's knowledge does not know, to live out one's heaven-allotted years and not die in mid-course — that is the fullness of knowledge. Even so, there is a difficulty. Knowledge must wait upon something before it can be fitting, yet that upon which it waits is never fixed. How do I know that what I call Heaven is not man? And that what I call man is not Heaven? Only when there is a true man is there true knowledge.

## Section 2

**ZH:** 何謂真人？古之真人，不逆寡，不雄成，不謨士。若然者，過而弗悔，當而不自得也。若然者，登高不慄，入水不濡，入火不熱。是知之能登假於道也若此。
**PY:** hé wèi zhēn rén？ gǔ zhī zhēn rén， bù nì guǎ， bù xióng chéng， bù mó shì。 ruò rán zhě， guò ér fú huǐ， dāng ér bù zì dé yě。 ruò rán zhě， dēng gāo bù lì， rù shuǐ bù rú， rù huǒ bù rè。 shì zhī zhī néng dēng jiǎ yú dào yě ruò cǐ。

**EN:** What is a true man? The true men of old did not resist the few, did not strut in success, did not scheme over affairs. Such a one could err without regret, and hit the mark without self-congratulation. Such a one could climb the heights without trembling, enter the water without being soaked, enter the fire without being burned. This is how it is with knowledge that can mount up to the Way.

## Section 3

**ZH:** 古之真人，其寢不夢，其覺無憂，其食不甘，其息深深。真人之息以踵，眾人之息以喉。屈服者，其嗌言若哇。其耆欲深者，其天機淺。
**PY:** gǔ zhī zhēn rén， qí qǐn bù mèng， qí jué wú yōu， qí shí bù gān， qí xī shēn shēn。 zhēn rén zhī xī yǐ zhǒng， zhòng rén zhī xī yǐ hóu。 qū fú zhě， qí ài yán ruò wa。 qí qí yù shēn zhě， qí tiān jī qiǎn。

**EN:** The true men of old slept without dreaming, woke without care, ate without savoring, and breathed from the depths. The true man breathes from his heels; ordinary men breathe from their throats. Those bent down in submission swallow their words as though gagging. Those whose cravings and desires run deep — their heaven-born impulse is shallow.

## Section 4

**ZH:** 古之真人，不知說生，不知惡死；其出不訢，其入不距；翛然而往，翛然而來而已矣。不忘其所始，不求其所終；受而喜之，忘而復之。是之謂不以心捐道，不以人助天。是之謂真人。若然者，其心志，其容寂，其顙頯，淒然似秋，煖然似春，喜怒通四時，與物有宜，而莫知其極。故聖人之用兵也，亡國而不失人心；利澤施於萬物，不為愛人。故樂通物，非聖人也；有親，非仁也；天時，非賢也；利害不通，非君子也；行名失己，非士也；亡身不真，非役人也。若狐不偕、務光、伯夷、叔齊、箕子胥餘、紀他、申徒狄，是役人之役，適人之適，而不自適其適者也。
**PY:** gǔ zhī zhēn rén， bù zhī shuō shēng， bù zhī è sǐ； qí chū bù xīn， qí rù bù jù； xiāo rán ér wǎng， xiāo rán ér lái ér yǐ yǐ。 bù wàng qí suǒ shǐ， bù qiú qí suǒ zhōng； shòu ér xǐ zhī， wàng ér fù zhī。 shì zhī wèi bù yǐ xīn juān dào， bù yǐ rén zhù tiān。 shì zhī wèi zhēn rén。 ruò rán zhě， qí xīn zhì， qí róng jì， qí sǎng kuí， qī rán shì qiū， nuǎn rán shì chūn， xǐ nù tōng sì shí， yǔ wù yǒu yí， ér mò zhī qí jí。 gù shèng rén zhī yòng bīng yě， wáng guó ér bù shī rén xīn； lì zé shī yú wàn wù， bù wèi ài rén。 gù lè tōng wù， fēi shèng rén yě； yǒu qīn， fēi rén yě； tiān shí， fēi xián yě； lì hài bù tōng， fēi jūn zǐ yě； xíng míng shī jǐ， fēi shì yě； wáng shēn bù zhēn， fēi yì rén yě。 ruò hú bù xié、 wù guāng、 bó yí、 shū qí、 jī zi xū yú、 jì tā、 shēn tú dí， shì yì rén zhī yì， shì rén zhī shì， ér bù zì shì qí shì zhě yě。

**EN:** The true men of old did not know how to delight in life, did not know how to hate death. They came forth without joy and went in without resistance; carelessly they came, carelessly they went, and that was all. They did not forget their beginning; they did not seek their end. They received and delighted in it; they forgot it and returned it. This is called not using the mind to injure the Way, not using the human to help the heavenly. This is called a true man. Such a one — his mind is steadfast, his countenance calm, his forehead broad and plain; cool as autumn, warm as spring; his joy and anger pass with the four seasons, fitting all things, and none knows his limit. So when the sage uses arms, he may destroy a state yet not lose the hearts of its people; his benefits shower down on the ten thousand things, yet not from love of men. Therefore: to delight in communing with things is not to be a sage; to have favorites is not to be humane; to calculate Heaven's seasons is not to be worthy; to fail to see through benefit and harm is not to be a gentleman; to act for a name and lose oneself is not to be a scholar; to lose one's person without the truth is not to command men. Such as Hu Buxie (狐不偕), Wuguang (務光), Boyi (伯夷), Shuqi (叔齊), Jizi Xuyu (箕子胥餘), Ji Ta (紀他), and Shentu Di (申徒狄) — these were the servants of others' servants, who suited others' suiting, but did not suit their own suiting.

## Section 5

**ZH:** 古之真人，其狀義而不朋，若不足而不承，與乎其觚而不堅也，張乎其虛而不華也，邴邴乎其似喜乎！崔乎其不得已乎！滀乎進我色也，與乎止我德也，厲乎其似世乎！謷乎其未可制也，連乎其似好閉也，悗乎忘其言也。以刑為體，以禮為翼，以知為時，以德為循。以刑為體者，綽乎其殺也；以禮為翼者，所以行於世也；以知為時者，不得已於事也；以德為循者，言其與有足者至於丘也，而人真以為勤行者也。故其好之也一，其弗好之也一。其一也一，其不一也一。其一，與天為徒；其不一，與人為徒。天與人不相勝也，是之謂真人。
**PY:** gǔ zhī zhēn rén， qí zhuàng yì ér bù péng， ruò bù zú ér bù chéng， yǔ hū qí gū ér bù jiān yě， zhāng hū qí xū ér bù huá yě， bǐng bǐng hū qí shì xǐ hū！ cuī hū qí bù dé yǐ hū！ chù hū jìn wǒ sè yě， yǔ hū zhǐ wǒ dé yě， lì hū qí shì shì hū！ áo hū qí wèi kě zhì yě， lián hū qí shì hǎo bì yě， mán hū wàng qí yán yě。 yǐ xíng wèi tǐ， yǐ lǐ wèi yì， yǐ zhī wèi shí， yǐ dé wèi xún。 yǐ xíng wèi tǐ zhě， chuò hū qí shā yě； yǐ lǐ wèi yì zhě， suǒ yǐ xíng yú shì yě； yǐ zhī wèi shí zhě， bù dé yǐ yú shì yě； yǐ dé wèi xún zhě， yán qí yǔ yǒu zú zhě zhì yú qiū yě， ér rén zhēn yǐ wèi qín xíng zhě yě。 gù qí hǎo zhī yě yī， qí fú hǎo zhī yě yī。 qí yī yě yī， qí bù yī yě yī。 qí yī， yǔ tiān wèi tú； qí bù yī， yǔ rén wèi tú。 tiān yǔ rén bù xiāng shèng yě， shì zhī wèi zhēn rén。

**EN:** The true men of old — their bearing was high but not ostentatious; seeming insufficient, they accepted nothing; poised in their singularity, they were not rigid; expansive in their emptiness, they were not showy. Brightly — as though they were happy! Toweringly — as though they could not help but move! Gatheringly — as color rises into my face! Easily — as virtue comes to rest in me! Sternly — as though one of the world! High and ungovernable! Continuously — as though fond of closing in! Vacantly — forgetting what they were saying! [uncertain: this string of descriptive 乎-clauses (邴邴乎…悗乎) is among the most disputed passages in the book; the renderings follow one coherent commentarial line, but several of the adverbs admit widely divergent readings.] They took punishment as the body, ritual as the wings, knowledge as timeliness, virtue as the course to follow. Taking punishment as the body, they were lenient in their killing. Taking ritual as wings, they walked through the world. Taking knowledge as timeliness, they acted from necessity. Taking virtue as their course, they walked with those who have feet up to the hill — and men truly thought them diligent walkers. So what they loved was one; what they did not love was one. Their oneness was one; their not-oneness was one. In their oneness they were companions of Heaven; in their not-oneness, companions of men. That Heaven and man do not overcome one another — this is called a true man.

## Section 6

**ZH:** 死生，命也，其有夜旦之常，天也。人之有所不得與，皆物之情也。彼特以天為父，而身猶愛之，而況其卓乎！人特以有君為愈乎己，而身猶死之，而況其真乎！泉涸，魚相與處於陸，相呴以溼，相濡以沫，不如相忘於江湖。與其譽堯而非桀，不如兩忘而化其道。夫大塊載我以形，勞我以生，佚我以老，息我以死。故善吾生者，乃所以善吾死也。夫藏舟於壑，藏山於澤，謂之固矣。然而夜半有力者負之而走，昧者不知也。藏大小有宜，猶有所遯。若夫藏天下於天下，而不得所遯，是恆物之大情也。特犯人之形而猶喜之，若人之形者，萬化而未始有極也，其為樂可勝計邪！故聖人將遊於物之所不得遯而皆存。善妖善老，善始善終，人猶效之，又況萬物之所係，而一化之所待乎！
**PY:** sǐ shēng， mìng yě， qí yǒu yè dàn zhī cháng， tiān yě。 rén zhī yǒu suǒ bù dé yǔ， jiē wù zhī qíng yě。 bǐ tè yǐ tiān wèi fù， ér shēn yóu ài zhī， ér kuàng qí zhuó hū！ rén tè yǐ yǒu jūn wèi yù hū jǐ， ér shēn yóu sǐ zhī， ér kuàng qí zhēn hū！ quán hé， yú xiāng yǔ chù yú lù， xiāng xǔ yǐ shī， xiāng rú yǐ mò， bù rú xiāng wàng yú jiāng hú。 yǔ qí yù yáo ér fēi jié， bù rú liǎng wàng ér huà qí dào。 fū dà kuài zài wǒ yǐ xíng， láo wǒ yǐ shēng， yì wǒ yǐ lǎo， xī wǒ yǐ sǐ。 gù shàn wú shēng zhě， nǎi suǒ yǐ shàn wú sǐ yě。 fū cáng zhōu yú hè， cáng shān yú zé， wèi zhī gù yǐ。 rán ér yè bàn yǒu lì zhě fù zhī ér zǒu， mèi zhě bù zhī yě。 cáng dà xiǎo yǒu yí， yóu yǒu suǒ dùn。 ruò fú cáng tiān xià yú tiān xià， ér bù dé suǒ dùn， shì héng wù zhī dà qíng yě。 tè fàn rén zhī xíng ér yóu xǐ zhī， ruò rén zhī xíng zhě， wàn huà ér wèi shǐ yǒu jí yě， qí wèi lè kě shèng jì xié！ gù shèng rén jiāng yóu yú wù zhī suǒ bù dé dùn ér jiē cún。 shàn yāo shàn lǎo， shàn shǐ shàn zhōng， rén yóu xiào zhī， yòu kuàng wàn wù zhī suǒ xì， ér yī huà zhī suǒ dài hū！

**EN:** Death and life are fated; that they have the constancy of night and day is Heaven. That in which man can have no part is the genuine condition of all things. They take Heaven alone as their father, and love it even in their persons — how much more that which is loftier still! Men take their lord as better than themselves, and die for him even in their persons — how much more for what is true! When the springs dry up, the fish huddle together on the land, wetting one another with their breath and dampening one another with spit — better to forget one another in the rivers and lakes. Rather than praising Yao and condemning Jie, better to forget them both and be transformed in the Way. The Great Clod burdens me with form, labors me with life, eases me with age, rests me with death. So what makes my life good is what makes my death good. Hide a boat in a ravine, hide a mountain in a marsh, and you call it secure. Yet in the middle of the night a strong man bears it away on his back, and the sleeping do not know it. Hiding the small in the large is fitting, yet something may still escape. But hide the world in the world, and there is nowhere for anything to escape to — this is the great truth of what endures. To have chanced into a human form alone is already a joy; but the human form is one of ten thousand transformations that have never begun and have no limit — how could such joy be reckoned! Therefore the sage roams where nothing can be lost and all things are preserved. To be good in early death, good in old age, good in beginning, good in end — even this men still imitate; how much more that on which all things depend, that upon which the one transformation waits!

## Section 7

**ZH:** 夫道，有情有信，無為無形；可傳而不可受，可得而不可見；自本自根，未有天地，自古以固存；神鬼神帝，生天生地；在太極之先而不為高，在六極之下而不為深；先天地生而不為久，長於上古而不為老。豨韋氏得之，以挈天地；伏犧氏得之，以襲氣母；維斗得之，終古不忒；日月得之，終古不息；堪坏得之，以襲崑崙；馮夷得之，以遊大川；肩吾得之，以處太山；黃帝得之，以登雲天；顓頊得之，以處玄宮；禺強得之，立乎北極；西王母得之，坐乎少廣，莫知其始，莫知其終；彭祖得之，上及有虞，下及五伯；傅說得之，以相武丁，奄有天下，乘東維，騎箕尾，而比於列星。
**PY:** fū dào， yǒu qíng yǒu xìn， wú wèi wú xíng； kě chuán ér bù kě shòu， kě dé ér bù kě jiàn； zì běn zì gēn， wèi yǒu tiān dì， zì gǔ yǐ gù cún； shén guǐ shén dì， shēng tiān shēng dì； zài tài jí zhī xiān ér bù wèi gāo， zài liù jí zhī xià ér bù wèi shēn； xiān tiān dì shēng ér bù wèi jiǔ， zhǎng yú shàng gǔ ér bù wèi lǎo。 xī wéi shì dé zhī， yǐ qiè tiān dì； fú xī shì dé zhī， yǐ xí qì mǔ； wéi dòu dé zhī， zhōng gǔ bù tè； rì yuè dé zhī， zhōng gǔ bù xī； kān huài dé zhī， yǐ xí kūn lún； féng yí dé zhī， yǐ yóu dà chuān； jiān wú dé zhī， yǐ chù tài shān； huáng dì dé zhī， yǐ dēng yún tiān； zhuān xū dé zhī， yǐ chù xuán gōng； yú qiáng dé zhī， lì hū běi jí； xī wáng mǔ dé zhī， zuò hū shǎo guǎng， mò zhī qí shǐ， mò zhī qí zhōng； péng zǔ dé zhī， shàng jí yǒu yú， xià jí wǔ bó； fù shuō dé zhī， yǐ xiāng wǔ dīng， yǎn yǒu tiān xià， chéng dōng wéi， qí jī wěi， ér bǐ yú liè xīng。

**EN:** The Way has genuine reality and trustworthiness, but no action and no form. It may be passed on, but cannot be received; it may be attained, but cannot be seen. It is its own source, its own root; before heaven and earth existed, it was there, secure from of old. It spiritualizes the ghosts and the gods; it gives birth to heaven and earth. It is before the supreme ultimate, yet is not high; beneath the six extremities, yet is not deep; born before heaven and earth, yet is not long-lived; older than high antiquity, yet is not old. Xiwei (豨韋氏) got it and took heaven and earth in hand; Fuxi got it and entered into the mother of breath; the Dipper got it and has never strayed from of old; the sun and moon got it and have never rested from of old; Kanpi (堪坏) got it and entered Mount Kunlun (崑崙); Pingyi (馮夷) got it and roams the great rivers; Jianwu got it and dwells on Mount Tai; the Yellow Emperor (黃帝) got it and ascended to the cloudy sky; Zhuanxu (顓頊) got it and dwells in the Dark Palace; Yuqiang (禺強) got it and stands at the North Pole; the Queen Mother of the West (西王母) got it and sits on Shaoguang (少廣) — none knows her beginning, none knows her end; Pengzu got it and lived from the age of Shun down to the five hegemons; Fu Yue (傅說) got it and became minister to Wuding (武丁), presiding over all beneath the sky — riding the Eastern Winch, mounted on Winnowing-basket and Tail, he took his place among the ranked stars.

## Section 8

**ZH:** 南伯子葵問乎女偊曰：「子之年長矣，而色若孺子，何也？」曰：「吾聞道矣。」南伯子葵曰：「道可得學邪？」曰：「惡！惡可！子非其人也。夫卜梁倚有聖人之才，而無聖人之道，我有聖人之道，而無聖人之才，吾欲以教之，庶幾其果為聖人乎！不然，以聖人之道告聖人之才，亦易矣。吾猶守而告之，參日而後能外天下；已外天下矣，吾又守之，七日而後能外物；已外物矣，吾又守之，九日而後能外生；已外生矣，而後能朝徹；朝徹，而後能見獨；見獨，而後能無古今；無古今，而後能入於不死不生。殺生者不死，生生者不生。其為物，無不將也，無不迎也；無不毀也，無不成也。其名為攖寧。攖寧也者，攖而後成者也。」南伯子葵曰：「子獨惡乎聞之？」曰：「聞諸副墨之子，副墨之子聞諸洛誦之孫，洛誦之孫聞之瞻明，瞻明聞之聶許，聶許聞之需役，需役聞之於謳，於謳聞之玄冥，玄冥聞之參寥，參寥聞之疑始。」

Hence it is said: the small man of Heaven is the gentleman of men; the gentleman of men is the small man of Heaven."

## Section 11

**ZH:** 顏回問仲尼曰：「孟孫才，其母死，哭泣無涕，中心不戚，居喪不哀。無是三者，以善處喪蓋魯國。固有無其實而得其名者乎？回壹怪之。」仲尼曰：「夫孟孫氏盡之矣，進於知矣。唯簡之而不得，夫已有所簡矣。孟孫氏不知所以生，不知所以死，不知就先，不知就後，若化為物，以待其所不知之化已乎！且方將化，惡知不化哉？方將不化，惡知已化哉？吾特與汝其夢未始覺者邪！且彼有駭形而無損心，有旦宅而無情死。孟孫氏特覺，人哭亦哭，是自其所以乃。且也，相與吾之耳矣，庸詎知吾所謂吾之乎？且汝夢為鳥而厲乎天，夢為魚而沒於淵，不識今之言者，其覺者乎，夢者乎？造適不及笑，獻笑不及排，安排而去化，乃入於寥天一。」
**PY:** yán huí wèn zhòng ní yuē：「 mèng sūn cái， qí mǔ sǐ， kū qì wú tì， zhōng xīn bù qī， jū sàng bù āi。 wú shì sān zhě， yǐ shàn chù sàng gài lǔ guó。 gù yǒu wú qí shí ér dé qí míng zhě hū？ huí yī guài zhī。」 zhòng ní yuē：「 fū mèng sūn shì jǐn zhī yǐ， jìn yú zhī yǐ。 wéi jiǎn zhī ér bù dé， fū yǐ yǒu suǒ jiǎn yǐ。 mèng sūn shì bù zhī suǒ yǐ shēng， bù zhī suǒ yǐ sǐ， bù zhī jiù xiān， bù zhī jiù hòu， ruò huà wèi wù， yǐ dài qí suǒ bù zhī zhī huà yǐ hū！ qiě fāng jiāng huà， è zhī bù huà zāi？ fāng jiāng bù huà， è zhī yǐ huà zāi？ wú tè yǔ rǔ qí mèng wèi shǐ jué zhě xié！ qiě bǐ yǒu hài xíng ér wú sǔn xīn， yǒu dàn zhái ér wú qíng sǐ。 mèng sūn shì tè jué， rén kū yì kū， shì zì qí suǒ yǐ nǎi。 qiě yě， xiāng yǔ wú zhī ěr yǐ， yōng jù zhī wú suǒ wèi wú zhī hū？ qiě rǔ mèng wèi niǎo ér lì hū tiān， mèng wèi yú ér méi yú yuān， bù shí jīn zhī yán zhě， qí jué zhě hū， mèng zhě hū？ zào shì bù jí xiào， xiàn xiào bù jí pái， ān pái ér qù huà， nǎi rù yú liáo tiān yī。」

**EN:** Yan Hui asked Confucius: "When the mother of Meng Suncai (孟孫才) died, he wept without tears, his inner heart did not grieve, and he dwelt in mourning without sorrow. Without these three, he is famed throughout Lu as one who handles mourning well. Is there really such a thing as getting the name without the substance? I am utterly puzzled by him." Confucius said: "Mengsun has gone all the way; he has gone beyond knowledge. He would simplify it further but cannot — and yet he has already simplified somewhat. Mengsun does not know why there is life; he does not know why there is death; he does not know to go to what is earlier; he does not know to go to what is later. As though transformed into a thing, he simply waits for the transformation he does not know — that is all. And when he is about to transform, how does he know he has not transformed already? When he is about not to transform, how does he know he has transformed? Perhaps you and I are those who dream and have never yet awoken! Moreover, for him there is a startling of the form but no injury to the mind; a dawn-change of lodging, but no death of the genuine. Mengsun alone is awake — when others weep, he weeps too; that is simply how he is with them. Besides, we keep saying 'I' of one another — but how do I know what I mean by 'I'? You dream you are a bird and soar to the sky; you dream you are a fish and sink into the deep. We cannot tell whether the one speaking now is awake or dreaming. One arrives at ease before one has time to smile; one offers a smile before one has time to compose it. Rest in the composition and let the transformation go — then one enters into the oneness of the empty sky." [uncertain: 造適不及笑，獻笑不及排，安排而去化 — an obscure closing; the rendering follows Guo Xiang's line, but the syntax admits several readings.]

## Section 12

**ZH:** 意而子見許由，許由曰：「堯何以資汝？」意而子曰：「堯謂我：『汝必躬服仁義，而明言是非。』」許由曰：「而奚為來軹？夫堯既已黥汝以仁義，而劓汝以是非矣，汝將何以遊夫遙蕩、恣睢、轉徙之途乎？」意而子曰：「雖然，吾願遊於其藩。」許由曰：「不然。夫盲者無以與乎眉目顏色之好，瞽者無以與乎青黃黼黻之觀。」意而子曰：「夫無莊之失其美，據梁之失其力，黃帝之亡其知，皆在鑪捶之間耳。庸詎知夫造物者之不息我黥而補我劓，使我乘成以隨先生邪？」許由曰：「噫！未可知也。我為汝言其大略。吾師乎！吾師乎！齏萬物而不為義，澤及萬世而不為仁，長於上古而不為老，覆載天地、刻彫眾形而不為巧。此所遊已。」
**PY:** yì ér zi jiàn xǔ yóu， xǔ yóu yuē：「 yáo hé yǐ zī rǔ？」 yì ér zi yuē：「 yáo wèi wǒ：『 rǔ bì gōng fú rén yì， ér míng yán shì fēi。』」 xǔ yóu yuē：「 ér xī wèi lái zhǐ？ fū yáo jì yǐ qíng rǔ yǐ rén yì， ér yì rǔ yǐ shì fēi yǐ， rǔ jiāng hé yǐ yóu fū yáo dàng、 zì suī、 zhuǎn xǐ zhī tú hū？」 yì ér zi yuē：「 suī rán， wú yuàn yóu yú qí fān。」 xǔ yóu yuē：「 bù rán。 fū máng zhě wú yǐ yǔ hū méi mù yán sè zhī hǎo， gǔ zhě wú yǐ yǔ hū qīng huáng fǔ fú zhī guān。」 yì ér zi yuē：「 fū wú zhuāng zhī shī qí měi， jù liáng zhī shī qí lì， huáng dì zhī wáng qí zhī， jiē zài lú chuí zhī jiān ěr。 yōng jù zhī fū zào wù zhě zhī bù xī wǒ qíng ér bǔ wǒ yì， shǐ wǒ chéng chéng yǐ suí xiān shēng xié？」 xǔ yóu yuē：「 yī！ wèi kě zhī yě。 wǒ wèi rǔ yán qí dà lüè。 wú shī hū！ wú shī hū！ jī wàn wù ér bù wèi yì， zé jí wàn shì ér bù wèi rén， zhǎng yú shàng gǔ ér bù wèi lǎo， fù zài tiān dì、 kè diāo zhòng xíng ér bù wèi qiǎo。 cǐ suǒ yóu yǐ。」

**EN:** Yi'erzi (意而子) went to see Xu You. Xu You said: "What has Yao given you?" Yi'erzi said: "Yao told me: 'You must yourself practice humaneness and rightness, and speak clearly of right and wrong.'" Xu You said: "Then why have you come here? Yao has already tattooed your face with humaneness and rightness, and cut off your nose with right and wrong. How will you roam that road — far-wandering, free-indulging, ever-shifting?" Yi'erzi said: "Even so, I would like to roam at least within its fence." Xu You said: "Impossible. The blind have no share in the beauty of brows and eyes and faces; the sightless have no share in the sight of blue and yellow and embroidered robes." Yi'erzi said: "Wuzhuang (無莊) lost her beauty; Juliang (據梁) lost his strength; the Yellow Emperor lost his knowledge — all within the work of the forge and hammer. How do you know that the Creator will not heal my tattoo and mend my nose, so that I may ride on wholeness and follow you, sir?" Xu You said: "Ah — that cannot be known. But I will tell you the gist of it. My master! My master! He grinds the ten thousand things to dust, yet is not righteous; his blessing reaches down ten thousand ages, yet he is not humane; he is older than high antiquity, yet not old; he covers and bears up heaven and earth, carves and fashions all the forms, yet is not skillful. In this I roam."

## Section 13

**ZH:** 顏回曰：「回益矣。」仲尼曰：「何謂也？」曰：「回忘仁義矣。」曰：「可矣，猶未也。」他日復見，曰：「回益矣。」曰：「何謂也？」曰：「回忘禮樂矣。」曰：「可矣，猶未也。」他日復見，曰：「回益矣。」曰：「何謂也？」曰：「回坐忘矣。」仲尼蹴然曰：「何謂坐忘？」顏回曰：「墮肢體，黜聰明，離形去知，同於大通，此謂坐忘。」仲尼曰：「同則無好也，化則無常也。而果其賢乎！丘也請從而後也。」
**PY:** yán huí yuē：「 huí yì yǐ。」 zhòng ní yuē：「 hé wèi yě？」 yuē：「 huí wàng rén yì yǐ。」 yuē：「 kě yǐ， yóu wèi yě。」 tā rì fù jiàn， yuē：「 huí yì yǐ。」 yuē：「 hé wèi yě？」 yuē：「 huí wàng lǐ lè yǐ。」 yuē：「 kě yǐ， yóu wèi yě。」 tā rì fù jiàn， yuē：「 huí yì yǐ。」 yuē：「 hé wèi yě？」 yuē：「 huí zuò wàng yǐ。」 zhòng ní cù rán yuē：「 hé wèi zuò wàng？」 yán huí yuē：「 duò zhī tǐ， chù cōng míng， lí xíng qù zhī， tóng yú dà tōng， cǐ wèi zuò wàng。」 zhòng ní yuē：「 tóng zé wú hǎo yě， huà zé wú cháng yě。 ér guǒ qí xián hū！ qiū yě qǐng cóng ér hòu yě。」

**EN:** Yan Hui said: "I have made progress." Confucius said: "What do you mean?" He said: "I have forgotten humaneness and rightness." Confucius said: "Good — but not yet." Another day he came again and said: "I have made progress." Confucius said: "What do you mean?" He said: "I have forgotten ritual and music." Confucius said: "Good — but not yet." Another day he came again and said: "I have made progress." Confucius said: "What do you mean?" He said: "I sit and forget." Confucius started and said: "What do you mean by sitting and forgetting?" Yan Hui said: "I let the limbs and body fall away, dismiss keen hearing and sight, leave the form and shed knowledge, and become one with the Great Pervasion. This is called sitting and forgetting." Confucius said: "Being one with it, you have no favorites; being transformed, you have nothing constant. You are truly worthy! I beg to follow along behind you."

## Section 14

**ZH:** 子輿與子桑友，而霖雨十日。子輿曰：「子桑殆病矣！」裹飯而往食之。至子桑之門，則若歌若哭，鼓琴曰：「父邪母邪！天乎人乎！」有不任其聲，而趨舉其詩焉。子輿入，曰：「子之歌詩，何故若是？」曰：「吾思乎使我至此極者而弗得也。父母豈欲吾貧哉？天無私覆，地無私載，天地豈私貧我哉？求其為之者而不得也。然而至此極者，命也夫！」
**PY:** zi yú yǔ zi sāng yǒu， ér lín yǔ shí rì。 zi yú yuē：「 zi sāng dài bìng yǐ！」 guǒ fàn ér wǎng shí zhī。 zhì zi sāng zhī mén， zé ruò gē ruò kū， gǔ qín yuē：「 fù xié mǔ xié！ tiān hū rén hū！」 yǒu bù rèn qí shēng， ér qū jǔ qí shī yān。 zi yú rù， yuē：「 zi zhī gē shī， hé gù ruò shì？」 yuē：「 wú sī hū shǐ wǒ zhì cǐ jí zhě ér fú dé yě。 fù mǔ qǐ yù wú pín zāi？ tiān wú sī fù， dì wú sī zài， tiān dì qǐ sī pín wǒ zāi？ qiú qí wèi zhī zhě ér bù dé yě。 rán ér zhì cǐ jí zhě， mìng yě fū！」

**EN:** Ziyu and Zisang (子桑) were friends. Once the rains fell in floods for ten days. Ziyu said: "Zisang must be in distress!" He wrapped up some rice and went to feed him. Coming to Zisang's gate, he heard something like singing, like weeping, to the sound of a zither: "Father! Mother! Heaven! Man!" The voice could no longer sustain itself, yet he hurried on through his song. Ziyu went in and said: "Why do you sing your song like that?" He said: "I have been pondering what it was that brought me to this extremity, and I cannot find it. Would my parents have wished me poor? Heaven covers all without partiality; earth bears all without partiality — would heaven and earth single me out for poverty? I seek its author and cannot find it. That I have nonetheless come to this extremity — it is fate!"
