# Delight in the Sword-fight (說劍)
Source: Zhuangzi _ Miscellaneous Chapters _ Delight in the Sword-fight - Chinese Text Project.html
Status: complete (7 sections)

## Section 1

**ZH:** 昔趙文王喜劍，劍士夾門而客三千餘人，日夜相擊於前，死傷者歲百餘人，好之不厭。如是三年，國衰，諸侯謀之。太子悝患之，募左右曰：「孰能說王之意止劍士者，賜之千金。」左右曰：「莊子當能。」
**PY:** xī zhào wén wáng xǐ jiàn， jiàn shì jiā mén ér kè sān qiān yú rén， rì yè xiāng jī yú qián， sǐ shāng zhě suì bǎi yú rén， hǎo zhī bù yàn。 rú shì sān nián， guó shuāi， zhū hóu móu zhī。 tài zi kuī huàn zhī， mù zuǒ yòu yuē：「 shú néng shuō wáng zhī yì zhǐ jiàn shì zhě， cì zhī qiān jīn。」 zuǒ yòu yuē：「 zhuāng zi dāng néng。」

**EN:** Of old, King Wen of Zhao delighted in swords. Swordsmen packed his gates — over three thousand guests — fighting one another day and night before him; the dead and wounded numbered over a hundred a year, and his delight never waned. After three years of this, the state declined, and the lords plotted against it. Crown Prince Kui (悝), distressed, offered a bounty to his court: "Whoever can persuade the king to stop the swordsmen shall have a thousand pieces of gold." His courtiers said: "Zhuangzi should be able to."

## Section 2

**ZH:** 太子乃使人以千金奉莊子。莊子弗受，與使者俱往見太子曰：「太子何以教周，賜周千金？」太子曰：「聞夫子明聖，謹奉千金以幣從者。夫子弗受，悝尚何敢言！」莊子曰：「聞太子所欲用周者，欲絕王之喜好也。使臣上說大王而逆王意，下不當太子，則身刑而死，周尚安所事金乎！使臣上說大王，下當太子，趙國何求而不得也？」太子曰：「然。吾王所見，唯劍士也。」莊子曰：「諾。周善為劍。」太子曰：「然吾王所見劍士，皆蓬頭、突鬢、垂冠，曼胡之纓，短後之衣，嗔目而語難，王乃說之。今夫子必儒服而見王，事必大逆。」莊子曰：「請治劍服。」治劍服三日，乃見太子。太子乃與見王，王脫白刃待之。
**PY:** tài zi nǎi shǐ rén yǐ qiān jīn fèng zhuāng zi。 zhuāng zi fú shòu， yǔ shǐ zhě jù wǎng jiàn tài zi yuē：「 tài zi hé yǐ jiào zhōu， cì zhōu qiān jīn？」 tài zi yuē：「 wén fū zǐ míng shèng， jǐn fèng qiān jīn yǐ bì cóng zhě。 fū zǐ fú shòu， kuī shàng hé gǎn yán！」 zhuāng zi yuē：「 wén tài zi suǒ yù yòng zhōu zhě， yù jué wáng zhī xǐ hào yě。 shǐ chén shàng shuō dà wáng ér nì wáng yì， xià bù dāng tài zi， zé shēn xíng ér sǐ， zhōu shàng ān suǒ shì jīn hū！ shǐ chén shàng shuō dà wáng， xià dāng tài zi， zhào guó hé qiú ér bù dé yě？」 tài zi yuē：「 rán。 wú wáng suǒ jiàn， wéi jiàn shì yě。」 zhuāng zi yuē：「 nuò。 zhōu shàn wèi jiàn。」 tài zi yuē：「 rán wú wáng suǒ jiàn jiàn shì， jiē péng tóu、 tū bìn、 chuí guān， màn hú zhī yīng， duǎn hòu zhī yī， chēn mù ér yǔ nán， wáng nǎi shuō zhī。 jīn fū zǐ bì rú fú ér jiàn wáng， shì bì dà nì。」 zhuāng zi yuē：「 qǐng zhì jiàn fú。」 zhì jiàn fú sān rì， nǎi jiàn tài zi。 tài zi nǎi yǔ jiàn wáng， wáng tuō bái rèn dài zhī。

**EN:** The crown prince sent men with a thousand gold to Zhuangzi. Zhuangzi did not accept it, but went with the messenger to see the crown prince: "What instruction does the crown prince have for Zhou, granting Zhou a thousand gold?" The crown prince said: "Hearing that you, Master, are bright and sage, I respectfully offer a thousand gold as gifts for your followers. If you will not accept it, how dare Kui say more!" Zhuangzi said: "I hear that what the crown prince wishes to use Zhou for is to put an end to the king's fondness. Suppose your servant persuades the great king above and crosses his will, while below I fail the crown prince — then my body is punished and dies; what use would Zhou have for gold! Suppose your servant persuades the great king above and answers the crown prince below — what, within the state of Zhao, could be asked and not gotten?" The crown prince said: "Just so. What my king sees is only swordsmen." Zhuangzi said: "Good. Zhou is good at swords." The crown prince said: "But the swordsmen my king sees all have tangled hair, bristling temples, drooping caps, coarse hanging tassels, jackets cut short behind, and glare and growl as they speak — that is what pleases the king. Now you, Master, are sure to see the king in the Ru robe — the affair is bound to go badly." Zhuangzi said: "Let a swordsman's garb be made." When the swordsman's garb was finished after three days, he saw the crown prince. The crown prince went with him to see the king, who bared a white blade and awaited him.

## Section 3

**ZH:** 莊子入殿門不趨，見王不拜。王曰：「子欲何以教寡人，使太子先？」曰：「臣聞大王喜劍，故以劍見王。」王曰：「子之劍何能禁制？」曰：「臣之劍，十步一人，千里不留行。」王大悅之，曰：「天下無敵矣。」莊子曰：「夫為劍者，示之以虛，開之以利，後之以發，先之以至。願得試之。」王曰：「夫子休就舍，待命令設戲請夫子。」王乃校劍士七日，死傷者六十餘人，得五六人，使奉劍於殿下，乃召莊子。王曰：「今日試使士敦劍。」莊子曰：「望之久矣。」王曰：「夫子所御杖，長短何如？」曰：「臣之所奉皆可。然臣有三劍，唯王所用，請先言而後試。」
**PY:** zhuāng zi rù diàn mén bù qū， jiàn wáng bù bài。 wáng yuē：「 zi yù hé yǐ jiào guǎ rén， shǐ tài zi xiān？」 yuē：「 chén wén dà wáng xǐ jiàn， gù yǐ jiàn jiàn wáng。」 wáng yuē：「 zi zhī jiàn hé néng jìn zhì？」 yuē：「 chén zhī jiàn， shí bù yī rén， qiān lǐ bù liú xíng。」 wáng dà yuè zhī， yuē：「 tiān xià wú dí yǐ。」 zhuāng zi yuē：「 fū wèi jiàn zhě， shì zhī yǐ xū， kāi zhī yǐ lì， hòu zhī yǐ fā， xiān zhī yǐ zhì。 yuàn dé shì zhī。」 wáng yuē：「 fū zǐ xiū jiù shě， dài mìng lìng shè xì qǐng fū zǐ。」 wáng nǎi xiào jiàn shì qī rì， sǐ shāng zhě liù shí yú rén， dé wǔ liù rén， shǐ fèng jiàn yú diàn xià， nǎi zhào zhuāng zi。 wáng yuē：「 jīn rì shì shǐ shì dūn jiàn。」 zhuāng zi yuē：「 wàng zhī jiǔ yǐ。」 wáng yuē：「 fū zǐ suǒ yù zhàng， zhǎng duǎn hé rú？」 yuē：「 chén zhī suǒ fèng jiē kě。 rán chén yǒu sān jiàn， wéi wáng suǒ yòng， qǐng xiān yán ér hòu shì。」

**EN:** Zhuangzi entered the palace gate without hurrying, and seeing the king did not bow. The king said: "What do you wish to teach me with, that you had the crown prince go first?" He said: "Your servant heard that the great king delights in swords, so I came to see the king with a sword." The king said: "What can your sword withstand?" He said: "Your servant's sword: one man every ten paces; a thousand li without pause." The king was greatly delighted: "Without rival under heaven!" Zhuangzi said: "In the use of the sword: show him the empty, open with the profitable, issue last, and arrive first. I beg leave to try it." The king said: "Rest at your lodging, Master; when I have ordered the bout arranged, I will invite you." The king then tried his swordsmen for seven days; the dead and wounded were over sixty; from the survivors he got five or six men, whom he had bear their swords below the hall — then he summoned Zhuangzi. The king said: "Today, try letting the men press their swords." Zhuangzi said: "I have long looked for it." The king said: "What length of blade does the master bear?" He said: "Whatever your servant bears will do. But your servant has three swords; let the king use them as he wishes. Let me speak first, then try."

## Section 4

**ZH:** 王曰：「願聞三劍。」曰：「有天子劍，有諸侯劍，有庶人劍。」王曰：「天子之劍何如？」曰：「天子之劍，以燕谿、石城為鋒，齊、岱為鍔，晉、魏為脊，周、宋為鐔，韓、魏為夾，包以四夷，裹以四時，繞以渤海，帶以常山，制以五行，論以刑德，開以陰陽，持以春夏，行以秋冬。此劍直之無前，舉之無上，案之無下，運之無旁，上決浮雲，下絕地紀。此劍一用，匡諸侯，天下服矣。此天子之劍也。」
**PY:** wáng yuē：「 yuàn wén sān jiàn。」 yuē：「 yǒu tiān zi jiàn， yǒu zhū hóu jiàn， yǒu shù rén jiàn。」 wáng yuē：「 tiān zi zhī jiàn hé rú？」 yuē：「 tiān zi zhī jiàn， yǐ yàn xī、 shí chéng wèi fēng， qí、 dài wèi è， jìn、 wèi wèi jí， zhōu、 sòng wèi xín， hán、 wèi wèi jiā， bāo yǐ sì yí， guǒ yǐ sì shí， rào yǐ bó hǎi， dài yǐ cháng shān， zhì yǐ wǔ xíng， lùn yǐ xíng dé， kāi yǐ yīn yáng， chí yǐ chūn xià， xíng yǐ qiū dōng。 cǐ jiàn zhí zhī wú qián， jǔ zhī wú shàng， àn zhī wú xià， yùn zhī wú páng， shàng jué fú yún， xià jué dì jì。 cǐ jiàn yī yòng， kuāng zhū hóu， tiān xià fú yǐ。 cǐ tiān zi zhī jiàn yě。」

**EN:** The king said: "I beg to hear of the three swords." He said: "There is the Son of Heaven's sword; there is the lord's sword; there is the common man's sword." The king said: "What is the Son of Heaven's sword like?" He said: "The Son of Heaven's sword takes Yanxi and Shicheng for its point; Qi and Mount Tai for its edge; Jin and Wei for its spine; Zhou and Song for its guard; Han and Wei for its hilt. It is wrapped in the four wilds, bundled in the four seasons, girded by the Bo Sea, belted by Mount Chang, ruled by the five phases, measured by punishment and virtue, opened by yin and yang, held by spring and summer, and wielded by autumn and winter. This sword, thrust straight, meets nothing before it; raised, meets nothing above it; pressed down, meets nothing below it; swung, meets nothing beside it; above, it cleaves the floating clouds; below, it severs the earth's warp. Once this sword is used, it corrects the lords, and all the world submits. This is the Son of Heaven's sword."

## Section 5

**ZH:** 文王芒然自失，曰：「諸侯之劍何如？」曰：「諸侯之劍，以知勇士為鋒，以清廉士為鍔，以賢良士為脊，以忠聖士為鐔，以豪桀士為夾。此劍值之亦無前，舉之亦無上，案之亦無下，運之亦無旁，上法圓天以順三光，下法方地以順四時，中和民意以安四鄉。此劍一用，如雷霆之震也，四封之內，無不賓服而聽從君命者矣。此諸侯之劍也。」
**PY:** wén wáng máng rán zì shī， yuē：「 zhū hóu zhī jiàn hé rú？」 yuē：「 zhū hóu zhī jiàn， yǐ zhī yǒng shì wèi fēng， yǐ qīng lián shì wèi è， yǐ xián liáng shì wèi jí， yǐ zhōng shèng shì wèi xín， yǐ háo jié shì wèi jiā。 cǐ jiàn zhí zhī yì wú qián， jǔ zhī yì wú shàng， àn zhī yì wú xià， yùn zhī yì wú páng， shàng fǎ yuán tiān yǐ shùn sān guāng， xià fǎ fāng dì yǐ shùn sì shí， zhōng hé mín yì yǐ ān sì xiāng。 cǐ jiàn yī yòng， rú léi tíng zhī zhèn yě， sì fēng zhī nèi， wú bù bīn fú ér tīng cóng jūn mìng zhě yǐ。 cǐ zhū hóu zhī jiàn yě。」

**EN:** King Wen, dazed and lost, said: "What is the lord's sword like?" He said: "The lord's sword takes knowing and brave scholars for its point; pure and honest scholars for its edge; worthy and good scholars for its spine; loyal and sage scholars for its guard; bold and outstanding scholars for its hilt. This sword, thrust straight, likewise meets nothing before it; raised, nothing above it; pressed down, nothing below it; swung, nothing beside it. Above it models the round heaven to follow the three lights; below it models the square earth to follow the four seasons; in between it harmonizes the people's will to quiet the four quarters. Once this sword is used, like the shock of thunder, within the four borders there is none who does not submit as guest and obey the lord's command. This is the lord's sword."

## Section 6

**ZH:** 王曰：「庶人之劍何如？」曰：「庶人之劍，蓬頭、突鬢、垂冠，曼胡之纓，短後之衣，瞋目而語難，相擊於前，上斬頸領，下決肝肺。此庶人之劍，無異於鬥雞，一旦命已絕矣，無所用於國事。今大王有天子之位，而好庶人之劍，臣竊為大王薄之。」
**PY:** wáng yuē：「 shù rén zhī jiàn hé rú？」 yuē：「 shù rén zhī jiàn， péng tóu、 tū bìn、 chuí guān， màn hú zhī yīng， duǎn hòu zhī yī， chēn mù ér yǔ nán， xiāng jī yú qián， shàng zhǎn jǐng lǐng， xià jué gān fèi。 cǐ shù rén zhī jiàn， wú yì yú dòu jī， yí dàn mìng yǐ jué yǐ， wú suǒ yòng yú guó shì。 jīn dà wáng yǒu tiān zi zhī wèi， ér hǎo shù rén zhī jiàn， chén qiè wèi dà wáng báo zhī。」

**EN:** The king said: "What is the common man's sword like?" He said: "The common man's sword — tangled hair, bristling temples, drooping caps, coarse hanging tassels, jackets cut short behind, glaring and growling as they strike one another in front, above cutting necks, below cleaving livers and lungs. This common man's sword is no different from fighting cocks: one morning its life is cut off, and it is of no use in the affairs of state. Now the great king holds the position of a Son of Heaven, yet delights in the common man's sword — in private I count it light of the great king."

## Section 7

**ZH:** 王乃牽而上殿，宰人上食，王三環之。莊子曰：「大王安坐定氣，劍事已畢奏矣。」於是文王不出宮三月，劍士皆服斃其處也。
**PY:** wáng nǎi qiān ér shàng diàn， zǎi rén shàng shí， wáng sān huán zhī。 zhuāng zi yuē：「 dà wáng ān zuò dìng qì， jiàn shì yǐ bì zòu yǐ。」 yú shì wén wáng bù chū gōng sān yuè， jiàn shì jiē fú bì qí chù yě。

**EN:** The king then took his hand and led him up the hall. When the cook brought the food, the king circled it thrice. Zhuangzi said: "Sit at ease, great king, and settle your breath — the matter of swords has been fully presented." Thereupon King Wen did not leave the palace for three months, and the swordsmen all lay down and died in their places.
