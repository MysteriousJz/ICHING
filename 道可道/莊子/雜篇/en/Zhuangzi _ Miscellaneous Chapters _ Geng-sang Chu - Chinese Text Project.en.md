# Geng-sang Chu (庚桑楚)
Source: Zhuangzi _ Miscellaneous Chapters _ Geng-sang Chu - Chinese Text Project.html
Status: complete (26 sections)

## Section 1

**ZH:** 老聃之役，有庚桑楚者，偏得老聃之道，以北居畏壘之山。其臣之畫然知者去之，其妾之挈然仁者遠之，擁腫之與居，鞅掌之為使。居三年，畏壘大壤。畏壘之民相與言曰：「庚桑子之始來，吾洒然異之。今吾日計之而不足，歲計之而有餘。庶幾其聖人乎！子胡不相與尸而祝之，社而稷之乎？」
**PY:** lǎo dān zhī yì， yǒu gēng sāng chǔ zhě， piān dé lǎo dān zhī dào， yǐ běi jū wèi lěi zhī shān。 qí chén zhī huà rán zhī zhě qù zhī， qí qiè zhī qiè rán rén zhě yuǎn zhī， yōng zhǒng zhī yǔ jū， yāng zhǎng zhī wèi shǐ。 jū sān nián， wèi lěi dà rǎng。 wèi lěi zhī mín xiāng yǔ yán yuē：「 gēng sāng zi zhī shǐ lái， wú sǎ rán yì zhī。 jīn wú rì jì zhī ér bù zú， suì jì zhī ér yǒu yú。 shù jǐ qí shèng rén hū！ zi hú bù xiāng yǔ shī ér zhù zhī， shè ér jì zhī hū？」

**EN:** Among the followers of Lao Dan was one Geng-sang Chu (庚桑楚), who had gotten a partial share of Lao Dan's Way and went north to dwell on the Mount of the Fearsome Rampart. Among his servants, those of marked cleverness he dismissed; among his concubines, those of conspicuous humaneness he kept at a distance; the lumpish he dwelt with, the rough-hewn he employed. After three years the Fearsome Rampart had a great harvest. The people of the Rampart said among themselves: "When Master Geng-sang first came, we looked askance and thought him strange. Now we reckon by the day and find no gain, but reckon by the year and there is more than enough. Perhaps he is a sage after all! Why should we not join together and enshrine him as our dead lord, and worship him as our god of soil and grain?"

## Section 2

**ZH:** 庚桑子聞之，南面而不釋然。弟子異之。庚桑子曰：「弟子何異於予？夫春氣發而百草生，正得秋而萬寶成。夫春與秋，豈無得而然哉？天道已行矣。吾聞至人尸居環堵之室，而百姓猖狂不知所如往。今以畏壘之細民而竊竊欲俎豆予于賢人之閒，我其杓之人邪？吾是以不釋於老聃之言。」
**PY:** gēng sāng zi wén zhī， nán miàn ér bù shì rán。 dì zǐ yì zhī。 gēng sāng zi yuē：「 dì zǐ hé yì yú yǔ？ fū chūn qì fā ér bǎi cǎo shēng， zhèng dé qiū ér wàn bǎo chéng。 fū chūn yǔ qiū， qǐ wú dé ér rán zāi？ tiān dào yǐ xíng yǐ。 wú wén zhì rén shī jū huán dǔ zhī shì， ér bǎi xìng chāng kuáng bù zhī suǒ rú wǎng。 jīn yǐ wèi lěi zhī xì mín ér qiè qiè yù zǔ dòu yǔ yú xián rén zhī xián， wǒ qí biāo zhī rén xié？ wú shì yǐ bù shì yú lǎo dān zhī yán。」

**EN:** Master Geng-sang heard of it and, sitting facing south, was not at ease. His disciples wondered at him. Master Geng-sang said: "Why do you wonder at me? When the breath of spring issues forth, the hundred grasses grow; when autumn comes in its due, the ten thousand treasures ripen. Do spring and autumn do this without attaining something? The Way of Heaven has run its course. I have heard that the utmost man dwells like a corpse within his four-square chamber, and the hundred clans, wild and free, do not know where to go. Now these petty people of the Rampart, whispering, wish to set me on sacrificial stands among the worthies — am I then one to be put on display! That is why I am not at ease with the words of Lao Dan."

## Section 3

**ZH:** 弟子曰：「不然。夫尋常之溝，巨魚無所還其體，而鯢鰌為之制；步仞之丘陵，巨獸無所隱其軀，而㜸狐為之祥。且夫尊賢授能，先善與利，自古堯、舜以然，而況畏壘之民乎？夫子亦聽矣！」
**PY:** dì zǐ yuē：「 bù rán。 fū xún cháng zhī gōu， jù yú wú suǒ hái qí tǐ， ér ní qiū wèi zhī zhì； bù rèn zhī qiū líng， jù shòu wú suǒ yǐn qí qū， ér niè hú wèi zhī xiáng。 qiě fū zūn xián shòu néng， xiān shàn yǔ lì， zì gǔ yáo、 shùn yǐ rán， ér kuàng wèi lěi zhī mín hū？ fū zǐ yì tīng yǐ！」

**EN:** His disciples said: "Not so. In a ditch of eight or sixteen feet, a great fish has no room to turn its body, and the minnow and loach lord it there. On a mound of a pace or a fathom, a great beast has no place to hide its frame, and the scheming fox makes it auspicious. Besides, to honor the worthy and empower the able, to put goodness and profit first — since ancient times Yao and Shun did thus; how much more should the people of the Rampart! Master, do let them have their way!"

## Section 4

**ZH:** 庚桑子曰：「小子來！夫函車之獸，介而離山，則不免於罔罟之患；吞舟之魚，碭而失水，則蟻能苦之。故鳥獸不厭高，魚鱉不厭深。夫全其形生之人，藏其身也，不厭深眇而已矣。且夫二子者，又何足以稱揚哉！是其於辯也，將妄鑿垣牆而殖蓬蒿也。簡髮而櫛，數米而炊，竊竊乎又何足以濟世哉！舉賢則民相軋，任知則民相盜。之數物者，不足以厚民。民之於利甚勤，子有殺父，臣有殺君，正晝為盜，日中穴杯。吾語女：大亂之本，必生於堯、舜之間，其末存乎千世之後。千世之後，其必有人與人相食者也。」
**PY:** gēng sāng zi yuē：「 xiǎo zi lái！ fū hán chē zhī shòu， jiè ér lí shān， zé bù miǎn yú wǎng gǔ zhī huàn； tūn zhōu zhī yú， dàng ér shī shuǐ， zé yǐ néng kǔ zhī。 gù niǎo shòu bù yàn gāo， yú biē bù yàn shēn。 fū quán qí xíng shēng zhī rén， cáng qí shēn yě， bù yàn shēn miǎo ér yǐ yǐ。 qiě fū èr zi zhě， yòu hé zú yǐ chēng yáng zāi！ shì qí yú biàn yě， jiāng wàng záo yuán qiáng ér zhí péng hāo yě。 jiǎn fà ér zhì， shù mǐ ér chuī， qiè qiè hū yòu hé zú yǐ jì shì zāi！ jǔ xián zé mín xiāng yà， rèn zhī zé mín xiāng dào。 zhī shù wù zhě， bù zú yǐ hòu mín。 mín zhī yú lì shèn qín， zi yǒu shā fù， chén yǒu shā jūn， zhèng zhòu wèi dào， rì zhōng xué bēi。 wú yǔ nǚ： dà luàn zhī běn， bì shēng yú yáo、 shùn zhī jiān， qí mò cún hū qiān shì zhī hòu。 qiān shì zhī hòu， qí bì yǒu rén yǔ rén xiāng shí zhě yě。」

**EN:** Master Geng-sang said: "Come here, young men! A beast big enough to hold a cart — let it stray alone from the mountains, and it cannot escape the calamity of nets. A fish big enough to swallow a boat — let it be tossed on the shore and lose the water, and even ants can torment it. So birds and beasts never tire of the heights; fish and turtles never tire of the depths. As for one who keeps his form and life whole, he hides his person away and never tires of the deep and remote. Besides, those two men — what are they fit to be praised for! In their discriminations, they would recklessly knock down the walls and plant weeds. To sort the hairs before combing, to count the grains before cooking — fussing and fretting, what good could that do the age! Raise the worthy and the people will crush one another; employ the clever and the people will rob one another. These several things are not enough to enrich the people. When the people strain after profit, sons kill their fathers; ministers kill their lords; in broad daylight they rob; at midday they bore through walls. I tell you: the root of great disorder is sure to have been born between Yao and Shun; its branches will survive a thousand generations hence. A thousand generations hence, there will surely be men eating men."

## Section 5

**ZH:** 南榮趎蹴然正坐曰：「若趎之年者已長矣，將惡乎託業以及此言邪？」庚桑子曰：「全汝形，抱汝生，無使汝思慮營營。若此三年，則可以及此言矣。」南榮趎曰：「目之與形，吾不知其異也，而盲者不能自見；耳之與形，吾不知其異也，而聾者不能自聞；心之與形，吾不知其異也，而狂者不能自得。形之與形亦辟矣，而物或閒之邪，欲相求而不能相得？今謂趎曰：『全汝形，抱汝生，勿使汝思慮營營。』趎勉聞道達耳矣。」庚桑子曰：「辭盡矣。曰：『奔蜂不能化藿蠋，越雞不能伏鵠卵，魯雞固能矣。』雞之與雞，其德非不同也，有能有不能者，其才固有巨小也。今吾才小，不足以化子，子胡不南見老子？」
**PY:** nán róng chú cù rán zhèng zuò yuē：「 ruò chú zhī nián zhě yǐ zhǎng yǐ， jiāng è hū tuō yè yǐ jí cǐ yán xié？」 gēng sāng zi yuē：「 quán rǔ xíng， bào rǔ shēng， wú shǐ rǔ sī lǜ yíng yíng。 ruò cǐ sān nián， zé kě yǐ jí cǐ yán yǐ。」 nán róng chú yuē：「 mù zhī yǔ xíng， wú bù zhī qí yì yě， ér máng zhě bù néng zì jiàn； ěr zhī yǔ xíng， wú bù zhī qí yì yě， ér lóng zhě bù néng zì wén； xīn zhī yǔ xíng， wú bù zhī qí yì yě， ér kuáng zhě bù néng zì dé。 xíng zhī yǔ xíng yì pì yǐ， ér wù huò xián zhī xié， yù xiāng qiú ér bù néng xiāng de？ jīn wèi chú yuē：『 quán rǔ xíng， bào rǔ shēng， wù shǐ rǔ sī lǜ yíng yíng。』 chú miǎn wén dào dá ěr yǐ。」 gēng sāng zi yuē：「 cí jǐn yǐ。 yuē：『 bēn fēng bù néng huà huò zhú， yuè jī bù néng fú hú luǎn， lǔ jī gù néng yǐ。』 jī zhī yǔ jī， qí dé fēi bù tóng yě， yǒu néng yǒu bù néng zhě， qí cái gù yǒu jù xiǎo yě。 jīn wú cái xiǎo， bù zú yǐ huà zi， zi hú bù nán jiàn lǎo zi？」

**EN:** Nanrong Chu (南榮趎) started upright in his seat: "One of my years is already old — by what work can I ever arrive at such words as these?" Master Geng-sang said: "Keep your form whole; embrace your life; do not let your thoughts and cares go scurrying. Thus for three years, and you can arrive at these words." Nanrong Chu said: "The eye and its form — I know no difference in them, yet the blind cannot see themselves. The ear and its form — I know no difference, yet the deaf cannot hear themselves. The heart and its form — I know no difference, yet the mad cannot keep hold of themselves. Form and form are indeed open to one another — but do things come between them, that seeking one another we cannot reach one another? Now you tell me: 'Keep your form whole; embrace your life; do not let your thoughts and cares go scurrying.' With effort I hear the Way — it reaches only my ears." Master Geng-sang said: "The words are at an end. It is said: 'The dashing bee cannot transform the cabbage-worm; the Yue fowl cannot hatch a swan's egg — but the Lu fowl surely can.' Between fowl and fowl the virtue is not different; that there is can and cannot lies in their capacities being by nature great or small. My capacity is small — not enough to transform you. Why do you not go south to see Laozi?"

## Section 6

**ZH:** 南榮趎贏糧，七日七夜至老子之所。老子曰：「子自楚之所來乎？」南榮趎曰：「唯。」老子曰：「子何與人偕來之眾也？」南榮趎懼然顧其後。老子曰：「子不知吾所謂乎？」南榮趎俯而慚，仰而歎曰：「今者吾忘吾答，因失吾問。」老子曰：「何謂也？」南榮趎曰；「不知乎？人謂我朱愚。知乎？反愁我軀。不仁則害人，仁則反愁我身；不義則傷彼，義則反愁我已。我安逃此而可？此三言者，趎之所患也，願因楚而問之。」老子曰：「向吾見若眉睫之間，吾因以得汝矣，今汝又言而信之。若規規然若喪父母，揭竿而求諸海也。女亡人哉！惘惘乎汝欲反汝情性而無由入，可憐哉！」
**PY:** nán róng chú yíng liáng， qī rì qī yè zhì lǎo zi zhī suǒ。 lǎo zi yuē：「 zi zì chǔ zhī suǒ lái hū？」 nán róng chú yuē：「 wéi。」 lǎo zi yuē：「 zi hé yǔ rén xié lái zhī zhòng yě？」 nán róng chú jù rán gù qí hòu。 lǎo zi yuē：「 zi bù zhī wú suǒ wèi hū？」 nán róng chú fǔ ér cán， yǎng ér tàn yuē：「 jīn zhě wú wàng wú dá， yīn shī wú wèn。」 lǎo zi yuē：「 hé wèi yě？」 nán róng chú yuē；「 bù zhī hū？ rén wèi wǒ zhū yú。 zhī hū？ fǎn chóu wǒ qū。 bù rén zé hài rén， rén zé fǎn chóu wǒ shēn； bù yì zé shāng bǐ， yì zé fǎn chóu wǒ yǐ。 wǒ ān táo cǐ ér kě？ cǐ sān yán zhě， chú zhī suǒ huàn yě， yuàn yīn chǔ ér wèn zhī。」 lǎo zi yuē：「 xiàng wú jiàn ruò méi jié zhī jiān， wú yīn yǐ dé rǔ yǐ， jīn rǔ yòu yán ér xìn zhī。 ruò guī guī rán ruò sàng fù mǔ， jiē gān ér qiú zhū hǎi yě。 nǚ wáng rén zāi！ wǎng wǎng hū rǔ yù fǎn rǔ qíng xìng ér wú yóu rù， kě lián zāi！」

**EN:** Nanrong Chu, carrying his provisions on his back, traveled seven days and seven nights to Laozi's place. Laozi said: "Have you come from Chu's place?" Nanrong Chu said: "Yes." Laozi said: "Why have you come with such a crowd of companions?" Nanrong Chu looked back behind him in alarm. Laozi said: "Do you not know what I mean?" Nanrong Chu hung his head in shame, then looked up and sighed: "Just now I forgot my answer — and thereby lost my question." Laozi said: "What do you mean?" Nanrong Chu said: "Not to know? Men call me a dull fool. To know? It brings worry back upon my body. Not to be humane? It injures others. To be humane? It brings worry back upon my person. Not to be righteous? It harms the other. To be righteous? It brings worry back upon myself. Where can I flee from this? These three sayings are what troubles me — I beg, through Chu, to ask about them." Laozi said: "A moment ago, seeing between your brows and lashes, I had you figured out; now your words confirm it. You are like one dazed, who has lost his father and mother, and lifts a pole to go sounding for them in the sea. You are a lost man! Dazed and dim, you wish to return to your genuine nature and find no way in — pitiful indeed!"

## Section 7

**ZH:** 南榮趎請入就舍，召其所好，去其所惡，十日自愁，復見老子。老子曰：「汝自洒濯，熟哉鬱鬱乎！然而其中津津乎猶有惡也。夫外韄者不可繁而捉，將內揵；內韄者不可繆而捉，將外揵。外、內韄者，道德不能持，而況放道而行者乎！」
**PY:** nán róng chú qǐng rù jiù shě， zhào qí suǒ hǎo， qù qí suǒ è， shí rì zì chóu， fù jiàn lǎo zi。 lǎo zi yuē：「 rǔ zì sǎ zhuó， shú zāi yù yù hū！ rán ér qí zhōng jīn jīn hū yóu yǒu è yě。 fū wài hù zhě bù kě fán ér zhuō， jiāng nèi qián； nèi hù zhě bù kě móu ér zhuō， jiāng wài qián。 wài、 nèi hù zhě， dào dé bù néng chí， ér kuàng fàng dào ér xíng zhě hū！」

**EN:** Nanrong Chu begged leave to enter the lodging. He summoned what he loved, dismissed what he hated; after ten days of self-affliction he saw Laozi again. Laozi said: "You have washed and scoured yourself — how thick the dregs still are! And yet within you, seeping through, there is still something foul. One bound without cannot be seized in his multiplicity — bar him within. One bound within cannot be seized in his intricacy — bar him without. One bound without and within — even the Way and its virtue cannot hold him; how much less one who takes the Way and walks in it!"

## Section 8

**ZH:** 南榮趎曰：「里人有病，里人問之，病者能言其病，然其病病者猶未病也。若趎之聞大道，譬猶飲藥以加病也，趎願聞衛生之經而已矣。」老子曰：「衛生之經，能抱一乎？能勿失乎？能無卜筮而知吉凶乎？能止乎？能已乎？能舍諸人而求諸己乎？能翛然乎？能侗然乎？能兒子乎？兒子終日嗥而嗌不嗄，和之至也；終日握而手不掜，共其德也；終日視而目不瞚，偏不在外也。行不知所之，居不知所為，與物委蛇，而同其波。是衛生之經已。」
**PY:** nán róng chú yuē：「 lǐ rén yǒu bìng， lǐ rén wèn zhī， bìng zhě néng yán qí bìng， rán qí bìng bìng zhě yóu wèi bìng yě。 ruò chú zhī wén dà dào， pì yóu yǐn yào yǐ jiā bìng yě， chú yuàn wén wèi shēng zhī jīng ér yǐ yǐ。」 lǎo zi yuē：「 wèi shēng zhī jīng， néng bào yī hū？ néng wù shī hū？ néng wú bǔ shì ér zhī jí xiōng hū？ néng zhǐ hū？ néng yǐ hū？ néng shě zhū rén ér qiú zhū jǐ hū？ néng xiāo rán hū？ néng dòng rán hū？ néng ér zi hū？ ér zi zhōng rì háo ér ài bù á， hé zhī zhì yě； zhōng rì wò ér shǒu bù yì， gòng qí dé yě； zhōng rì shì ér mù bù shùn， piān bù zài wài yě。 xíng bù zhī suǒ zhī， jū bù zhī suǒ wèi， yǔ wù wēi yí， ér tóng qí bō。 shì wèi shēng zhī jīng yǐ。」

**EN:** Nanrong Chu said: "When a villager falls ill and the villagers ask after him, if the sick man can speak of his sickness, then though he is sick of his sickness he is not yet sick. When I hear the great Way, it is like drinking medicine to aggravate the illness. I beg only to hear the canon of guarding life." Laozi said: "The canon of guarding life: Can you embrace the One? Can you not lose it? Can you know fortune and misfortune without tortoise and stalks? Can you stop? Can you cease? Can you let others go and seek it in yourself? Can you be free and easy? Can you be blank and simple? Can you be a little child? A little child howls all day, yet its throat never grows hoarse — that is the utmost of harmony. It grips all day, yet its hand never cramps — that is the sharing of its virtue. It stares all day, yet its eyes never flicker — its leaning is not outward. It moves and knows not where it goes; it dwells and knows not what it does; it winds and bends with things and shares their wave. This is the canon of guarding life."

## Section 9

**ZH:** 南榮趎曰：「然則是至人之德已乎？」曰：「非也。是乃所謂冰解凍釋者能乎？夫至人者，相與交食乎地而交樂乎天，不以人物利害相攖，不相與為怪，不相與為謀，不相與為事，翛然而往，侗然而來。是謂衛生之經已。」曰：「然則是至乎？」曰：「未也。吾固告汝曰：『能兒子乎？』兒子動不知所為，行不知所之，身若槁木之枝而心若死灰。若是者，禍亦不至，福亦不來。禍福無有，惡有人災也？」
**PY:** nán róng chú yuē：「 rán zé shì zhì rén zhī dé yǐ hū？」 yuē：「 fēi yě。 shì nǎi suǒ wèi bīng jiě dòng shì zhě néng hū？ fū zhì rén zhě， xiāng yǔ jiāo shí hū dì ér jiāo lè hū tiān， bù yǐ rén wù lì hài xiāng yīng， bù xiāng yǔ wèi guài， bù xiāng yǔ wèi móu， bù xiāng yǔ wèi shì， xiāo rán ér wǎng， dòng rán ér lái。 shì wèi wèi shēng zhī jīng yǐ。」 yuē：「 rán zé shì zhì hū？」 yuē：「 wèi yě。 wú gù gào rǔ yuē：『 néng ér zi hū？』 ér zi dòng bù zhī suǒ wèi， xíng bù zhī suǒ zhī， shēn ruò gǎo mù zhī zhī ér xīn ruò sǐ huī。 ruò shì zhě， huò yì bù zhì， fú yì bù lái。 huò fú wú yǒu， è yǒu rén zāi yě？」

**EN:** Nanrong Chu said: "Is this, then, the virtue of the utmost man?" He said: "No. This is what is called the thawing of the ice, the release of the frost — that is what it can do. The utmost man shares his food from the earth and shares his joy from Heaven; he is not jostled by benefit and harm from men and things; he does not join in oddities, does not join in schemes, does not join in undertakings; free and easy he goes, blank and simple he comes. This is called the canon of guarding life." He said: "Then is that the utmost?" He said: "Not yet. I told you before: 'Can you be a little child?' A little child moves and knows not what it does; walks and knows not where it goes; its body is like the branch of a withered tree and its heart like dead ashes. Being such, misfortune does not arrive and fortune does not come. With no misfortune and no fortune — how could there be any calamity from men?"

## Section 10

**ZH:** 宇泰定者，發乎天光。發乎天光者，人見其人。人有修者，乃今有恆；有恆者，人舍之，天助之。人之所舍，謂之天民；天之所助，謂之天子。學者，學其所不能學也；行者，行其所不能行也；辯者，辯其所不能辯也。知止乎其所不能知，至矣。若有不即是者，天鈞敗之。
**PY:** yǔ tài dìng zhě， fā hū tiān guāng。 fā hū tiān guāng zhě， rén jiàn qí rén。 rén yǒu xiū zhě， nǎi jīn yǒu héng； yǒu héng zhě， rén shě zhī， tiān zhù zhī。 rén zhī suǒ shě， wèi zhī tiān mín； tiān zhī suǒ zhù， wèi zhī tiān zi。 xué zhě， xué qí suǒ bù néng xué yě； xíng zhě， xíng qí suǒ bù néng xíng yě； biàn zhě， biàn qí suǒ bù néng biàn yě。 zhī zhǐ hū qí suǒ bù néng zhī， zhì yǐ。 ruò yǒu bù jí shì zhě， tiān jūn bài zhī。

**EN:** When the inner chamber is composed and settled, it sends forth Heaven's light. When it sends forth Heaven's light, men see the man. When a man has cultivation, only then is there constancy; when there is constancy, men lodge with him, Heaven helps him. He whom men lodge with is called Heaven's citizen; he whom Heaven helps is called Heaven's son. To learn is to learn what cannot be learned; to practice is to practice what cannot be practiced; to dispute is to dispute what cannot be disputed. Knowledge that stops at what it cannot know — that is the utmost. If there is any that is not thus, the potter's wheel of Heaven breaks it.

## Section 11

**ZH:** 備物以將形，藏不虞以生心，敬中以達彼，若是而萬惡至者，皆天也，而非人也，不足以滑成，不可內於靈臺。靈臺者有持，而不知其所持，而不可持者也。不見其誠己而發，每發而不當，業入而不舍，每更為失。為不善乎顯明之中者，人得而誅之；為不善乎幽閒之中者，鬼得而誅之。明乎人、明乎鬼者，然後能獨行。
**PY:** bèi wù yǐ jiāng xíng， cáng bù yú yǐ shēng xīn， jìng zhōng yǐ dá bǐ， ruò shì ér wàn è zhì zhě， jiē tiān yě， ér fēi rén yě， bù zú yǐ huá chéng， bù kě nèi yú líng tái。 líng tái zhě yǒu chí， ér bù zhī qí suǒ chí， ér bù kě chí zhě yě。 bù jiàn qí chéng jǐ ér fā， měi fā ér bù dāng， yè rù ér bù shě， měi gèng wèi shī。 wèi bù shàn hū xiǎn míng zhī zhōng zhě， rén dé ér zhū zhī； wèi bù shàn hū yōu xián zhī zhōng zhě， guǐ dé ér zhū zhī。 míng hū rén、 míng hū guǐ zhě， rán hòu néng dú xíng。

**EN:** Furnish things to sustain the form; store up no forethought to give life to the heart; reverence the center to reach out to others — if one is thus and ten thousand evils arrive, they are all from Heaven and not from man; they are not enough to disturb one's completion; they must not enter the spirit-tower. The spirit-tower has something it holds, yet does not know what it holds, and cannot hold it. To issue forth without seeing one's own sincerity — every issue misses the mark; to let affairs enter and not let them go — every change is another loss. One who does ill in the midst of the open light — men get to punish him. One who does ill in the midst of the dark and hidden — ghosts get to punish him. Clear about men, clear about ghosts — only then can one walk alone.

## Section 12

**ZH:** 券內者行乎無名，券外者志乎期費。行乎無名者，唯庸有光；志乎期費者，唯賈人也，人見其跂，猶之魁然。與物窮者，物入焉；與物且者，其身之不能容，焉能容人！不能容人者無親，無親者盡人。兵莫憯於志，鏌鋣為下；寇莫大於陰陽，無所逃於天地之間。非陰陽賊之，心則使之也。
**PY:** quàn nèi zhě xíng hū wú míng， quàn wài zhě zhì hū qī fèi。 xíng hū wú míng zhě， wéi yōng yǒu guāng； zhì hū qī fèi zhě， wéi jiǎ rén yě， rén jiàn qí qí， yóu zhī kuí rán。 yǔ wù qióng zhě， wù rù yān； yǔ wù qiě zhě， qí shēn zhī bù néng róng， yān néng róng rén！ bù néng róng rén zhě wú qīn， wú qīn zhě jǐn rén。 bīng mò cǎn yú zhì， mò yé wèi xià； kòu mò dà yú yīn yáng， wú suǒ táo yú tiān dì zhī jiān。 fēi yīn yáng zéi zhī， xīn zé shǐ zhī yě。

**EN:** One whose tally is within walks in the nameless; one whose tally is without sets his will on expectations and expenses. One who walks in the nameless has the light of the ordinary alone; one whose will is on expectations and expenses is a mere merchant — men see him on tiptoe and think him grand. One who goes to the end with things — things enter into him. One who marks off things as other — if his own person cannot be contained, how can he contain others! One who cannot contain others has no kin; one who has no kin is cut off from men. No weapon is crueler than the will — beside it the Moye sword is low. No enemy is greater than yin and yang — there is no escaping them between heaven and earth. It is not yin and yang that prey on us: the heart makes them do it.

## Section 13

**ZH:** 道通，其分也，其成也毀也。所惡乎分者，其分也以備；所以惡乎備者，其有以備。故出而不反，見其鬼；出而得，是謂得死。滅而有實，鬼之一也。以有形者象無形者而定矣。
**PY:** dào tōng， qí fēn yě， qí chéng yě huǐ yě。 suǒ è hū fēn zhě， qí fēn yě yǐ bèi； suǒ yǐ è hū bèi zhě， qí yǒu yǐ bèi。 gù chū ér bù fǎn， jiàn qí guǐ； chū ér dé， shì wèi dé sǐ。 miè ér yǒu shí， guǐ zhī yī yě。 yǐ yǒu xíng zhě xiàng wú xíng zhě ér dìng yǐ。

**EN:** The Way passes through all. What is divided is completed; what is completed is destroyed. What is hateful about division is that the division is made for completeness; and what is hateful about completeness is that there is a something to complete. Therefore to go out and not return is to see one's ghost; to go out and get something is called getting death. To be extinguished yet have substance — this is to be one of the ghosts. Let what has form be the image of what has no form — and there is settlement.

## Section 14

**ZH:** 出無本，入無竅。有實而無乎處，有長而無乎本剽，有所出而無竅者有實。有實而無乎處者，宇也；有長而無本剽者，宙也。有乎生，有乎死，有乎出，有乎入，入出而無見其形，是謂天門。天門者，無有也，萬物出乎無有。有不能以有為有，必出乎無有，而無有一無有。聖人藏乎是。
**PY:** chū wú běn， rù wú qiào。 yǒu shí ér wú hū chù， yǒu zhǎng ér wú hū běn piāo， yǒu suǒ chū ér wú qiào zhě yǒu shí。 yǒu shí ér wú hū chù zhě， yǔ yě； yǒu zhǎng ér wú běn piāo zhě， zhòu yě。 yǒu hū shēng， yǒu hū sǐ， yǒu hū chū， yǒu hū rù， rù chū ér wú jiàn qí xíng， shì wèi tiān mén。 tiān mén zhě， wú yǒu yě， wàn wù chū hū wú yǒu。 yǒu bù néng yǐ yǒu wèi yǒu， bì chū hū wú yǒu， ér wú yǒu yī wú yǒu。 shèng rén cáng hū shì。

**EN:** It comes forth without root; it enters without aperture. There is substance yet nowhere it is placed; there is duration yet no beginning or tip; there is that which comes forth without aperture — and there is substance. Having substance yet nowhere it is placed — this is space. Having duration yet no beginning or tip — this is time. There is birth, there is death, there is coming forth, there is entering; entering and coming forth, and its form is never seen — this is called the gate of Heaven. The gate of Heaven is nothing; the ten thousand things come forth from nothing. Being cannot make being from being; it must come forth from nothing — and nothing is one with nothing. The sage hides in this.

## Section 15

**ZH:** 古之人，其知有所至矣。惡乎至？有以為未始有物者，至矣盡矣，弗可以加矣。其次以為有物矣，將以生為喪也，以死為反也，是以分已。其次曰始無有，既而有生，生俄而死；以無有為首，以生為體，以死為尻。孰知有無死生之一守者，吾與之為友。是三者雖異，公族也，昭、景也，著戴也，甲氏也，著封也。非一也。
**PY:** gǔ zhī rén， qí zhī yǒu suǒ zhì yǐ。 è hū zhì？ yǒu yǐ wèi wèi shǐ yǒu wù zhě， zhì yǐ jǐn yǐ， fú kě yǐ jiā yǐ。 qí cì yǐ wèi yǒu wù yǐ， jiāng yǐ shēng wèi sàng yě， yǐ sǐ wèi fǎn yě， shì yǐ fēn yǐ。 qí cì yuē shǐ wú yǒu， jì ér yǒu shēng， shēng é ér sǐ； yǐ wú yǒu wèi shǒu， yǐ shēng wèi tǐ， yǐ sǐ wèi kāo。 shú zhī yǒu wú sǐ shēng zhī yī shǒu zhě， wú yǔ zhī wèi yǒu。 shì sān zhě suī yì， gōng zú yě， zhāo、 jǐng yě， zhù dài yě， jiǎ shì yě， zhù fēng yě。 fēi yī yě。

**EN:** The knowledge of the men of old had somewhere it arrived. Where did it arrive? Some held that there had never begun to be things — that is the utmost, the exhaustion; nothing can be added. The next held that there are things, taking life as a loss and death as a return — and here division began. The next said: in the beginning there was nothing; then there was life; life for a moment, and death — taking nothing as the head, life as the body, death as the tail. Who knows that being and nothing, death and life, are one keeping — with him I would be friends. These three, though different, are of one clan — like the Zhaos and the Jings, distinguished by what they wear; like the house of Jia, distinguished by its fief — not one. [uncertain: the closing genealogy simile (昭、景…甲氏) is obscure; rendered following the reading that the three views are branches of one stock, distinguished only by outward marks.]

## Section 16

**ZH:** 有生，黬也，披然曰移是。嘗言移是，非所言也。雖然，不可知者也。臘者之有膍胲，可散而不可散也；觀室者周於寢廟，又適其偃焉，為是舉移是。
**PY:** yǒu shēng， yán yě， pī rán yuē yí shì。 cháng yán yí shì， fēi suǒ yán yě。 suī rán， bù kě zhī zhě yě。 là zhě zhī yǒu pí hǎi， kě sàn ér bù kě sàn yě； guān shì zhě zhōu yú qǐn miào， yòu shì qí yǎn yān， wèi shì jǔ yí shì。

**EN:** There is life — a black blur; spreading apart, it is called "the shifting this." Let me try to speak of the shifting this — though it is not something words can speak. Even so, it cannot be known. At the winter sacrifice there are the tripe and hooves — they can be scattered, yet cannot be scattered; one viewing a house goes all round the chamber and shrine, and then goes on to the privy — for such occasions we raise the shifting this. [uncertain: §16 and §17 form a notoriously gnomic doublet on 移是 ("the shifting this"); the tripe-and-privy examples illustrate that what is proper to mention depends on occasion — no single account covers the whole.]

## Section 17

**ZH:** 請嘗言移是。是以生為本，以知為師，因以乘是非；果有名實，因以己為質；使人以己為節，因以死償節。若然者，以用為知，以不用為愚，以徹為名，以窮為辱。移是，今之人也，是蜩與學鳩同於同也。
**PY:** qǐng cháng yán yí shì。 shì yǐ shēng wèi běn， yǐ zhī wèi shī， yīn yǐ chéng shì fēi； guǒ yǒu míng shí， yīn yǐ jǐ wèi zhì； shǐ rén yǐ jǐ wèi jié， yīn yǐ sǐ cháng jié。 ruò rán zhě， yǐ yòng wèi zhī， yǐ bù yòng wèi yú， yǐ chè wèi míng， yǐ qióng wèi rǔ。 yí shì， jīn zhī rén yě， shì tiáo yǔ xué jiū tóng yú tóng yě。

**EN:** Let me try to speak of the shifting this. It takes life as the root and knowledge as the teacher, and thereby rides on right and wrong. It turns out there are names and substances, so it takes itself as their measure; it makes others take itself as their rule, and even pays out death to requite the rule. Men like this count employment knowledge, unemployment folly; success a name, failure a disgrace. The shifting this — it is the men of today; it is the cicada and the little dove, alike in their alikeness.

## Section 18

**ZH:** 蹍市人之足，則辭以放驁，兄則以嫗，大親則已矣。故曰：至禮有不人，至義不物，至知不謀，至仁無親，至信辟金。
**PY:** niǎn shì rén zhī zú， zé cí yǐ fàng ào， xiōng zé yǐ yù， dà qīn zé yǐ yǐ。 gù yuē： zhì lǐ yǒu bù rén， zhì yì bù wù， zhì zhī bù móu， zhì rén wú qīn， zhì xìn pì jīn。

**EN:** Tread on a stranger's foot in the market, and one apologizes for one's carelessness; tread on an elder brother's foot, and a soothing word suffices; tread on a parent's foot, and nothing at all is said. Hence it is said: utmost ritual has no others; utmost rightness has no things; utmost knowledge does not scheme; utmost humaneness has no kin; utmost trust dismisses gold.

## Section 19

**ZH:** 徹志之勃，解心之繆，去德之累，達道之塞。富、貴、顯、嚴、名、利六者，勃志也；容、動、色、理、氣、意六者，繆心也；惡、欲、喜、怒、哀、樂六者，累德也；去、就、取、與、知、能六者，塞道也。此四六者不盪胸中則正，正則靜，靜則明，明則虛，虛則無為而無不為也。
**PY:** chè zhì zhī bó， jiě xīn zhī móu， qù dé zhī lèi， dá dào zhī sāi。 fù、 guì、 xiǎn、 yán、 míng、 lì liù zhě， bó zhì yě； róng、 dòng、 sè、 lǐ、 qì、 yì liù zhě， móu xīn yě； è、 yù、 xǐ、 nù、 āi、 lè liù zhě， lèi dé yě； qù、 jiù、 qǔ、 yǔ、 zhī、 néng liù zhě， sāi dào yě。 cǐ sì liù zhě bù dàng xiōng zhōng zé zhèng， zhèng zé jìng， jìng zé míng， míng zé xū， xū zé wú wèi ér wú bù wèi yě。

**EN:** Penetrate the turbidity of the will; untie the knots of the heart; remove the burdens of virtue; open the blockages of the Way. Riches, rank, eminence, awe, name, profit — these six are the turbidity of the will. Bearing, movement, expression, manner, breath, intent — these six are the knots of the heart. Hate, desire, joy, anger, sorrow, delight — these six are the burdens of virtue. Departing, approaching, taking, giving, knowing, ability — these six are the blockages of the Way. When these four sixes do not tumble the breast, one is upright; upright, still; still, bright; bright, empty; empty — and without action, while nothing is not done.

## Section 20

**ZH:** 道者，德之欽也；生者，德之光也；性者，生之質也。性之動謂之為，為之偽謂之失。
**PY:** dào zhě， dé zhī qīn yě； shēng zhě， dé zhī guāng yě； xìng zhě， shēng zhī zhì yě。 xìng zhī dòng wèi zhī wèi， wèi zhī wěi wèi zhī shī。

**EN:** The Way is virtue's reverence; life is virtue's light; nature is life's substance. Nature's motion is called action; action's falsity is called loss.

## Section 21

**ZH:** 知者，接也；知者，謨也；知者之所不知，猶睨也。
**PY:** zhī zhě， jiē yě； zhī zhě， mó yě； zhī zhě zhī suǒ bù zhī， yóu nì yě。

**EN:** Knowledge is contact; knowledge is plotting; what the knower does not know is as a sidelong glance.

## Section 22

**ZH:** 動以不得已之謂德，動無非我之謂治，名相反而實相順也。
**PY:** dòng yǐ bù dé yǐ zhī wèi dé， dòng wú fēi wǒ zhī wèi zhì， míng xiāng fǎn ér shí xiāng shùn yě。

**EN:** To move only from the unavoidable is called virtue; to move with nothing not one's own is called order. The names are opposite; the substance accords.

## Section 23

**ZH:** 羿工乎中微而拙於使人無己譽，聖人工乎天而拙乎人。夫工乎天而俍乎人者，唯全人能之。
**PY:** yì gōng hū zhōng wēi ér zhuō yú shǐ rén wú jǐ yù， shèng rén gōng hū tiān ér zhuō hū rén。 fū gōng hū tiān ér liáng hū rén zhě， wéi quán rén néng zhī。

**EN:** Yi was skilled at hitting the mark but clumsy at making men not praise him; the sage is skilled at Heaven but clumsy at men. To be skilled at Heaven and good at men — only the whole man can do that.

## Section 24

**ZH:** 唯蟲能蟲，唯蟲能天。全人惡天，惡人之天，而況吾天乎人乎！
**PY:** wéi chóng néng chóng， wéi chóng néng tiān。 quán rén è tiān， è rén zhī tiān， ér kuàng wú tiān hū rén hū！

**EN:** Only the insect can be the insect; only the insect can be at Heaven. The whole man hates Heaven — hates the Heaven in man — how much more the self's Heaven set against men!

## Section 25

**ZH:** 一雀適羿，羿必得之，威也；以天下為之籠，則雀無所逃。是故湯以胞人籠伊尹，秦穆公以五羊之皮籠百里奚。是故非以其所好籠之而可得者，無有也。
**PY:** yī què shì yì， yì bì děi zhī， wēi yě； yǐ tiān xià wèi zhī lóng， zé què wú suǒ táo。 shì gù tāng yǐ bāo rén lóng yī yǐn， qín mù gōng yǐ wǔ yáng zhī pí lóng bǎi lǐ xī。 shì gù fēi yǐ qí suǒ hǎo lóng zhī ér kě dé zhě， wú yǒu yě。

**EN:** Let one sparrow come within Yi's reach, and Yi is sure to get it — such is his power. But make the whole world his cage, and the sparrow has nowhere to escape. Thus Tang caged Yi Yin with a cook's office, and Duke Mu of Qin caged Baili Xi with five ram skins. So: that a man can be caught except by caging him with what he loves — that has never happened.

## Section 26

**ZH:** 介者拸畫，外非譽也；胥靡登高而不懼，遺死生也。夫復謵不餽而忘人，忘人，因以為天人矣。故敬之而不喜，侮之而不怒者，唯同乎天和者為然。出怒不怒，則怒出於不怒矣；出為無為，則為出於無為矣。欲靜則平氣，欲神則順心，有為也。欲當則緣於不得已，不得已之類，聖人之道。
**PY:** jiè zhě yí huà， wài fēi yù yě； xū mí dēng gāo ér bù jù， yí sǐ shēng yě。 fū fù xí bù kuì ér wàng rén， wàng rén， yīn yǐ wèi tiān rén yǐ。 gù jìng zhī ér bù xǐ， wǔ zhī ér bù nù zhě， wéi tóng hū tiān hé zhě wèi rán。 chū nù bù nù， zé nù chū yú bù nù yǐ； chū wèi wú wèi， zé wèi chū yú wú wèi yǐ。 yù jìng zé píng qì， yù shén zé shùn xīn， yǒu wèi yě。 yù dāng zé yuán yú bù dé yǐ， bù dé yǐ zhī lèi， shèng rén zhī dào。

**EN:** The one-footed man casts aside adornment, for he stands beyond blame and praise; the convict climbs the heights without fear, for he has forgotten death and life. When one answers without requiting and forgets men — forgetting men, he thereby becomes a man of Heaven. Therefore: honored, he is not pleased; insulted, he is not angered — only one who is one with Heaven's harmony can be so. To issue anger without anger — then anger comes forth from non-anger; to issue action without action — then action comes forth from non-action. Wishing for stillness, level the breath; wishing for spirit, follow the heart — these are still actions. Wishing to be fitting, follow along with the unavoidable; the kind of the unavoidable — that is the Way of the sage.
