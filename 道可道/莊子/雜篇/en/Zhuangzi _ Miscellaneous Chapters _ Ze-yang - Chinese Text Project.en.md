# Ze-yang (則陽)
Source: Zhuangzi _ Miscellaneous Chapters _ Ze-yang - Chinese Text Project.html
Status: complete (22 sections)

## Section 1

**ZH:** 則陽游於楚，夷節言之於王，王未之見，夷節歸。彭陽見王果曰：「夫子何不譚我於王？」王果曰：「我不若公閱休。」彭陽曰：「公閱休奚為者邪？」曰：「冬則擉鱉於江，夏則休乎山樊。有過而問者，曰：『此予宅也。』夫夷節已不能，而況我乎！吾又不若夷節。夫夷節之為人也，無德而有知，不自許，以之神其交，固顛冥乎富貴之地，非相助以德，相助消也。夫凍者假衣於春，暍者反冬乎冷風。夫楚王之為人也，形尊而嚴，其於罪也，無赦如虎，非夫佞人、正德，其孰能橈焉！故聖人，其窮也使家人忘其貧，其達也使王公忘其爵祿而化卑。其於物也，與之為娛矣；其於人也，樂物之通而保己焉。故或不言而飲人以和，與人並立而使人化。父子之宜，彼其乎歸居，而一閒其所施。其於人心者，若是其遠也。故曰待公閱休。」
**PY:** zé yáng yóu yú chǔ， yí jié yán zhī yú wáng， wáng wèi zhī jiàn， yí jié guī。 péng yáng jiàn wáng guǒ yuē：「 fū zǐ hé bù tán wǒ yú wáng？」 wáng guǒ yuē：「 wǒ bù ruò gōng yuè xiū。」 péng yáng yuē：「 gōng yuè xiū xī wèi zhě xié？」 yuē：「 dōng zé chuò biē yú jiāng， xià zé xiū hū shān fán。 yǒu guò ér wèn zhě， yuē：『 cǐ yǔ zhái yě。』 fū yí jié yǐ bù néng， ér kuàng wǒ hū！ wú yòu bù ruò yí jié。 fū yí jié zhī wèi rén yě， wú dé ér yǒu zhī， bù zì xǔ， yǐ zhī shén qí jiāo， gù diān míng hū fù guì zhī dì， fēi xiāng zhù yǐ dé， xiāng zhù xiāo yě。 fū dòng zhě jiǎ yī yú chūn， yē zhě fǎn dōng hū lěng fēng。 fū chǔ wáng zhī wèi rén yě， xíng zūn ér yán， qí yú zuì yě， wú shè rú hǔ， fēi fū nìng rén、 zhèng dé， qí shú néng ráo yān！ gù shèng rén， qí qióng yě shǐ jiā rén wàng qí pín， qí dá yě shǐ wáng gōng wàng qí jué lù ér huà bēi。 qí yú wù yě， yǔ zhī wèi yú yǐ； qí yú rén yě， lè wù zhī tōng ér bǎo jǐ yān。 gù huò bù yán ér yǐn rén yǐ hé， yǔ rén bìng lì ér shǐ rén huà。 fù zǐ zhī yí， bǐ qí hū guī jū， ér yī xián qí suǒ shī。 qí yú rén xīn zhě， ruò shì qí yuǎn yě。 gù yuē dài gōng yuè xiū。」

**EN:** Ze-yang, roaming in Chu, was spoken of to the king by Yi Jie (夷節); the king did not receive him, and Yi Jie went home. Pengyang (彭陽) saw Wang Guo (王果) and said: "Why do you not speak of me to the king, sir?" Wang Guo said: "I am no match for Gong Yuexiu (公閱休)." Pengyang said: "What manner of man is Gong Yuexiu?" He said: "In winter he spears turtles in the river; in summer he rests in the mountain hedge. When a passerby asks him, he says: 'This is my house.' Yi Jie could not manage it — how much less I! I am no match even for Yi Jie. Yi Jie as a man has no virtue but has knowledge; he does not give himself license, yet with that he spiritizes his dealings — he is surely sunk and darkened in the land of riches and rank. Such men do not help one another with virtue; they help one another to diminish. The freezing borrow clothes in spring; the heat-struck go back to winter for the cold wind. Now the king of Chu as a man is august and stern in bearing; toward offenses he is unforgiving as a tiger. Unless it be a flatterer or one of upright virtue, who could bend him! So the sage: in straits he makes his household forget their poverty; in success he makes kings and dukes forget their titles and stipends and transforms them into humility. Toward things, he makes them his delight; toward men, he delights in the communion of things and preserves himself. So without speaking he gives men harmony to drink; standing beside men he makes them transform. The fitness of father and son: they go home and dwell, while he is wholly at ease in what he gives out. Toward the hearts of men he is thus far away. Therefore I said: wait upon Gong Yuexiu."

## Section 2

**ZH:** 聖人達綢繆，周盡一體矣，而不知其然，性也。復命搖作而以天為師，人則從而命之也。憂乎知而所行恆無幾時，其有止也若之何？
**PY:** shèng rén dá chóu móu， zhōu jǐn yī tǐ yǐ， ér bù zhī qí rán， xìng yě。 fù mìng yáo zuò ér yǐ tiān wèi shī， rén zé cóng ér mìng zhī yě。 yōu hū zhī ér suǒ xíng héng wú jǐ shí， qí yǒu zhǐ yě ruò zhī hé？

**EN:** The sage penetrates the entanglements; he is roundly one with things, yet does not know how it is so — that is nature. Returning to destiny, stirring and acting, he takes Heaven as teacher; men follow and give it its name. But to fret over knowledge while one's practice has never lasted out a season — how will it ever come to a stop!

## Section 3

**ZH:** 生而美者，人與之鑑，不告則不知其美於人也。若知之，若不知之，若聞之，若不聞之，其可喜也終無已，人之好之亦無已，性也。聖人之愛人也，人與之名，不告則不知其愛人也。若知之，若不知之，若聞之，若不聞之，其愛人也終無已，人之安之亦無已，性也。
**PY:** shēng ér měi zhě， rén yǔ zhī jiàn， bù gào zé bù zhī qí měi yú rén yě。 ruò zhī zhī， ruò bù zhī zhī， ruò wén zhī， ruò bù wén zhī， qí kě xǐ yě zhōng wú yǐ， rén zhī hǎo zhī yì wú yǐ， xìng yě。 shèng rén zhī ài rén yě， rén yǔ zhī míng， bù gào zé bù zhī qí ài rén yě。 ruò zhī zhī， ruò bù zhī zhī， ruò wén zhī， ruò bù wén zhī， qí ài rén yě zhōng wú yǐ， rén zhī ān zhī yì wú yǐ， xìng yě。

**EN:** One born beautiful is given a mirror by others; if they do not tell her, she does not know she is more beautiful than others. As though knowing, as though not knowing; as though hearing, as though not hearing — her loveliness never ends, and others' delight in her never ends. That is nature. The sage's love for men: others give it its name; if they do not tell him, he does not know he loves men. As though knowing, as though not knowing; as though hearing, as though not hearing — his loving men never ends, and men's resting in it never ends. That is nature.

## Section 4

**ZH:** 舊國舊都，望之暢然；雖使丘陵草木之緡，入之者十九，猶之暢然。況見見聞聞者也？以十仞之臺縣眾閒者也！
**PY:** jiù guó jiù dōu， wàng zhī chàng rán； suī shǐ qiū líng cǎo mù zhī mín， rù zhī zhě shí jiǔ， yóu zhī chàng rán。 kuàng jiàn jiàn wén wén zhě yě？ yǐ shí rèn zhī tái xiàn zhòng xián zhě yě！

**EN:** The old country, the old capital — to gaze on it is pure joy. Though hills and mounds and grass and trees be tangled into it till nine parts in ten are buried, still it is pure joy. How much more to see the seeing and hear the hearing! — like a ten-fathom terrace hung among the crowd!

## Section 5

**ZH:** 冉相氏得其環中以隨成，與物無終無始，無幾無時日。與物化者，一不化者也，闔嘗舍之！夫師天而不得師天，與物皆殉，其以為事也若之何？夫聖人未始有天，未始有人，未始有始，未始有物，與世偕行而不替，所行之備而不洫，其合之也若之何？
**PY:** rǎn xiāng shì dé qí huán zhōng yǐ suí chéng， yǔ wù wú zhōng wú shǐ， wú jǐ wú shí rì。 yǔ wù huà zhě， yī bù huà zhě yě， hé cháng shě zhī！ fū shī tiān ér bù dé shī tiān， yǔ wù jiē xùn， qí yǐ wèi shì yě ruò zhī hé？ fū shèng rén wèi shǐ yǒu tiān， wèi shǐ yǒu rén， wèi shǐ yǒu shǐ， wèi shǐ yǒu wù， yǔ shì xié xíng ér bù tì， suǒ xíng zhī bèi ér bù xù， qí hé zhī yě ruò zhī hé？

**EN:** The house of Ranxiang got the center of the ring and followed things to completion — with things, no end, no beginning, no season, no day. One who transforms along with things has a oneness that does not transform — when has he ever left it! One who would take Heaven as teacher and cannot take Heaven as teacher goes to martyrdom with things — how would his serving as an affair turn out! The sage has never yet had Heaven, never yet had man, never yet had a beginning, never yet had things; he walks with the age and does not wear out; his walk is complete and not blocked — how could it fail to join!

## Section 6

**ZH:** 湯得其司御門尹登恆為之傅之，從師而不囿，得其隨成；為之司其名之名，嬴法得其兩見。仲尼之盡慮，為之傅之。
**PY:** tāng dé qí sī yù mén yǐn dēng héng wèi zhī fù zhī， cóng shī ér bù yòu， dé qí suí chéng； wèi zhī sī qí míng zhī míng， yíng fǎ dé qí liǎng jiàn。 zhòng ní zhī jǐn lǜ， wèi zhī fù zhī。

**EN:** Tang got his driver, the gatekeeper Yin Dengheng (登恆), and took him as his tutor: following the teacher without being bounded by him, he got his following-to-completion. In governing names he named names; and in the excess of method he got his double view. Confucius, exhausting thought, took him as his tutor. [uncertain: the chain of teachers here (門尹登恆, and Confucius taking "him" as tutor) is elliptical; the rendering follows the received text without forcing the references.]

## Section 7

**ZH:** 容成氏曰：「除日無歲，無內無外。」
**PY:** róng chéng shì yuē：「 chú rì wú suì， wú nèi wú wài。」

**EN:** The house of Rongcheng said: "Take away the days and there is no year; no within, no without."

## Section 8

**ZH:** 魏瑩與田侯牟約，田侯牟背之。魏瑩怒，將使人刺之。
**PY:** wèi yíng yǔ tián hóu móu yuē， tián hóu móu bèi zhī。 wèi yíng nù， jiāng shǐ rén cì zhī。

**EN:** Wei Ying (魏瑩) had made a covenant with Marquis Tian Mou (田侯牟), and Marquis Tian Mou broke it. Wei Ying was enraged and was about to send men to assassinate him.

## Section 9

**ZH:** 犀首聞而恥之，曰：「君為萬乘之君也，而以匹夫從讎！衍請受甲二十萬，為君攻之，虜其人民，係其牛馬，使其君內熱發於背，然後拔其國。忌也出走，然後抶其背，折其脊。」
**PY:** xī shǒu wén ér chǐ zhī， yuē：「 jūn wèi wàn chéng zhī jūn yě， ér yǐ pǐ fū cóng chóu！ yǎn qǐng shòu jiǎ èr shí wàn， wèi jūn gōng zhī， lǔ qí rén mín， xì qí niú mǎ， shǐ qí jūn nèi rè fā yú bèi， rán hòu bá qí guó。 jì yě chū zǒu， rán hòu chì qí bèi， zhé qí jí。」

**EN:** Rhinoceros-Head (犀首, Gongsun Yan) heard of it and was ashamed: "You are the lord of ten thousand chariots, yet you would avenge yourself like a common man! I, Yan, beg two hundred thousand armed men: for you I will attack him, capture his people, take his oxen and horses, until their lord's inward fever breaks out on his back; then I will pluck out his state. When Tian Ji flees, I will beat his back and break his spine."

## Section 10

**ZH:** 季子聞而恥之，曰：「築十仞之城，城者既十仞矣，則又壞之，此胥靡之所苦也。今兵不起七年矣，此王之基也。衍亂人，不可聽也。」
**PY:** jì zi wén ér chǐ zhī， yuē：「 zhù shí rèn zhī chéng， chéng zhě jì shí rèn yǐ， zé yòu huài zhī， cǐ xū mí zhī suǒ kǔ yě。 jīn bīng bù qǐ qī nián yǐ， cǐ wáng zhī jī yě。 yǎn luàn rén， bù kě tīng yě。」

**EN:** Jizi (季子) heard of it and was ashamed: "To build a ten-fathom wall, and when the wall is already ten fathoms high, to knock it down again — that is the convict laborers' misery. Now arms have not been raised for seven years — that is the foundation of your kingship. Yan is a man of disorder. He must not be heeded."

## Section 11

**ZH:** 華子聞而醜之，曰：「善言伐齊者，亂人也；善言勿伐者，亦亂人也；謂伐之與不伐亂人也者，又亂人也。」王曰：「然則若何？」曰：「君求其道而已矣。」
**PY:** huá zi wén ér chǒu zhī， yuē：「 shàn yán fá qí zhě， luàn rén yě； shàn yán wù fá zhě， yì luàn rén yě； wèi fá zhī yǔ bù fá luàn rén yě zhě， yòu luàn rén yě。」 wáng yuē：「 rán zé ruò hé？」 yuē：「 jūn qiú qí dào ér yǐ yǐ。」

**EN:** Huazi (華子) heard of it and was disgusted: "One who speaks well for attacking Qi is a man of disorder; one who speaks well against attacking is also a man of disorder; and one who says that both the for-attacking and the against-attacking are men of disorder is again a man of disorder." The king said: "Then what?" He said: "Seek only the Way, my lord, and have done."

## Section 12

**ZH:** 惠子聞之而見戴晉人。戴晉人曰：「有所謂蝸者，君知之乎？」曰：「然。」「有國於蝸之左角者曰觸氏，有國於蝸之右角者曰蠻氏，時相與爭地而戰，伏尸數萬，逐北旬有五日而後反。」君曰：「噫！其虛言與？」曰：「臣請為君實之。君以意在四方上下有窮乎？」君曰：「無窮。」曰：「知遊心於無窮，而反在通達之國，若存若亡乎？」君曰：「然。」曰：「通達之中有魏，於魏中有梁，於梁中有王。王與蠻氏，有辯乎？」君曰：「無辯。」客出而君惝然若有亡也。
**PY:** huì zi wén zhī ér jiàn dài jìn rén。 dài jìn rén yuē：「 yǒu suǒ wèi wō zhě， jūn zhī zhī hū？」 yuē：「 rán。」「 yǒu guó yú wō zhī zuǒ jiǎo zhě yuē chù shì， yǒu guó yú wō zhī yòu jiǎo zhě yuē mán shì， shí xiāng yǔ zhēng dì ér zhàn， fú shī shù wàn， zhú běi xún yǒu wǔ rì ér hòu fǎn。」 jūn yuē：「 yī！ qí xū yán yǔ？」 yuē：「 chén qǐng wèi jūn shí zhī。 jūn yǐ yì zài sì fāng shàng xià yǒu qióng hū？」 jūn yuē：「 wú qióng。」 yuē：「 zhī yóu xīn yú wú qióng， ér fǎn zài tōng dá zhī guó， ruò cún ruò wáng hū？」 jūn yuē：「 rán。」 yuē：「 tōng dá zhī zhōng yǒu wèi， yú wèi zhōng yǒu liáng， yú liáng zhōng yǒu wáng。 wáng yǔ mán shì， yǒu biàn hū？」 jūn yuē：「 wú biàn。」 kè chū ér jūn chǎng rán ruò yǒu wáng yě。

**EN:** Huizi heard of it and brought Dai Jinren (戴晉人) to see the king. Dai Jinren said: "There is a creature called the snail — does my lord know it?" The king said: "Yes." "On the snail's left horn there is a state called Chu (觸氏); on the snail's right horn a state called Man (蠻氏). From time to time they contend for territory and make war: corpses by the tens of thousands; the pursuit of the routed, a fortnight and five days, before they return." The king said: "Ah! Is that not an empty tale?" He said: "Let your servant make it real for you, my lord. Do you hold that the four quarters, up and down, have an end?" The king said: "No end." He said: "Knowing to let the mind roam in the endless, and turning back to the kingdoms of thoroughfares — do they not seem hardly there at all?" The king said: "Yes." He said: "Within the thoroughfares there is Wei; within Wei there is Liang; within Liang there is the king. Between the king and the Man clan — is there any difference?" The king said: "No difference." The guest went out, and the king sat dazed, as though he had lost something.

## Section 13

**ZH:** 客出，惠子見。君曰：「客，大人也，聖人不足以當之。」惠子曰：「夫吹筦也，猶有嗃也；吹劍首者，吷而已矣。堯、舜，人之所譽也；道堯、舜於戴晉人之前，譬猶一吷也。」
**PY:** kè chū， huì zi jiàn。 jūn yuē：「 kè， dà rén yě， shèng rén bù zú yǐ dāng zhī。」 huì zi yuē：「 fū chuī guǎn yě， yóu yǒu hè yě； chuī jiàn shǒu zhě， xuè ér yǐ yǐ。 yáo、 shùn， rén zhī suǒ yù yě； dào yáo、 shùn yú dài jìn rén zhī qián， pì yóu yī xuè yě。」

**EN:** The guest gone, Huizi entered. The king said: "That guest is a great man; the sages are not his match." Huizi said: "Blow a flute and there is at least a note; blow across a sword's pommel-ring and there is a mere hiss. Yao and Shun are what men praise; but to discourse of Yao and Shun before Dai Jinren is like one little hiss."

## Section 14

**ZH:** 孔子之楚，舍於蟻丘之漿。其鄰有夫妻臣妾登極者，子路曰：「是稯稯何為者邪？」仲尼曰：「是聖人僕也。是自埋於民，自藏於畔。其聲銷，其志無窮，其口雖言，其心未嘗言，方且與世違而心不屑與之俱。是陸沈者也，是其市南宜僚邪？」子路請往召之。孔子曰：「已矣！彼知丘之著於己也，知丘之適楚也，以丘為必使楚王之召己也，彼且以丘為佞人也。夫若然者，其於佞人也羞聞其言，而況親見其身乎！而何以為存？」子路往視之，其室虛矣。
**PY:** kǒng zi zhī chǔ， shě yú yǐ qiū zhī jiāng。 qí lín yǒu fū qī chén qiè dēng jí zhě， zi lù yuē：「 shì zōng zōng hé wèi zhě xié？」 zhòng ní yuē：「 shì shèng rén pú yě。 shì zì mái yú mín， zì cáng yú pàn。 qí shēng xiāo， qí zhì wú qióng， qí kǒu suī yán， qí xīn wèi cháng yán， fāng qiě yǔ shì wéi ér xīn bù xiè yǔ zhī jù。 shì lù shěn zhě yě， shì qí shì nán yí liáo xié？」 zi lù qǐng wǎng zhào zhī。 kǒng zi yuē：「 yǐ yǐ！ bǐ zhī qiū zhī zhù yú jǐ yě， zhī qiū zhī shì chǔ yě， yǐ qiū wèi bì shǐ chǔ wáng zhī zhào jǐ yě， bǐ qiě yǐ qiū wèi nìng rén yě。 fū ruò rán zhě， qí yú nìng rén yě xiū wén qí yán， ér kuàng qīn jiàn qí shēn hū！ ér hé yǐ wèi cún？」 zi lù wǎng shì zhī， qí shì xū yǐ。

**EN:** Confucius, on his way to Chu, lodged at the drink-shop of Ant Hill. His neighbors — a couple with their serving folk — had climbed to the rooftop. Zilu said: "What is that swarm of people doing?" Confucius said: "That is the household of a sage. He has buried himself among the people; he has hidden himself on the edge. His fame has melted; his will is boundless; though his mouth speaks, his heart has never spoken; he is on the point of defying the world, and his heart disdains to keep it company. This is one who is submerged on dry land. Is it not Yiliao of Shinan?" Zilu begged leave to go and call him. Confucius said: "Enough! He knows that I make much of him; he knows I am on my way to Chu; he will take it that I am sure to have the king of Chu summon him — and he will take me for a flatterer. Such a man is ashamed even to hear the words of a flatterer — how much more to see his person! How could he still be there?" Zilu went to look — the house was empty.

## Section 15

**ZH:** 長梧封人問子牢曰：「君為政焉勿鹵莽，治民焉勿滅裂。昔予為禾，耕而鹵莽之，則其實亦鹵莽而報予；芸而滅裂之，其實亦滅裂而報予。予來年變齊，深其耕而熟耰之，其禾蘩以滋，予終年厭飧。」莊子聞之曰：「今人之治其形，理其心，多有似封人之所謂：遁其天，離其性，滅其情，亡其神，以眾為。故鹵莽其性者，欲惡之孽，為性萑葦蒹葭，始萌以扶吾形，尋擢吾性，並潰漏發，不擇所出，漂疽疥癕，內熱溲膏是也。」
**PY:** zhǎng wú fēng rén wèn zi láo yuē：「 jūn wèi zhèng yān wù lǔ mǎng， zhì mín yān wù miè liè。 xī yǔ wèi hé， gēng ér lǔ mǎng zhī， zé qí shí yì lǔ mǎng ér bào yǔ； yún ér miè liè zhī， qí shí yì miè liè ér bào yǔ。 yǔ lái nián biàn qí， shēn qí gēng ér shú yōu zhī， qí hé fán yǐ zī， yǔ zhōng nián yàn sūn。」 zhuāng zi wén zhī yuē：「 jīn rén zhī zhì qí xíng， lǐ qí xīn， duō yǒu shì fēng rén zhī suǒ wèi： dùn qí tiān， lí qí xìng， miè qí qíng， wáng qí shén， yǐ zhòng wèi。 gù lǔ mǎng qí xìng zhě， yù è zhī niè， wèi xìng huán wěi jiān jiā， shǐ méng yǐ fú wú xíng， xún zhuó wú xìng， bìng kuì lòu fā， bù zé suǒ chū， piāo jū jiè yōng， nèi rè sōu gāo shì yě。」

**EN:** The border guard of Changwu (長梧) asked Zilao (子牢): "In governing, do not be slipshod; in ordering the people, do not be careless. Of old I farmed grain: when I ploughed slipshod, the crop repaid me slipshod; when I weeded careless, the crop repaid me careless. The next year I changed all that: I ploughed deep and harrowed fine, and the grain grew thick and rich — I ate my fill the whole year round." Zhuangzi, hearing of this, said: "The men of today, in ordering their bodies and regulating their hearts, mostly do as the border guard said: they flee their Heaven, forsake their nature, extinguish their genuine feelings, lose their spirit — all to be with the crowd. So one who treats his nature slipshod: the sprouts of desire and hate become reeds and rushes of the nature; first they shoot up to prop up my form, then they pluck up my nature; all burst out in rot and leak, choosing no channel — running sores and scabby ulcers, inward heat and dripping grease are such."

## Section 16

**ZH:** 柏矩學於老聃，曰：「請之天下遊。」老聃曰：「已矣！天下猶是也。」又請之，老聃曰：「汝將何始？」曰：「始於齊。」至齊，見辜人焉，推而強之，解朝服而幕之，號天而哭之曰：「子乎子乎！天下有大菑，子獨先離之！」曰：「莫為盜！莫為殺人！榮辱立，然後睹所病；貨財聚，然後睹所爭。今立人之所病，聚人之所爭，窮困人之身，使無休時，欲無至此，得乎！古之君人者，以得為在民，以失為在己；以正為在民，以枉為在己。故一形有失其形者，退而自責。今則不然。匿為物而愚不識，大為難而罪不敢，重為任而罰不勝，遠其塗而誅不至。民知力竭，則以偽繼之，日出多偽，士民安得不偽！夫力不足則偽，知不足則欺，財不足則盜。盜竊之行，於誰責而可乎？」
**PY:** bǎi jǔ xué yú lǎo dān， yuē：「 qǐng zhī tiān xià yóu。」 lǎo dān yuē：「 yǐ yǐ！ tiān xià yóu shì yě。」 yòu qǐng zhī， lǎo dān yuē：「 rǔ jiāng hé shǐ？」 yuē：「 shǐ yú qí。」 zhì qí， jiàn gū rén yān， tuī ér qiáng zhī， jiě cháo fú ér mù zhī， hào tiān ér kū zhī yuē：「 zi hū zi hū！ tiān xià yǒu dà zāi， zi dú xiān lí zhī！」 yuē：「 mò wèi dào！ mò wèi shā rén！ róng rǔ lì， rán hòu dǔ suǒ bìng； huò cái jù， rán hòu dǔ suǒ zhēng。 jīn lì rén zhī suǒ bìng， jù rén zhī suǒ zhēng， qióng kùn rén zhī shēn， shǐ wú xiū shí， yù wú zhì cǐ， dé hū！ gǔ zhī jūn rén zhě， yǐ dé wèi zài mín， yǐ shī wèi zài jǐ； yǐ zhèng wèi zài mín， yǐ wǎng wèi zài jǐ。 gù yī xíng yǒu shī qí xíng zhě， tuì ér zì zé。 jīn zé bù rán。 nì wèi wù ér yú bù shí， dà wèi nán ér zuì bù gǎn， zhòng wèi rèn ér fá bù shèng， yuǎn qí tú ér zhū bù zhì。 mín zhī lì jié， zé yǐ wěi jì zhī， rì chū duō wěi， shì mín ān dé bù wěi！ fū lì bù zú zé wěi， zhī bù zú zé qī， cái bù zú zé dào。 dào qiè zhī xíng， yú shuí zé ér kě hū？」

**EN:** Bai Ju (柏矩) studied under Lao Dan and said: "I beg leave to roam the world." Lao Dan said: "Enough! The world is just like this." He begged again. Lao Dan said: "Where will you begin?" He said: "I will begin in Qi." Coming to Qi, he saw a corpse of one executed; he pushed and straightened it, took off his court robe and covered it, and cried out to heaven, weeping: "Alas, alas! The world has a great calamity — and you alone are the first to meet it!" He said: "Do not be a robber! Do not kill men! When honor and disgrace are set up, then we see the sickness; when goods and wealth are gathered, then we see the contention. Now we set up what sickens men and gather what they contend for; we press and wear their bodies with never a resting time — and then to wish they not come to this: can that be? The rulers of men in ancient times counted attainment as lying with the people, failure as lying with themselves; counted right as lying with the people, wrong as lying with themselves. So when a single form lost its form, they withdrew and blamed themselves. Now it is not so. They hide the devices and call ignorant those who cannot see them; they make great difficulties and count criminal those who dare not; they lay on heavy burdens and punish those who cannot bear them; they stretch the road far and execute those who do not arrive. When the people's knowledge and strength are spent, falsehood follows; and when falsehood is issued daily, how can the soldiers and people not be false! Strength insufficient breeds falsehood; knowledge insufficient breeds deceit; wealth insufficient breeds theft. For the practice of theft — on whom may the blame rightly be laid?"

## Section 17

**ZH:** 蘧伯玉行年六十而六十化，未嘗不始於是之而卒詘之以非也，未知今之所謂是之非五十九年非也。萬物有乎生而莫見其根，有乎出而莫見其門。人皆尊其知之所知，而莫知恃其知之所不知而後知，可不謂大疑乎！已乎已乎！且無所逃。此所謂然與，然乎？
**PY:** qú bó yù xíng nián liù shí ér liù shí huà， wèi cháng bù shǐ yú shì zhī ér zú qū zhī yǐ fēi yě， wèi zhī jīn zhī suǒ wèi shì zhī fēi wǔ shí jiǔ nián fēi yě。 wàn wù yǒu hū shēng ér mò jiàn qí gēn， yǒu hū chū ér mò jiàn qí mén。 rén jiē zūn qí zhī zhī suǒ zhī， ér mò zhī shì qí zhī zhī suǒ bù zhī ér hòu zhī， kě bù wèi dà yí hū！ yǐ hū yǐ hū！ qiě wú suǒ táo。 cǐ suǒ wèi rán yǔ， rán hū？

**EN:** Qu Boyu at sixty had transformed sixty times; never had he not begun by approving something and ended by rejecting it as wrong. He could not know whether what he now calls right is not the wrong of fifty-nine years. The ten thousand things have their birth, yet none sees their root; they have their coming forth, yet none sees their gate. All men honor what their knowledge knows; none knows to rely on what his knowledge does not know, and afterward to know. Can this not be called the great doubt! Enough, enough! Besides, there is no escaping it. Is this what is called so? Is it so?

## Section 18

**ZH:** 仲尼問於大史大弢、伯常騫、狶韋曰：「夫衛靈公飲酒湛樂，不聽國家之政；田獵畢弋，不應諸侯之際。其所以為靈公者何邪？」大弢曰：「是因是也。」伯常騫曰：「夫靈公有妻三人，同濫而浴。史鰌奉御而進所，搏幣而扶翼。其慢若彼之甚也，見賢人若此其肅也，是其所以為靈公也。」狶韋曰：「夫靈公也死，卜葬於故墓不吉，卜葬於沙丘而吉。掘之數仞，得石槨焉，洗而視之，有銘焉，曰：『不馮其子，靈公奪而里之。』夫靈公之為靈也久矣，之二人何足以識之？」
**PY:** zhòng ní wèn yú dà shǐ dà tāo、 bó cháng qiān、 xī wéi yuē：「 fū wèi líng gōng yǐn jiǔ zhàn lè， bù tīng guó jiā zhī zhèng； tián liè bì yì， bù yīng zhū hóu zhī jì。 qí suǒ yǐ wèi líng gōng zhě hé xié？」 dà tāo yuē：「 shì yīn shì yě。」 bó cháng qiān yuē：「 fū líng gōng yǒu qī sān rén， tóng làn ér yù。 shǐ qiū fèng yù ér jìn suǒ， bó bì ér fú yì。 qí màn ruò bǐ zhī shèn yě， jiàn xián rén ruò cǐ qí sù yě， shì qí suǒ yǐ wèi líng gōng yě。」 xī wéi yuē：「 fū líng gōng yě sǐ， bo zàng yú gù mù bù jí， bo zàng yú shā qiū ér jí。 jué zhī shù rèn， dé shí guǒ yān， xǐ ér shì zhī， yǒu míng yān， yuē：『 bù féng qí zi， líng gōng duó ér lǐ zhī。』 fū líng gōng zhī wèi líng yě jiǔ yǐ， zhī èr rén hé zú yǐ shí zhī？」

**EN:** Confucius asked the Grand Historians Datao (大弢), Bochangqian (伯常騫), and Xiwei: "Duke Ling of Wei drank wine and drowned in pleasure; he did not attend to the government of the state; he hunted with net and dart; he did not answer the envoys of the lords. Why then was he called Duke Ling ('the Numinous')?" Datao said: "It is because of just that." Bochangqian said: "Duke Ling had three wives, and bathed with them in the same tub. When Shi Qiu (史鰌) presented himself at court to offer service, he held his gift-tokens and helped him along. So disorderly in that, so reverent on meeting a worthy — that is why he is called the Numinous." Xiwei said: "When Duke Ling died, they divined burial at the old tomb — inauspicious; they divined burial at Sand Dune — auspicious. Digging several fathoms, they found a stone vault; washing it, they saw an inscription: 'Not relying on his sons, Duke Ling seized and dwelt here.' Duke Ling was the Numinous for a long time already — how could these two men know that!"

## Section 19

**ZH:** 少知問於大公調曰：「何謂丘里之言？」大公調曰：「丘里者，合十姓百名而以為風俗也。合異以為同，散同以為異。今指馬之百體而不得馬，而馬係於前者，立其百體而謂之馬也。是故丘山積卑而為高，江河合水而為大，大人合并而為公。是以自外入者，有主而不執；由中出者，有正而不距。四時殊氣，天不賜，故歲成；五官殊職，君不私，故國治；文武大人不賜，故德備；萬物殊理，道不私，故無名。無名故無為，無為而無不為。時有終始，世有變化，禍福淳淳，至有所拂者而有所宜；自殉殊面，有所正者有所差。比於大澤，百材皆度；觀於大山，木石同壇。此之謂丘里之言。」
**PY:** shǎo zhī wèn yú dà gōng diào yuē：「 hé wèi qiū lǐ zhī yán？」 dà gōng diào yuē：「 qiū lǐ zhě， hé shí xìng bǎi míng ér yǐ wèi fēng sú yě。 hé yì yǐ wèi tóng， sàn tóng yǐ wèi yì。 jīn zhǐ mǎ zhī bǎi tǐ ér bù dé mǎ， ér mǎ xì yú qián zhě， lì qí bǎi tǐ ér wèi zhī mǎ yě。 shì gù qiū shān jī bēi ér wèi gāo， jiāng hé hé shuǐ ér wèi dà， dà rén hé bìng ér wèi gōng。 shì yǐ zì wài rù zhě， yǒu zhǔ ér bù zhí； yóu zhōng chū zhě， yǒu zhèng ér bù jù。 sì shí shū qì， tiān bù cì， gù suì chéng； wǔ guān shū zhí， jūn bù sī， gù guó zhì； wén wǔ dà rén bù cì， gù dé bèi； wàn wù shū lǐ， dào bù sī， gù wú míng。 wú míng gù wú wèi， wú wèi ér wú bù wèi。 shí yǒu zhōng shǐ， shì yǒu biàn huà， huò fú chún chún， zhì yǒu suǒ fú zhě ér yǒu suǒ yí； zì xùn shū miàn， yǒu suǒ zhèng zhě yǒu suǒ chà。 bǐ yú dà zé， bǎi cái jiē dù； guān yú dà shān， mù shí tóng tán。 cǐ zhī wèi qiū lǐ zhī yán。」

**EN:** Shao Zhi (少知) asked Grand Duke Diao (大公調): "What is meant by the saying of the mound and village?" Grand Duke Diao said: "The mound and village join ten surnames and a hundred names into one custom. Join the different to make the same; scatter the same to make the different. Now point to the hundred parts of a horse and you do not get a horse; but a horse is tied before us — set up its hundred parts and call it a horse. So mounds and hills heap up the low to be high; rivers join waters to be great; the great man joins and brings together to be public. Therefore what enters from without has its master but is not held; what issues from within has its rectitude but is not resisted. The four seasons have different breaths: Heaven does not bestow, therefore the year is completed. The five offices have different duties: the lord does not favor, therefore the state is ordered. The civil and martial differ: the great man does not bestow, therefore virtue is complete. The ten thousand things have different patterns: the Way does not favor, therefore it is nameless. Nameless, therefore without action; without action, yet nothing is not done. The seasons have their endings and beginnings; the age has its changes and transformations; misfortune and fortune flow round — there is that which defies, and there is that which fits. Each martyrs himself to his own face: where there is rectitude there is discrepancy. Compare the great marsh: the hundred materials are all measured in it. Look at the great mountain: trees and stones share one altar. This is called the saying of the mound and village."

## Section 20

**ZH:** 少知曰：「然則謂之道，足乎？」大公調曰：「不然。今計物之數，不止於萬，而期曰『萬物』者，以數之多者號而讀之也。是故天地者，形之大者也；陰陽者，氣之大者也；道者為之公。因其大而號以讀之，則可也。已有之矣，乃將得比哉！則若以斯辯，譬猶狗馬，其不及遠矣。」
**PY:** shǎo zhī yuē：「 rán zé wèi zhī dào， zú hū？」 dà gōng diào yuē：「 bù rán。 jīn jì wù zhī shù， bù zhǐ yú wàn， ér qī yuē『 wàn wù』 zhě， yǐ shù zhī duō zhě hào ér dú zhī yě。 shì gù tiān dì zhě， xíng zhī dà zhě yě； yīn yáng zhě， qì zhī dà zhě yě； dào zhě wèi zhī gōng。 yīn qí dà ér hào yǐ dú zhī， zé kě yě。 yǐ yǒu zhī yǐ， nǎi jiāng dé bǐ zāi！ zé ruò yǐ sī biàn， pì yóu gǒu mǎ， qí bù jí yuǎn yǐ。」

**EN:** Shao Zhi said: "Then to call it the Way — is that enough?" Grand Duke Diao said: "Not so. Now reckon the number of things — it does not stop at ten thousand, yet we set the term 'ten thousand things' by taking the largest number and naming it by convention. So heaven and earth are the greatest of forms; yin and yang are the greatest of breaths; the Way is their commonality. To name it by convention from its greatness is admissible. But once there is a name for it, how could it bear comparison! So if you dispute in this way, it is like dog and horse: the falling short is far indeed."

## Section 21

**ZH:** 少知曰：「四方之內，六合之裏，萬物之所生惡起？」太公調曰：「陰陽相照、相蓋、相治，四時相代、相生、相殺，欲惡去就於是橋起，雌雄片合於是庸有。安危相易，禍福相生，緩急相摩，聚散以成。此名實之可紀，精微之可志也。隨序之相理，橋運之相使，窮則反，終則始。此物之所有，言之所盡，知之所至，極物而已。覩道之人，不隨其所廢，不原其所起，此議之所止。」
**PY:** shǎo zhī yuē：「 sì fāng zhī nèi， liù hé zhī lǐ， wàn wù zhī suǒ shēng è qǐ？」 tài gōng diào yuē：「 yīn yáng xiāng zhào、 xiāng gài、 xiāng zhì， sì shí xiāng dài、 xiāng shēng、 xiāng shā， yù è qù jiù yú shì qiáo qǐ， cí xióng piàn hé yú shì yōng yǒu。 ān wēi xiāng yì， huò fú xiàng shēng， huǎn jí xiāng mó， jù sàn yǐ chéng。 cǐ míng shí zhī kě jì， jīng wēi zhī kě zhì yě。 suí xù zhī xiāng lǐ， qiáo yùn zhī xiāng shǐ， qióng zé fǎn， zhōng zé shǐ。 cǐ wù zhī suǒ yǒu， yán zhī suǒ jǐn， zhī zhī suǒ zhì， jí wù ér yǐ。 dǔ dào zhī rén， bù suí qí suǒ fèi， bù yuán qí suǒ qǐ， cǐ yì zhī suǒ zhǐ。」

**EN:** Shao Zhi said: "Within the four quarters, inside the six coordinates — from what do the ten thousand things arise in their birth?" Grand Duke Diao said: "Yin and yang illumine, cover, and order one another; the four seasons succeed, give birth to, and destroy one another; desire and aversion, departure and approach, thereupon rise like a bridge; male and female join in halves, and so there is constant possession. Safety and danger trade places; misfortune and fortune give birth to one another; slow and fast grind one another; gathering and scattering are formed of one another. These names and substances can be recorded; these fine and subtle things can be noted. What follows the sequence is ordered in turn; what bridges the motion is driven in turn; exhausted, it returns; ended, it begins. This is what things possess; this is where words end; this is where knowledge arrives — the utmost of things, that is all. One who beholds the Way does not follow them to their ruin nor trace them to their rise — here discussion stops."

## Section 22

**ZH:** 少知曰：「季真之莫為，接子之或使，二家之議，孰正於其情？孰偏於其理？」太公調曰：「雞鳴狗吠，是人之所知，雖有大知，不能以言讀其所自化，又不能以意其所將為。斯而析之，精至於無倫，大至於不可圍，或之使，莫之為，未免於物而終以為過。或使則實，莫為則虛。有名有實，是物之居；無名無實，在物之虛。可言可意，言而愈疏。未生不可忌，已死不可阻。死生非遠也，理不可睹。或之使，莫之為，疑之所假。吾觀之本，其往無窮；吾求之末，其來無止。無窮、無止，言之無也，與物同理；或使、莫為，言之本也，與物終始。道不可有，有不可無。道之為名，所假而行。或使莫為，在物一曲，夫胡為於大方？言而足，則終日言而盡道；言而不足，則終日言而盡物。道、物之極，言、默不足以載；非言非默，議其有極。」
**PY:** shǎo zhī yuē：「 jì zhēn zhī mò wèi， jiē zi zhī huò shǐ， èr jiā zhī yì， shú zhèng yú qí qíng？ shú piān yú qí lǐ？」 tài gōng diào yuē：「 jī míng gǒu fèi， shì rén zhī suǒ zhī， suī yǒu dà zhī， bù néng yǐ yán dú qí suǒ zì huà， yòu bù néng yǐ yì qí suǒ jiāng wèi。 sī ér xī zhī， jīng zhì yú wú lún， dà zhì yú bù kě wéi， huò zhī shǐ， mò zhī wèi， wèi miǎn yú wù ér zhōng yǐ wèi guò。 huò shǐ zé shí， mò wèi zé xū。 yǒu míng yǒu shí， shì wù zhī jū； wú míng wú shí， zài wù zhī xū。 kě yán kě yì， yán ér yù shū。 wèi shēng bù kě jì， yǐ sǐ bù kě zǔ。 sǐ shēng fēi yuǎn yě， lǐ bù kě dǔ。 huò zhī shǐ， mò zhī wèi， yí zhī suǒ jiǎ。 wú guān zhī běn， qí wǎng wú qióng； wú qiú zhī mò， qí lái wú zhǐ。 wú qióng、 wú zhǐ， yán zhī wú yě， yǔ wù tóng lǐ； huò shǐ、 mò wèi， yán zhī běn yě， yǔ wù zhōng shǐ。 dào bù kě yǒu， yǒu bù kě wú。 dào zhī wèi míng， suǒ jiǎ ér xíng。 huò shǐ mò wèi， zài wù yī qū， fū hú wèi yú dà fāng？ yán ér zú， zé zhōng rì yán ér jǐn dào； yán ér bù zú， zé zhōng rì yán ér jǐn wù。 dào、 wù zhī jí， yán、 mò bù zú yǐ zài； fēi yán fēi mò， yì qí yǒu jí。」

**EN:** Shao Zhi said: "Jizhen holds there is nothing that acts; Jiezi holds there is something that acts. Of these two schools' accounts, which is right on the true condition? Which is lopsided in its pattern?" Grand Duke Diao said: "Cocks crow, dogs bark — this all men know. Yet even with great knowledge, one cannot read in words whence they transform, nor guess in thought what they are about to do. Analyze it further: fine to the unrankable, great to the unencompassable — 'something acts,' 'nothing acts' — neither escapes things, and both end as excess. 'Something acts' is substantial; 'nothing acts' is empty. With name, with substance — that is the lodging of things; without name, without substance — that is the emptiness of things. What can be spoken and thought — the more spoken, the more distant. What has not been born cannot be forbidden; what is already dead cannot be stopped. Death and life are not far, yet the pattern cannot be seen. 'Something acts,' 'nothing acts' — these are what doubt borrows. I look at the root: its going is endless; I seek the tip: its coming is without stop. Endless, unstopping — the nothing of words, sharing the pattern of things. 'Acts' and 'does not act' — the root of words, ending and beginning with things. The Way cannot be had; being cannot be made nothing. 'The Way' as a name is borrowed in walking. 'Something acts,' 'nothing acts' — each in one cramped corner of things: what would they do in the great method? If words sufficed, a day's talking would exhaust the Way; if words do not suffice, a day's talking exhausts mere things. The Way, the utmost of things — words and silence alike cannot carry. Neither words nor silence: here discussion has its utmost."
