# Lie Yu-kou (列禦寇)
Source: Zhuangzi _ Miscellaneous Chapters _ Lie Yu-kou - Chinese Text Project.html
Status: complete (19 sections)

## Section 1

**ZH:** 列御寇之齊，中道而反，遇伯昏瞀人。伯昏瞀人曰：「奚方而反？」曰：「吾驚焉。」曰：「惡乎驚？」曰：「吾嘗食於十漿，而五漿先饋。」伯昏瞀人曰：「若是，則汝何為驚已？」曰：「夫內誠不解，形諜成光，以外鎮人心，使人輕乎貴老，而齏其所患。夫漿特為食羹之貨，多餘之贏，其為利也薄，其為權也輕，而猶若是，而況於萬乘之主乎！身勞於國而知盡於事，彼將任我以事而效我以功，吾是以驚。」伯昏瞀人曰：「善哉觀乎！汝處已，人將保汝矣。」
**PY:** liè yù kòu zhī qí， zhōng dào ér fǎn， yù bó hūn mào rén。 bó hūn mào rén yuē：「 xī fāng ér fǎn？」 yuē：「 wú jīng yān。」 yuē：「 è hū jīng？」 yuē：「 wú cháng shí yú shí jiāng， ér wǔ jiāng xiān kuì。」 bó hūn mào rén yuē：「 ruò shì， zé rǔ hé wèi jīng yǐ？」 yuē：「 fū nèi chéng bù jiě， xíng dié chéng guāng， yǐ wài zhèn rén xīn， shǐ rén qīng hū guì lǎo， ér jī qí suǒ huàn。 fū jiāng tè wèi shí gēng zhī huò， duō yú zhī yíng， qí wèi lì yě báo， qí wèi quán yě qīng， ér yóu ruò shì， ér kuàng yú wàn chéng zhī zhǔ hū！ shēn láo yú guó ér zhī jǐn yú shì， bǐ jiāng rèn wǒ yǐ shì ér xiào wǒ yǐ gōng， wú shì yǐ jīng。」 bó hūn mào rén yuē：「 shàn zāi guān hū！ rǔ chù yǐ， rén jiāng bǎo rǔ yǐ。」

**EN:** Lie Yikou, on his way to Qi, turned back midway and met Bahun Maoren. Bahun Maoren said: "Why have you turned back?" He said: "I was startled." He said: "Startled at what?" He said: "I was eating at a ten-drink stand, and five of the drinks were served me first." Bahun Maoren said: "If that is all, why were you startled?" He said: "The sincerity within had not loosened, yet my form spilled into a radiance; by that outwardness I overawed men's hearts, and made men slight the old and honored, and gathered in the trouble to come. Now a drink-stand sells only food and broth; the surplus of its gain, its profit, is thin; its power, slight — and still it was thus. How much more the lord of ten thousand chariots! His body labors for the state, his knowledge is spent on affairs; he will charge me with a task and exact of me its achievement — that is why I was startled." Bahun Maoren said: "Well observed! But settle yourself — men will be making you their stay."

## Section 2

**ZH:** 無幾何而往，則戶外之屨滿矣。伯昏瞀人北面而立，敦杖蹙之乎頤，立有間，不言而出。賓者以告列子，列子提屨，跣而走，暨乎門，曰：「先生既來，曾不發藥乎？」曰：「已矣！吾固告汝曰『人將保汝』，果保汝矣。非汝能使人保汝，而汝不能使人無保汝也，而焉用之感豫出異也！必且有感，搖而本才，又無謂也。與汝遊者，又莫汝告也，彼所小言，盡人毒也。莫覺莫悟，何相孰也！巧者勞而知者憂，無能者無所求，飽食而敖遊，汎若不繫之舟，虛而敖遊者也。」
**PY:** wú jǐ hé ér wǎng， zé hù wài zhī jù mǎn yǐ。 bó hūn mào rén běi miàn ér lì， dūn zhàng cù zhī hū yí， lì yǒu jiān， bù yán ér chū。 bīn zhě yǐ gào liè zi， liè zi tí jù， xiǎn ér zǒu， jì hū mén， yuē：「 xiān shēng jì lái， céng bù fā yào hū？」 yuē：「 yǐ yǐ！ wú gù gào rǔ yuē『 rén jiāng bǎo rǔ』， guǒ bǎo rǔ yǐ。 fēi rǔ néng shǐ rén bǎo rǔ， ér rǔ bù néng shǐ rén wú bǎo rǔ yě， ér yān yòng zhī gǎn yù chū yì yě！ bì qiě yǒu gǎn， yáo ér běn cái， yòu wú wèi yě。 yǔ rǔ yóu zhě， yòu mò rǔ gào yě， bǐ suǒ xiǎo yán， jǐn rén dú yě。 mò jué mò wù， hé xiāng shú yě！ qiǎo zhě láo ér zhī zhě yōu， wú néng zhě wú suǒ qiú， bǎo shí ér áo yóu， fàn ruò bù xì zhī zhōu， xū ér áo yóu zhě yě。」

**EN:** Not long after, when Bahun Maoren went there, the shoes outside the door were full. Bahun Maoren stood facing north, leaning his staff on his wrinkled chin; he stood a while and went out without a word. The usher told Liezi; Liezi picked up his shoes and ran barefoot to the gate: "Since you have come, sir, will you not issue your medicine?" He said: "Enough! I told you before, 'Men will make you their stay' — and they have indeed. It is not that you can make men make you their stay; it is that you cannot keep them from making you their stay. What use is it to be moved to delight and stand out as strange! Wherever there is stirring, it shakes your root-talent — and that is meaningless. Those who roam with you never tell you so: their small words are all men's poison. Unawakened, unawoke — how do you help one another! The clever toil; the knowing worry; the capable have what they seek; the incapable seek nothing: fed full, they roam at play, drifting like an unmoored boat — empty and roaming at play."

## Section 3

**ZH:** 鄭人緩也呻吟裘氏之地。祗三年而緩為儒，潤河九里，澤及三族，使其弟墨。儒、墨相與辯，其父助翟。十年而緩自殺。其父夢之，曰：「使而子為墨者，予也。闔胡嘗視其良，既為秋柏之實矣！」夫造物者之報人也，不報其人而報其人之天。彼故使彼。夫人以己為有以異於人，以賤其親，齊人之井，飲者相捽也。故曰：「今之世皆緩也。」自是，有德者以不知也，而況有道者乎！古者謂之遁天之刑。
**PY:** zhèng rén huǎn yě shēn yín qiú shì zhī dì。 zhī sān nián ér huǎn wèi rú， rùn hé jiǔ lǐ， zé jí sān zú， shǐ qí dì mò。 rú、 mò xiāng yǔ biàn， qí fù zhù dí。 shí nián ér huǎn zì shā。 qí fù mèng zhī， yuē：「 shǐ ér zi wèi mò zhě， yǔ yě。 hé hú cháng shì qí liáng， jì wèi qiū bǎi zhī shí yǐ！」 fū zào wù zhě zhī bào rén yě， bù bào qí rén ér bào qí rén zhī tiān。 bǐ gù shǐ bǐ。 fū rén yǐ jǐ wèi yǒu yǐ yì yú rén， yǐ jiàn qí qīn， qí rén zhī jǐng， yǐn zhě xiāng zuó yě。 gù yuē：「 jīn zhī shì jiē huǎn yě。」 zì shì， yǒu dé zhě yǐ bù zhī yě， ér kuàng yǒu dào zhě hū！ gǔ zhě wèi zhī dùn tiān zhī xíng。

**EN:** A man of Zheng named Huan (緩) chanted his studies in the land of Qiu. In just three years Huan became a Ru; his enrichment reached nine li of the river; his blessing reached the three kindreds; and he made his younger brother a Mohist. Ru and Mohist disputed with one another, and their father helped the Mohist Di. After ten years Huan killed himself. In a dream his father heard him say: "It was I who made your son a Mohist. Why not come see how well I have done — I am already the fruit of autumn cypress!" The Creator's recompense of men does not recompense the man; it recompenses the man's Heaven. That made him be that. For a man to count himself different from other men and so to slight his own parent is like the Qi folk's well, whose drinkers wrestle one another for it. Hence it is said: "The whole age is full of Huans." Self-satisfied: even those of virtue count themselves unknowing — how much more those who have the Way! The ancients called this the punishment of fleeing Heaven.

## Section 4

**ZH:** 聖人安其所安，不安其所不安；眾人安其所不安，不安其所安。
**PY:** shèng rén ān qí suǒ ān， bù ān qí suǒ bù ān； zhòng rén ān qí suǒ bù ān， bù ān qí suǒ ān。

**EN:** The sage is at ease in what is at ease, not at ease in what is not at ease; the crowd is at ease in what is not at ease, not at ease in what is at ease.

## Section 5

**ZH:** 莊子曰：「知道易，勿言難。知而不言，所以之天也；知而言之，所以之人也。古之人，天而不人。」
**PY:** zhuāng zi yuē：「 zhī dào yì， wù yán nán。 zhī ér bù yán， suǒ yǐ zhī tiān yě； zhī ér yán zhī， suǒ yǐ zhī rén yě。 gǔ zhī rén， tiān ér bù rén。」

**EN:** Zhuangzi said: "To know the Way is easy; to say nothing of it is hard. To know and not speak — that leads to Heaven; to know and speak — that leads to men. The men of old were at Heaven, not at men."

## Section 6

**ZH:** 朱泙漫學屠龍於支離益，單千金之家，三年技成，而無所用其巧。
**PY:** zhū píng màn xué tú lóng yú zhī lí yì， dān qiān jīn zhī jiā， sān nián jì chéng， ér wú suǒ yòng qí qiǎo。

**EN:** Zhu Pingman (朱泙漫) learned to butcher dragons from Splay-limb Yi (支離益). He spent his family's thousand in gold; after three years his skill was complete — and there was no place to use his art.

## Section 7

**ZH:** 聖人以必不必，故無兵；眾人以不必必之，故多兵。順於兵，故行有求。兵，恃之則亡。
**PY:** shèng rén yǐ bì bù bì， gù wú bīng； zhòng rén yǐ bù bì bì zhī， gù duō bīng。 shùn yú bīng， gù xíng yǒu qiú。 bīng， shì zhī zé wáng。

**EN:** The sage makes necessity of the unnecessary — therefore he is without strife. The crowd makes the unnecessary necessary — therefore strife abounds. To go along with strife is to act with seeking. Strife: rely on it, and you perish.

## Section 8

**ZH:** 小夫之知，不離苞苴竿牘，敝精神乎蹇淺，而欲兼濟道物，太一形虛。若是者，迷惑於宇宙，形累不知太初。彼至人者，歸精神乎無始，而甘冥乎無何有之鄉。水流乎無形，發泄乎太清。悲哉乎！汝為知在毫毛，而不知大寧！
**PY:** xiǎo fū zhī zhī， bù lí bāo jū gān dú， bì jīng shén hū jiǎn qiǎn， ér yù jiān jì dào wù， tài yī xíng xū。 ruò shì zhě， mí huò yú yǔ zhòu， xíng lèi bù zhī tài chū。 bǐ zhì rén zhě， guī jīng shén hū wú shǐ， ér gān míng hū wú hé yǒu zhī xiāng。 shuǐ liú hū wú xíng， fā xiè hū tài qīng。 bēi zāi hū！ rǔ wèi zhī zài háo máo， ér bù zhī dà níng！

**EN:** The petty man's knowledge never leaves his parcel-gifts and bamboo letters; he wearies his spirit on trifles and shallows, yet wishes to succor the Way and things alike, to be one with the One and empty the form. Men like that are lost and deluded in the universe; their form-burden never knows the Grand Beginning. The utmost man returns his spirit to the beginningless, and sweetly darkens in the land of Nothing-Whatever. Water flows from the formless; it issues and pours from the great clarity. How sad! Your knowledge is in the tip of a hair, and you do not know the great tranquility!

## Section 9

**ZH:** 宋人有曹商者，為宋王使秦。其往也，得車數乘；王說之，益車百乘。反於宋，見莊子曰：「夫處窮閭阨巷，困窘織屨，槁項黃馘者，商之所短也；一悟萬乘之主，而從車百乘者，商之所長也。」莊子曰：「秦王有病召醫，破癰潰痤者得車一乘，舐痔者得車五乘，所治愈下，得車愈多。子豈治其痔邪？何得車之多也？子行矣！」
**PY:** sòng rén yǒu cáo shāng zhě， wèi sòng wáng shǐ qín。 qí wǎng yě， dé chē shù chéng； wáng shuō zhī， yì chē bǎi chéng。 fǎn yú sòng， jiàn zhuāng zi yuē：「 fū chù qióng lǘ è xiàng， kùn jiǒng zhī jù， gǎo xiàng huáng guó zhě， shāng zhī suǒ duǎn yě； yī wù wàn chéng zhī zhǔ， ér cóng chē bǎi chéng zhě， shāng zhī suǒ zhǎng yě。」 zhuāng zi yuē：「 qín wáng yǒu bìng zhào yī， pò yōng kuì cuó zhě dé chē yī chéng， shì zhì zhě dé chē wǔ chéng， suǒ zhì yù xià， dé chē yù duō。 zi qǐ zhì qí zhì xié？ hé dé chē zhī duō yě？ zi xíng yǐ！」

**EN:** A man of Song named Cao Shang (曹商) was sent by the king of Song as envoy to Qin. On his going he received several carriages; the king of Qin was so pleased with him that he added a hundred carriages more. Returning to Song, he saw Zhuangzi and said: "To dwell in a poor lane and narrow alley, hard-pressed, weaving sandals, with a withered neck and sallow face — that is Shang's shortness. To awaken the lord of ten thousand chariots at a word, and come home with a hundred carriages behind — that is Shang's length." Zhuangzi said: "When the king of Qin falls ill and calls physicians: one who lances a boil or drains an abscess gets one carriage; one who licks his hemorrhoids gets five. The lower the cure, the more the carriages. Were you treating his hemorrhoids? How else did you get so many carriages! Be gone!"

## Section 10

**ZH:** 魯哀公問於顏闔曰：「吾以仲尼為貞幹，國其有瘳乎？」曰：「殆哉圾乎！仲尼方且飾羽而畫，從事華辭，以支為旨，忍性以視民而不知不信，受乎心，宰乎神，夫何足以上民！彼宜女與？予頤與？誤而可矣。今使民離實學偽，非所以視民也。為後世慮，不若休之，難治也。」
**PY:** lǔ āi gōng wèn yú yán hé yuē：「 wú yǐ zhòng ní wèi zhēn gàn， guó qí yǒu chōu hū？」 yuē：「 dài zāi jī hū！ zhòng ní fāng qiě shì yǔ ér huà， cóng shì huá cí， yǐ zhī wèi zhǐ， rěn xìng yǐ shì mín ér bù zhī bù xìn， shòu hū xīn， zǎi hū shén， fū hé zú yǐ shàng mín！ bǐ yí nǚ yǔ？ yǔ yí yǔ？ wù ér kě yǐ。 jīn shǐ mín lí shí xué wěi， fēi suǒ yǐ shì mín yě。 wèi hòu shì lǜ， bù ruò xiū zhī， nán zhì yě。」

**EN:** Duke Ai of Lu asked Yan He: "If I make Confucius my pillar of state, will the state be healed?" He said: "Perilous indeed! Confucius for his part decks out feathers and paints, pursues flowery phrases, takes the branches for the gist; he hardens his nature to show the people, not knowing it is untrustworthy; received in his heart, lorded by his spirit — how is he fit to stand above the people! Is he fitting for you? Or nourishing for me? By error it might do. But to make the people leave substance and study falsity is not the way to show the people. For the consideration of later ages, better to let it be — it is hard to order."

## Section 11

**ZH:** 施於人而不忘，非天布也。商賈不齒，雖以事齒之，神者勿齒。為外刑者，金與木也；為內刑者，動與過也。宵人之離外刑者，金木訊之；離內刑者，陰陽食之。夫免乎外內之刑者，唯真人能之。
**PY:** shī yú rén ér bù wàng， fēi tiān bù yě。 shāng jiǎ bù chǐ， suī yǐ shì chǐ zhī， shén zhě wù chǐ。 wèi wài xíng zhě， jīn yǔ mù yě； wèi nèi xíng zhě， dòng yǔ guò yě。 xiāo rén zhī lí wài xíng zhě， jīn mù xùn zhī； lí nèi xíng zhě， yīn yáng shí zhī。 fū miǎn hū wài nèi zhī xíng zhě， wéi zhēn rén néng zhī。

**EN:** To bestow on men and not forget it is not Heaven's giving. The merchant is not counted among men; though affairs rank him, the spirit does not rank him. What makes the outer punishment is metal and wood; what makes the inner punishment is motion and excess. The petty man who meets the outer punishment is judged by metal and wood; who meets the inner punishment is eaten by yin and yang. To be free of outer and inner punishments — only the true man can do that.

## Section 12

**ZH:** 孔子曰：「凡人心險於山川，難於知天。天猶有春秋冬夏旦暮之期，人者厚貌深情。故有貌愿而益，有長若不肖，有順懁而達，有堅而縵，有緩而釬。故其就義若渴者，其去義若熱。故君子遠使之而觀其忠，近使之而觀其敬，煩使之而觀其能，卒然問焉而觀其知，急與之期而觀其信，委之以財而觀其仁，告之以危而觀其節，醉之以酒而觀其側，雜之以處而觀其色。九徵至，不肖人得矣。
**PY:** kǒng zi yuē：「 fán rén xīn xiǎn yú shān chuān， nán yú zhī tiān。 tiān yóu yǒu chūn qiū dōng xià dàn mù zhī qī， rén zhě hòu mào shēn qíng。 gù yǒu mào yuàn ér yì， yǒu zhǎng ruò bù xiào， yǒu shùn xuān ér dá， yǒu jiān ér màn， yǒu huǎn ér hàn。 gù qí jiù yì ruò kě zhě， qí qù yì ruò rè。 gù jūn zǐ yuǎn shǐ zhī ér guān qí zhōng， jìn shǐ zhī ér guān qí jìng， fán shǐ zhī ér guān qí néng， zú rán wèn yān ér guān qí zhī， jí yǔ zhī qī ér guān qí xìn， wěi zhī yǐ cái ér guān qí rén， gào zhī yǐ wēi ér guān qí jié， zuì zhī yǐ jiǔ ér guān qí cè， zá zhī yǐ chù ér guān qí sè。 jiǔ zhēng zhì， bù xiào rén dé yǐ。

**EN:** Confucius said: "The hearts of men are more perilous than mountains and rivers, harder to know than Heaven. Heaven at least has its seasons of spring and autumn, winter and summer, dawn and dusk; but men have thick faces and deep feelings. So there are those of honest looks who are full of schemes; those of elder looks who are unworthy; those of mild manner who are ruthless; those firm yet slack; those slow yet hard. So one who approaches rightness as though thirsty abandons it as though scalded. Therefore the gentleman sends him far to watch his loyalty; keeps him near to watch his reverence; burdens him with tasks to watch his ability; questions him suddenly to watch his knowledge; presses him with deadlines to watch his trust; entrusts him with wealth to watch his humaneness; tells him of danger to watch his measure; makes him drunk with wine to watch his manners; mingles him in company to watch his countenance. When these nine proofs arrive, the unworthy man is gotten."

## Section 13

**ZH:** 正考父一命而傴，再命而僂，三命而俯，循牆而走，孰敢不軌！如而夫者，一命而呂鉅，再命而於車上舞，三命而名諸父，孰協唐、許！
**PY:** zhèng kǎo fù yī mìng ér yǔ， zài mìng ér lóu， sān mìng ér fǔ， xún qiáng ér zǒu， shú gǎn bù guǐ！ rú ér fū zhě， yī mìng ér lǚ jù， zài mìng ér yú chē shàng wǔ， sān mìng ér míng zhū fù， shú xié táng、 xǔ！

**EN:** Zheng Kaofu (正考父), at his first appointment, bowed his back; at his second, bent like a hunchback; at his third, stooped to the ground and walked along the wall. Who would dare not follow his rule! But one like that fellow: at his first appointment, puffed with pride; at his second, dancing in his carriage; at his third, calling his elders by name. Who could accord with Tang (Yao) and Xu (You) in him!

## Section 14

**ZH:** 賊莫大乎德有心而心有眼，及其有眼也而內視，內視而敗矣。凶德有五，中德為首。何謂中德？中德也者，有以自好也而吡其所不為者也。
**PY:** zéi mò dà hū dé yǒu xīn ér xīn yǒu yǎn， jí qí yǒu yǎn yě ér nèi shì， nèi shì ér bài yǐ。 xiōng dé yǒu wǔ， zhōng dé wèi shǒu。 hé wèi zhōng dé？ zhōng dé yě zhě， yǒu yǐ zì hǎo yě ér bǐ qí suǒ bù wèi zhě yě。

**EN:** No thief is greater than virtue with an intentional heart, and a heart with eyes. When it has eyes and looks inward — looking inward, it is ruined. There are five ill virtues, of which the middle virtue is chief. What is the middle virtue? The middle virtue is to have one's own preferences and scorn what one does not do.

## Section 15

**ZH:** 窮有八極，達有三必，形有六府。美、髯、長、大、壯、麗、勇、敢，八者俱過人也，因以是窮。緣循、偃佒、困畏不若人，三者俱通達。知慧外通，勇動多怨，仁義多責。達生之情者傀，達於知者肖；達大命者隨，達小命者遭。
**PY:** qióng yǒu bā jí， dá yǒu sān bì， xíng yǒu liù fǔ。 měi、 rán、 zhǎng、 dà、 zhuàng、 lì、 yǒng、 gǎn， bā zhě jù guò rén yě， yīn yǐ shì qióng。 yuán xún、 yǎn yǎng、 kùn wèi bù ruò rén， sān zhě jù tōng dá。 zhī huì wài tōng， yǒng dòng duō yuàn， rén yì duō zé。 dá shēng zhī qíng zhě guī， dá yú zhī zhě xiào； dá dà mìng zhě suí， dá xiǎo mìng zhě zāo。

**EN:** Failure has eight extremities; success has three necessities; the form has six storehouses. Beauty, beard, height, size, strength, grace, courage, daring — these eight, surpassing other men, are thereby their owner's failure. Following along, humbling oneself, being pressed and fearing as though beneath others — these three wholly succeed. Knowledge and wisdom open outward; courage in motion multiplies resentment; humaneness and rightness multiply blame. One who penetrates the genuine condition of life is great; one who penetrates knowledge is small. One who penetrates great destiny follows; one who penetrates small destiny meets his lot.

## Section 16

**ZH:** 人有見宋王者，錫車十乘，以其十乘驕稚莊子。莊子曰：「河上有家貧恃緯蕭而食者，其子沒於淵，得千金之珠。其父謂其子曰『取石來鍛之！夫千金之珠，必在九重之淵而驪龍頷下，子能得珠者，必遭其睡也。使驪龍而寤，子尚奚微之有哉！』今宋國之深，非直九重之淵也；宋王之猛，非直驪龍也。子能得車者，必遭其睡也。使宋王而寤，子為齏粉夫！」
**PY:** rén yǒu jiàn sòng wáng zhě， xī chē shí chéng， yǐ qí shí chéng jiāo zhì zhuāng zi。 zhuāng zi yuē：「 hé shàng yǒu jiā pín shì wěi xiāo ér shí zhě， qí zi méi yú yuān， dé qiān jīn zhī zhū。 qí fù wèi qí zi yuē『 qǔ shí lái duàn zhī！ fū qiān jīn zhī zhū， bì zài jiǔ chóng zhī yuān ér lí lóng hàn xià， zi néng dé zhū zhě， bì zāo qí shuì yě。 shǐ lí lóng ér wù， zi shàng xī wēi zhī yǒu zāi！』 jīn sòng guó zhī shēn， fēi zhí jiǔ chóng zhī yuān yě； sòng wáng zhī měng， fēi zhí lí lóng yě。 zi néng dé chē zhě， bì zāo qí shuì yě。 shǐ sòng wáng ér wù， zi wèi jī fěn fū！」

**EN:** A man had an audience with the king of Song and was granted ten carriages, with which he strutted his triumph before Zhuangzi. Zhuangzi said: "By the river there is a poor family living on woven rushes. The son dived into the deep and got a pearl worth a thousand gold. His father said to him: 'Bring a stone to smash it! A pearl worth a thousand gold must lie in the ninefold deep, beneath the chin of the black dragon. That you could get the pearl — you must have met it asleep. Had the black dragon woken, what little of you would be left!' Now the depth of the state of Song is more than the ninefold deep; the fierceness of the king of Song is more than the black dragon. That you could get the carriages — you must have met him asleep. When the king of Song wakes, you will be ground to powder!"

## Section 17

**ZH:** 或聘於莊子，莊子應其使曰：「子見夫犧牛乎？衣以文繡，食以芻叔，及其牽而入於太廟，雖欲為孤犢，其可得乎！」
**PY:** huò pìn yú zhuāng zi， zhuāng zi yīng qí shǐ yuē：「 zi jiàn fū xī niú hū？ yī yǐ wén xiù， shí yǐ chú shū， jí qí qiān ér rù yú tài miào， suī yù wèi gū dú， qí kě dé hū！」

**EN:** Someone came with an offer of employment to Zhuangzi. Zhuangzi answered his messenger: "Have you seen the sacrificial ox? It is robed in patterned embroidery, fed on fodder and beans — and when it is led into the grand temple, though it longs to be a solitary calf again, can that be had!"

## Section 18

**ZH:** 莊子將死，弟子欲厚葬之。莊子曰：「吾以天地為棺槨，以日月為連璧，星辰為珠璣，萬物為齎送。吾葬具豈不備邪？何以加此！」弟子曰：「吾恐烏鳶之食夫子也。」莊子曰：「在上為烏鳶食，在下為螻蟻食，奪彼與此，何其偏也！」
**PY:** zhuāng zi jiāng sǐ， dì zǐ yù hòu zàng zhī。 zhuāng zi yuē：「 wú yǐ tiān dì wèi guān guǒ， yǐ rì yuè wèi lián bì， xīng chén wèi zhū jī， wàn wù wèi jī sòng。 wú zàng jù qǐ bù bèi xié？ hé yǐ jiā cǐ！」 dì zǐ yuē：「 wú kǒng wū yuān zhī shí fū zǐ yě。」 zhuāng zi yuē：「 zài shàng wèi wū yuān shí， zài xià wèi lóu yǐ shí， duó bǐ yǔ cǐ， hé qí piān yě！」

**EN:** When Zhuangzi was about to die, his disciples wished to give him a lavish burial. Zhuangzi said: "Heaven and earth are my coffin and vault; the sun and moon are my paired jades; the stars are my pearls; the ten thousand things are my funeral gifts. Are my burial furnishings not complete? What could be added!" His disciples said: "We fear the crows and kites will eat you, Master." Zhuangzi said: "Above ground I feed the crows and kites; below ground I feed the ants and moles. To take from those and give to these — how partial that would be!"

## Section 19

**ZH:** 以不平平，其平也不平；以不徵徵，其徵也不徵。明者唯為之使，神者徵之。夫明之不勝神也久矣，而愚者恃其所見入於人，其功外也，不亦悲乎！
**PY:** yǐ bù píng píng， qí píng yě bù píng； yǐ bù zhēng zhēng， qí zhēng yě bù zhēng。 míng zhě wéi wèi zhī shǐ， shén zhě zhēng zhī。 fū míng zhī bù shèng shén yě jiǔ yǐ， ér yú zhě shì qí suǒ jiàn rù yú rén， qí gōng wài yě， bù yì bēi hū！

**EN:** To level with the unlevel — that leveling is not level. To attest with the unattested — that attestation is not attestation. The bright are made to serve by things; the spirit attests. That the bright cannot match the spirit has been long so; and the fool, trusting what he sees, enters among men — his work is outward. Is that not sad!
